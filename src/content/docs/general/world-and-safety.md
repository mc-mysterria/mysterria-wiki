Mysterria is one shared survival world, and some of its rules differ from most servers. Read this before you pick a place to live.

## How big is the map?

| World | Size | Border |
|---|---|---|
| Overworld | 30,000 × 30,000 blocks | 15,000 blocks from 0, 0 in every direction |
| Nether | 16,000 × 16,000 blocks | 8,000 blocks from 0, 0 |
| The End | 20,000 × 20,000 blocks | 10,000 blocks from 0, 0 |

Most Beyonder content lives in the Overworld. The live map is at [map.mysterria.net](https://map.mysterria.net).

## Can I go to the Nether and the End?

Only as a Beyonder of a high enough Sequence:

| World | Also called | Who can enter | Danger |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nether | Spirit World | Beyonders at **Sequence 7 or stronger** | Slowly raises your Madness while you stay |
| The End | Astral World | Beyonders at **Sequence 4 or stronger** | Raises your Madness faster |

Players who are not Beyonders, or not strong enough, are stopped at the portal. See [Madness and Mutation](/magic/mutation/).

## Is there /home, /spawn or /tpa?

No. There is no `/home`, `/sethome`, `/spawn`, `/tpa`, `/rtp` or `/back`, and they will not be added. Plan your trips and choose where you settle with care. Joining a [town](/guides/towns/) helps a lot. Fast movement is one of the rewards of Pathways such as [Door](https://www.mysterria.net/en/pathways/door).

## Where is PvP on?

PvP is **off** in most of the world. It is on only in places you enter on purpose:

- [Cosmos Incursion](/guides/cosmos-incursions/) zones, which open as timed events. Their colour tells you what you can lose there.
- **The Forsaken Land of the Gods**, a permanent high-stakes PvP zone where it is always night.
- **Town claims** where the owner has turned PvP on for some roles.
- **Duels, FFA and Bedwars** events, which you join yourself with `/pvpevent` and `/bedwars`.

Under the [server rules](https://www.mysterria.net/en/rules), entering a PvP zone is your consent to be attacked there. Outside one, any ability that damages, drains or debuffs another player counts as an attack, whatever it is called. Pulling or teleporting someone into a fight they did not agree to is also against the rules.

## What happens when I die?

- Your items go into a **grave** where you died. **Anyone can open it**, so get back quickly. After about 10 minutes the grave breaks and drops what is left on the ground.
- A Beyonder also gains **1 to 2% temporary [Madness](/magic/mutation/)** from each death.
- An ordinary death does not cost you Sequence progress. The exception is the Death zone of an Incursion, where Sequence 4 and stronger Beyonders can regress. Holding a [Uniqueness](/magic/uniqueness/) also adds its own death penalty.

## Can people grief my base?

Griefing and stealing are against the rules. Claim your land with a town (`/lands`) so it is protected, and report griefing to staff. Agreed wars and agreed raids between towns are not griefing.

## Where is the loot?

Every chest in a naturally generated structure (villages, mineshafts, temples, shipwrecks, bastions, ruined portals and more) is a **Beyonder loot chest**. When Beyonder loot drops, it pops out next to the chest and glows for a few seconds.

Each player gets a chest's loot **only once**. After 12 hours to 2 days the chest refills for players who have not looted it yet, so new structures are worth more than old ones. See [Loot and Loot Focus](/magic/loot/).

## When does the world reset?

The world and all Beyonder progress reset between **epochs**, which last several months. Epoch II began on 1 May 2026 with The Great Reset. The next reset gives everyone the same fresh start.

## Other things to know

- Server restarts happen on a schedule. `/uar` shows when the next one is.
- `/lang` changes the language of the plugin's messages.
- Bedrock players can join through Geyser, but some custom menus and Pathway icons do not draw correctly.
- About one night in ten becomes a [Crimson Moon](/magic/crimson-moon/), when Beyonder creatures spawn far more often.
