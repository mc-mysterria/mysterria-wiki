Mysterria is a **Lord of the Mysteries (LOTM) Minecraft server**. The Beyonder system runs on the server through its own plugin, so you play with an ordinary Minecraft client. You need no mod or modpack. This page takes you from the server list to your first Sequence.

## 1. Connect to the server

| What | Details |
|---|---|
| **Server address** | `mc.mysterria.net` |
| **Edition** | Java Edition. Bedrock can join through Geyser, with some custom menus and icons drawn wrong |
| **Version** | The [player guide](https://www.mysterria.net/en/guide/connect) recommends Minecraft Java **26.2** |
| **Mods** | None needed. The optional [Client Mod](/guides/client-mod/) adds native menus and a small Acting bonus |

<Figure src={ipPreview} alt="The Edit Server Info screen with mc.mysterria.net as the server address" caption="Add mc.mysterria.net in Multiplayer." />

1. Open **Multiplayer**, add `mc.mysterria.net` and join.
2. **Accept the server resource pack** when Minecraft asks.
3. In the lobby server selector, choose **Mysteries**.
4. Read what the lobby NPC says before you leave. It helps you find your way.

<Notice tone="warn" title="Everything looks like plain Minecraft?">
  Then the resource pack did not load. Edit the server entry, set **Server Resource Packs** to **Enabled**, and reconnect. If it still fails, update your Java client. More fixes are in the main site's [troubleshooting guide](https://www.mysterria.net/en/guide/troubleshooting).
</Notice>

## 2. Register (offline accounts only)

Official (premium) Minecraft accounts log in automatically and skip this step.

Offline accounts register once, then log in on every later visit:

- `/register <password> <password>` the first time
- `/login <password>` after that

Remember your password.

## 3. Verify your account

Verifying links your Minecraft account to your Discord account and your profile on mysterria.net.

1. Sign in on [mysterria.net](https://www.mysterria.net/en/profile) and open your **profile**.
2. Click **Generate Code**.
3. In game, type `/verify` and enter the 8-character code in the window that opens. You can also type `/verify <code>`.

<Figure src={generate} alt="The Verify Minecraft Account box on the website profile, with a Generate Code button" caption="Generate the code on your website profile." />

<Figure src={code} alt="The in-game verification window asking for the code" caption="Then enter it in game." contain />

Verified players get exclusive Discord channels, a unique chat prefix and role, and full store access. If the code does not work, generate a fresh one and try again, or open a ticket on Discord.

## 4. Choose your starter bonus

Soon after you join, a menu shows all 22 Pathways. Pick one, then choose one of two starter bonuses.

<Figure src={pathways} alt="The Pathway selection menu with the 22 Pathway emblems" caption="The Pathway menu you see as a new player." />

| | Path of Shortcut | Path of Resolve |
|---|---|---|
| **You get** | Sequence 9 of the chosen Pathway, right away | Your recipe guarantee starts one chest away |
| **Permanent cost** | Your maximum Spirituality is **10% lower forever**, at every Sequence | None |
| **Best for** | Playing with abilities today | Learning brewing and keeping full power |

**How Path of Resolve pays out:** open `/magic`, click **Loot Focus**, choose **Recipes** and keep **Any Sequence**. Your next loot chest then gives you a recipe. The recipe can be for **any Pathway and Sequence**, so it may not match the Pathway you clicked. See [Loot Focus](/magic/loot/#loot-focus-pity).

<Notice tone="info" title="The Pathway you click does not lock you in">
  You become a Beyonder only when you drink a Sequence 9 potion, and you join that potion's Pathway. If the menu said Fool but you later drink a Door potion, you are Door. The starter bonus itself cannot be picked again. If you made a genuine new-player mistake, open a ticket on Discord. Staff sometimes give a reset.
</Notice>

## 5. Brew and drink your first potion

Sequence 9 needs a **recipe**, a **Magic Cauldron** (a Crude, Ritual or Grand Altar, see [Brewing](/magic/brewing/)), the recipe's **ingredients**, and a successful brew. It needs no Acting and no ritual, and it costs no Madness. Brewed potions expire after 48 hours, so brew when you are ready to drink.

## 6. Check your character

Your Sequence, Acting, Health, Spirituality and Madness are all on your character screen:

- Open your **inventory**, click the **Pathway icon** in the top-left corner, and hover over your character's head, or
- type `/coi menu character`, or
- with the [Client Mod](/guides/client-mod/), press **M**. The mod removes the inventory icon.

## Useful commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/magic` | Newbie guide, with Loot Focus |
| `/guide`, `/faq`, `/hints` | In-game guide book, common questions, hints |
| `/bounty` | Acting bounties |
| `/subspace` | Dungeons, their entrances and your cooldowns |
| `/emporium`, `/daily`, `/vote` | Brilliant Emporium, daily rewards, voting |
| `/wallet` | Your money |
| `/lands` | Create or manage a town |
| `/msg <player>`, `/r` | Private messages |

There is **no** `/home`, `/sethome`, `/spawn`, `/tpa`, `/rtp` or `/back`. The full list is on [Commands](/general/commands/).

## Before you settle

- Travel takes planning, because there are no teleport commands. Pick your base with care, or join a town.
- PvP is off in most of the world, and griefing is against the rules.
- Every chest in a generated structure is a Beyonder loot chest, and each one pays **you** once.
- The world resets between epochs. Epoch II began on 1 May 2026.

Read [World and Safety](/general/world-and-safety/) for the details, and the [server rules](https://www.mysterria.net/en/rules) before you play.
