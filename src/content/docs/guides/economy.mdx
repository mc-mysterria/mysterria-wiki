Mysterria has two currencies. **Money** (Coppets, Licks and Verl Dors) sits in your `/wallet` and pays for towns, the [Agora](/guides/agora/) and Wild Beyonder trades. **Energy Shards** are an item you get by selling spare Beyonder ingredients to villagers, and they pay for [Secret Order](/guides/orders/) levels and Wild Beyonder trades.

[Emporium points](/guides/emporium/) are a separate balance that only works in the Brilliant Emporium.

## What are Coppets, Licks and Verl Dors?

| Coin | Worth |
|---|---|
| **Coppet** | The base unit |
| **Lick** | 64 Coppets |
| **Verl Dor** | 64 Licks, or 4,096 Coppets |

Your balance is always counted in Coppets. When a menu shows a price with a `$` sign, such as a town level that asks for `$5000`, it means 5,000 Coppets.

## How do I use my wallet?

Type `/wallet`. The wallet menu shows your balance and links to the server store and Discord. Click **Balance** to open the vault screen, where you can:

- Click a coin to withdraw it as a physical item.
- Click a coin item in your inventory to deposit it into your balance.
- Click a sellable item from your inventory to see its price and confirm the sale.

The **Settings** button switches between three looks for the wallet menu.

Physical coins let you pay another player by hand or make trades that ask for a coin. To break a coin into smaller ones, hold **one** coin in your main hand and type `/shatter`. A Verl Dor becomes 64 Licks, and a Lick becomes 64 Coppets.

## How do I make money?

- Sell items anonymously to other players in the [Agora](/guides/agora/), the underground market.
- Claim [Battle Pass](/guides/battle-pass/) tiers. Free and premium tiers pay from 100 to 1,500 Coppets each.
- Sell ore in the wallet. It is small but steady.
- Trade with other players and take part in events.

### Which ores can I sell?

Only three ores have a price. Everything else sells for nothing.

| Ore | Price per block |
|---|---|
| Diamond Ore (and Deepslate Diamond Ore) | 2 Coppets |
| Emerald Ore (and Deepslate Emerald Ore) | 3 Coppets |
| Ancient Debris | 5 Coppets |

- Mine diamond and emerald ore with a **Silk Touch** pickaxe, so the ore drops as a block you can sell. Ancient Debris always drops as itself.
- You can sell up to **512 Coppets' worth per day**. The sell screen shows **Cannot sell** and your remaining daily limit once you reach it.

## What is money used for?

- Founding and running a [town](/guides/towns/), including extra claims, upkeep and levels.
- Buying from other players in the [Agora](/guides/agora/) and renting a stall there.
- [Wild Beyonder](/magic/wild-beyonders/) trades, which cost money, Energy Shards **and** physical Verl Dor coins.
- Some villager trades (below).

### Do villagers take coins?

Only for some trades. Trades for **enchanted books** and **items with durability** (tools, weapons, armour) cost Coppets instead of emeralds, at 2 Coppets per emerald of the original price. Every other trade still uses emeralds.

A villager only trades once its AI is switched off. Trap it in a 2 by 1 space it cannot walk out of.

### Is there a /shop?

No. Buy building blocks from the **Quartermaster** in the [Agora](/guides/agora/).

## Energy Shards

<Figure src={shard} alt="A stack of Energy Shards with the item tooltip: a fragment of mystical energy, used as currency for organization upgrades" caption="Energy Shards are a real item in your inventory, not a wallet balance." />

Energy Shards are not stored in `/wallet`. They sit in your inventory or a chest and can be lost or stolen like any other item.

### How do I get Energy Shards?

Sell spare Beyonder ingredients to a **villager with no profession** or a **nitwit**:

1. Carry the ingredients you want to sell.
2. Right-click the villager. A chat message offers to buy them: *"The wandering trader offers to buy N items for X Energy Shards"*.
3. Click **[Trade All]** in that message, or type `/tradeall`.
4. A **Trade Preview** menu lists everything it will buy and what each stack is worth. Click a stack to take it out of the deal.
5. Click **CONFIRM TRADE**. The ingredients are taken and the shards go into your inventory.

If you carry nothing it wants, the villager *"finds nothing of interest in your pack"*.

The villager buys **Pathway ingredients only**, not recipes, potions or other items. Stronger Sequences are worth more:

| Ingredient Sequence | Shards each (almost all ingredients) |
|---|---|
| Sequence 9 | 10 |
| Sequence 8 | 15 |
| Sequence 7 | 20 |
| Sequence 6 | 25 |
| Sequence 5 and stronger | 30 |

<Notice tone="warn" title="Check the preview">
The trade takes every matching stack in your inventory. Remove anything you want to keep in the Trade Preview before you confirm, or leave it in a chest first.
</Notice>

Energy Shards also come from the [Battle Pass](/guides/battle-pass/) (200 to 3,000 shards on many tiers) and from events.

### What are Energy Shards used for?

- [Secret Order](/guides/orders/) levels, which cost 50, 200, 1,000 and 5,000 shards for levels 1 to 4, paid into the Order's shared pool.
- Secret Order extras such as Battle Cry, permanent upgrades, founding a Secret Lair and its teleport crystals.
- [Wild Beyonder](/magic/wild-beyonders/) trades, together with money and physical Verl Dor coins.

The Emporium's **Shard of ...** items, such as *Shard of Boon Token*, are not Energy Shards. They are [crafting fragments](/guides/emporium/#what-are-emporium-fragments) for one specific reward.
