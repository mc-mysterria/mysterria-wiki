A **town** is a piece of protected land that you and your friends share. Nobody outside the town can build, break or open your chests there. Towns grow through **five levels**, can join into [nations and go to war](/guides/nations-and-wars/), and great builds can earn a permanent [Town Perk](#town-perks).

Towns use the Lands plugin, so every town command starts with `/lands` (or `/land`). Most things can also be done from the town menu, which opens when you type `/lands`.

## How do I create a town?

```
/lands create <name>
```

- It costs **32 Coppets**, taken from your [wallet](/guides/economy/).
- It claims a 3 by 3 area of chunks around you for free (the chunk you stand in plus one in every direction).
- The name needs 5 to 24 Latin letters or numbers. Spaces are allowed.
- You can own **one** town, and be trusted in one town.

## How do I claim more land?

```
/lands claim       claim the chunk you are standing in
/lands unclaim     unclaim it
```

| What | Cost |
|---|---|
| Each extra chunk | 10 Coppets |
| Unclaiming a chunk | You get 15% back |

- New claims must **touch your existing land**.
- You cannot claim within **1 chunk** of another town (3 chunks if that town belongs to a nation), unless that town gives your role permission to claim on its border.
- A town starts with room for **100 chunks**, plus **5 more for each trusted member**. Higher [levels](#town-levels) add more.
- By default **only the Owner** can claim. The Owner can give that permission to other roles in the role settings.

The short commands `/claim` and `/unclaim` are not available to players. Use `/lands claim` and `/lands unclaim`.

## Basic town commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/lands` | Open the town menu |
| `/lands trust <player>` | Invite a player. They accept with `/lands accept` |
| `/lands untrust <player>` | Remove a player |
| `/lands setrole <player> <role>` | Change a member's role |
| `/lands ban <player>` | Ban a player from your land |
| `/lands deposit <amount>` | Put money into the town bank |
| `/lands withdraw <amount>` | Take money out (Owner only by default) |
| `/lands rename <name>` | Rename the town for 10,000 Coppets, at most once a day |
| `/lands setowner <player>` | Hand the town to someone else for 5,000 Coppets from the town bank |
| `/lands upkeep`, `/lands taxes` | See the next upkeep and tax payments |
| `/lands delete` | Disband your town |

**Enter and leave messages** are set in the town menu with the **Enter Message** item. There are no `/lands greeting` or `/lands farewell` commands.

Land teleports (`/lands spawn`, `/lands wild`, teleporting to a chunk) cost so much that they are effectively switched off. Mysterria has no `/home` or `/tpa`, so plan your travel.

**Town chat:** type `!l`, `!land` or `!lands` in front of a message.

## Town roles

Each role controls what its players may do on your land. The Owner can change the permissions of every role in the town menu.

| Role | Who | Default permissions |
|---|---|---|
| **Owner** | The founder | Everything |
| **Admin** | Trusted officers | Build, break, use containers and doors, trust, untrust, ban and set roles, edit land and role settings. Cannot claim, withdraw money or manage wars by default |
| **Member** | Regular residents | Build, break, farm, use containers, deposit money |
| **Tenant** | Players renting an area | Build and use things in their rented area, and decide who else may use it |
| **Nation** | Members of the same nation | Use doors and mechanisms, enter, pick up items, claim on your border |
| **Ally** | Members of allied towns | The same as Nation |
| **Untrusted** | Any visitor | Enter, pick up items, attack monsters |

**PvP is off** in towns by default for every role. The Owner can turn it on per role, for example for an arena.

If you attack another player, you are combat-tagged for **20 seconds**. Logging out while tagged kills you.

## How much does a town cost to run?

### Upkeep

- Every town pays **3 Coppets per claimed chunk** every **7 days**, at 23:00. The money comes out of the town bank and goes to the server bank, *The Narrator*.
- New towns pay nothing for their first **2 days**.
- From 2 days before payment, members who can deposit get a reminder every hour if the bank is short.
- If the bank cannot pay, your chunks are **not** removed.
- Higher [levels](#town-levels) cut upkeep by up to 40%.

Towns in a nation pay taxes to the nation instead of upkeep to the server. See [Nations and Wars](/guides/nations-and-wars/).

### Taxes

The Owner can charge trusted members a tax each payment cycle, up to **10 Coppets** per area. A member who cannot pay is untrusted automatically.

## Town levels

A town levels up **automatically** when it meets the requirements, and **drops a level** if it stops meeting them, for example when the bank balance falls. Every level needs **5 trusted players** and a balance in the **town bank** (`/lands deposit`).

| Level | Name | Trusted players | Town bank | Extra claims | Extra member slots | Upkeep |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Shelter** | - | - | - | - | Full |
| 2 | **Hamlet** | 5 | 5,000 Coppets | +50 | +25 | -10% |
| 3 | **Parish** | 5 | 12,500 Coppets | +100 | +50 | -20% |
| 4 | **Borough** | 5 | 25,000 Coppets | +150 | +75 | -30% |
| 5 | **Domain** | 5 | 40,000 Coppets | +200 | +100 | -40% |

**Domain is the highest level.** There are no levels above it. A town starts with 26 member slots.

A Domain can found a **nation**. See [Nations and Wars](/guides/nations-and-wars/).

## Why towns matter for other systems

- A town claim is where you can hide your own [Agora](/guides/agora/) entrance.
- Churches can link their treasury to a town. See [Churches](/magic/churches/).
- Large towns with great builds can earn a [Town Perk](#town-perks).

## Town Perks

A **Town Perk** is a permanent area effect that staff place on a town after it finishes a major building project. It is not bought or claimed by command. Staff judge the build, the town and its people.

### How do I get a Town Perk?

1. **Build** something impressive that fits the perk you want, for example a cathedral, a great library or a fortress wall.
2. **Submit** it. Open a support ticket on Discord with the coordinates and screenshots.
3. **Evaluation.** Staff look at the build quality, the town's level and its population.
4. **Activation.** If approved, the perk is placed on an area of your town.

### Which perks exist?

| Perk | What it does | Town level | Trusted players | Demigods |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Agriculture** | Crops grow much faster | Hamlet (2) | 5 | - |
| **Mob Protection** | Hostile mobs do not spawn in the area | Borough (4) | 12 | - |
| **Potion Effects** | Lasting effects such as Speed, Haste or Night Vision | Domain (5) | 18 | - |
| **Experience Boost** | More experience while in the area | Domain (5) | 22 | - |
| **Armor Repair** | Slowly repairs worn armour | Domain (5) | 30 | - |
| **Acting Boost** | Faster Acting while you stand in the area | Domain (5) | 40 | 3 |
| **Spirituality** | Restores Spirituality on a timer | Domain (5) | 45 | 5 |

- **Trusted players** are the town's trusted members. **Demigods** are trusted members at Sequence 4 or stronger.
- The perk system was first written for towns with 10 levels. Experience Boost, Armor Repair, Acting Boost and Spirituality used to ask for levels 6, 8 and 10, which no longer exist. On today's scale the highest a town can be is **Domain**, so for those perks the player and Demigod counts and the build itself decide.
- The build should suggest the perk. A dragon statue fits Mob Protection, and a cathedral fits Spirituality.

### How strong are the perks?

Each perk is set up by staff for its town, so numbers differ. Perks active on the server today include:

- **Acting Boost:** +18% Acting speed in one town and +45% in another, for players standing in the area.
- **Spirituality:** 50 Spirituality every 20 seconds in three towns, 50 every 10 seconds in one, and 30 every 10 seconds in another.
- **Mob Protection:** a 100-block radius around one town's centre.

A perk covers a circle around a monument, a box or other shape around a building, or a town's whole claim.

### Tips for approval

1. **Fit the theme.** The perk should match what you built.
2. **Scale matters.** Small houses will not qualify. Think of a world wonder that defines the landscape.
3. **Finish it.** Do not submit unfinished builds or empty shells. Staff check inside and out.
