**Couriers** are undead messengers that carry items to other players, wherever they are and even while they are offline. Summon one with a **Horn of Summoning**, give it your items and pick the recipient.

## How do I get a Horn of Summoning?

1. Find **Courier Horn Shards** in loot chests in the Overworld. Each newly generated chest has a **15%** chance to hold one.
2. Collect **3 shards** and craft them in an **L shape** in a crafting table:

<Figure src={hornCraft} alt="Three Courier Horn Shards in an L shape in a crafting grid, making a Horn of Summoning" caption="The Horn of Summoning recipe." contain />

The horn is **not used up**. You can summon a courier with it once every **5 minutes**.

## Courier types

Your horn always summons the best courier you have access to.

| Courier | Items it carries | Delivery time | Waits for your items | How to get it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Skeleton** (Bone Runner) | 1 | 5 minutes | 5 minutes | Any Horn of Summoning |
| **Jellyfish** | 2 | 5 minutes | 5 minutes | Jellyfish Courier in the [store](https://www.mysterria.net/en/store) |
| **Incinerated Courier** | 2 | 3 minutes | 3 minutes | Incinerated Courier in the [store](https://www.mysterria.net/en/store) |

Couriers carry **single items only**, not stacks. A stack of 64 is refused, so split it first. **Shulker boxes** are refused too.

## How do I send items?

1. **Right-click** with the Horn of Summoning. Your courier appears next to you.
2. **Drop** each item (press `Q`) within **3 blocks** of the courier. It takes the item and confirms in chat.
3. When the courier is full (1 item for a Skeleton, 2 for the others), the **Select Recipient** menu opens after about 2 seconds.
4. Pick the player, then click **Confirm Delivery**.

The courier leaves with your items. You and the recipient both get a chat message when the delivery starts and when it arrives.

If you do not finish in time, the courier disappears after its waiting time (5 minutes, or 3 for the Incinerated Courier) and any items it already took come back to you.

## Where do couriers work?

**Summoning** works in the Overworld, the Nether, the Manipulation Pool, the Mirror Realm, the Nightmare Pool and a [Secret Lair](/guides/orders/#secret-lair-level-4). It does not work in the End.

**Delivery** only happens while the recipient is in the **Overworld** or the **Nether**. If the recipient is somewhere else, in spectator mode or above Y 320, the courier waits and tries again every 5 seconds until they are back.

## Can I send items to an offline player?

Yes. The items are kept and delivered the next time the player logs in, once the delivery time has passed. A delivery that is still waiting after **30 days** is deleted, so do not send items to players who have left the server.

## Couriers and the Agora

Leave your Horn of Summoning at the **Courier Post** in the [Agora](/guides/agora/#courier-post) and everything you buy there is delivered by your courier. You can take the horn back whenever you like.
