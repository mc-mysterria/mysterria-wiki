Outer Gods Anchors (OGA) is a server-wide co-op event. An Outer God plants a seed in the wild, the land around it turns into alien corrupted terrain, and defenders spawn to protect it. Players fight the corruption back, break the god's heart, and spend the **Contribution Points** they earn in the `/oga rewards` shop, the only shop that sells every Boon Token.

Other players cannot hurt you inside an anchor, and you cannot die there, so it is safe for every Sequence.

## Which Outer Gods can appear?

Each anchor belongs to one of five Outer Gods, with its own look, its own debuff and its own growth speed:

- Circle of Inevitability
- Monarch of Decay (grows slowest)
- Mother Goddess of Depravity
- Mother Tree of Desire (grows fast)
- Primordial Hunger (grows fastest)

## When does an anchor appear?

The server tries to plant a new anchor about **every 3 days** (give or take 12 hours), and only when at least **5 players** are online. No attempts start between 02:00 and 08:00 server time.

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Where | Overworld only, 300 to 3,000 blocks from 0, 0, never in oceans or rivers |
| Distance from towns | 80 to 160 blocks from the nearest claim |
| How many | At most 3 anchors at once |

### Phase 1: Incubation

For about **24 hours** the seed is hidden. Every 30 minutes the chat carries a rumour that names the god and how long is left, with a short countdown bar. Players within 40 blocks of the hidden seed hear its ambient sound.

### Phase 2: The anchor awakens

When incubation ends, the chat announces the god and the **coordinates**. The seed becomes a **Heart**: a Magma block inside a Bedrock shell. Corruption spreads from it in tendrils, up to 400 blocks out, and defenders spawn: [Wild Beyonders](/magic/wild-beyonders/), hostile NPCs and, from stage 2, mini-bosses.

`/oga where` lists every active anchor with its coordinates, integrity, stage and direction from you.

## How do we defeat an anchor?

The anchor has an **Integrity** bar (shown as a boss bar with its stage, 1 to 5). You cannot hit the Heart while it is shielded. Instead you wear Integrity down from the outside:

| Action | Integrity damage |
|---|---|
| Kill a defender | 0.1 |
| Kill an NPC | 0.5 |
| Kill a mini-boss | 3 |
| Destroy a sector Nexus | 5 |

If nobody fights, the anchor slowly heals, but never above the best stage players have pushed it to, so progress carries over between evenings.

### Phase 3: Heart Raid

Below **10% Integrity** the shell cracks open with lava breaches. Break the Magma Heart within **45 minutes**. If you fail, the Heart shields itself for 2 minutes, regains 15 Integrity and grows faster for a while.

When the Heart breaks, the defenders vanish and the land slowly restores itself over the next 24 hours.

:::note[Your buildings are safe]
The corruption never enters land claims, and it never touches chests, other containers, signs, beds or similar blocks. Unclaimed builds can be overgrown, but everything is restored after the anchor is defeated.
:::

### What happens to an anchor nobody fights?

After 72 hours with nobody fighting it, the god starves: it stops healing, loses Integrity and finally collapses on its own. Contributors then get only **half** of what a victory would have paid.

## Which tools help against the corruption?

| Tool | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Nexus** | The field is split into 5 sectors, each with a Nexus (a crying obsidian block) near the fight. Mine it (several hits) to stop growth in that direction for good and damage the anchor |
| **Ritual** | 3 or more players stand within 6 blocks of a Nexus for 20 seconds. Growth slows to 40% for 10 minutes. Each Nexus can host one every 15 minutes |
| **Warding Pylon** | Place a **Lodestone** inside the field, then right-click it with **Glowstone Dust** to fuel it (1 minute per dust, up to 30 minutes). A fuelled Pylon holds the corruption back in its direction, keeps defenders from spawning within 24 blocks, and drains your Corruption Pressure. Up to 3 per anchor |
| **Censer** | Bought in `/oga rewards`. Right-click a corrupted block to turn the ground back to normal in a small radius. 5 charges, 15 seconds apart. Enough cleansing pushes the edge back |

Pylons and Censers also earn Contribution Points, so players who do not like fighting can still help.

## What is Corruption Pressure?

Inside the field a **Corruption Pressure** bar fills up. Weaker Sequences fill it faster: a Sequence 9 Beyonder fills it about ten times as fast as a Sequence 0.

- Kills relieve pressure, and standing by a fuelled Pylon drains it.
- Above 50% the god's own debuff starts to affect you, getting stronger as the bar fills.
- At 100% you take 1 damage per second (never lethal). After 8 seconds you are thrown 15 blocks outside the field with a little Madness (never pushing you above 95), and your pressure resets.

Step outside for 30 seconds to let it drain.

## What happens if I fall in battle?

You cannot die inside an anchor. Fatal damage leaves you **Downed**: you cannot move or cast and you glow. An ally who sneaks next to you for 5 seconds revives you with 10 health. If nobody revives you within 45 seconds, you are put outside the field with a short debuff and lose 15% of your score for that anchor. You keep your items.

## How do Contribution Points work?

You earn Contribution Points for kills, mini-bosses, breaking a Nexus, rituals, fuelling Pylons, cleansing, reviving allies, holding ground while you fight, and a big bonus for breaking the Heart. Holding ground only counts if you have fought in the last 3 minutes, so standing idle earns nothing.

Weaker Sequences earn more for the same work: up to x1.3 at Sequence 9, down to x0.85 at Sequence 0. You need at least 10 points on an anchor to be paid. Your points are saved between anchors, so you can save up.

## Rules

PvP, stealing and griefing during the event are forbidden. Friendly fire is off inside the corrupted area anyway, and Madness from other players' abilities is blocked. See the [Rules](https://www.mysterria.net/en/rules).
