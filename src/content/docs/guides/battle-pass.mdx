The **Battle Pass** turns ordinary play into rewards. You complete quests, quests give points, and points raise your tier. Many tiers carry a reward: money, Energy Shards, Emporium points, Acting Bottles, dungeon keys and two boons.

Open it with `/battlepass` or `/bp`.

## How does the Battle Pass work?

1. Complete **daily quests** and **weekly quests**. Each one gives Battle Pass points.
2. Points fill your tier. When a tier is full you move to the next one.
3. When you reach a reward tier, open **Rewards** in the Battle Pass menu and **click the reward to claim it**. Rewards are not given automatically.

Every player has the **free pass**. The **premium pass** adds a second reward track and extra premium-only quests.

## Quests

### Daily quests

- You have **9 daily quests** at a time: 4 easy, 3 medium and 2 hard.
- They reset every day at **11:59 Kyiv time**.
- Each is worth **25 to 50 points**. Examples: tame 3 wolves, reach experience level 15.

### Weekly quests

- A new week of quests unlocks every **7 days**, on Saturday at 07:00 Kyiv time. The current season started on 16 May 2026 and has **26 weeks**; the last week unlocks on 7 November 2026.
- Earlier weeks stay open, and you do not need to finish one week to start the next.
- Most weekly quests are worth **95 points**. Premium-only weekly quests are worth **112**.

## How many points do I need?

| Tiers | Points per tier |
|---|---|
| Ordinary tiers 1 to 75 | 150 |
| Reward tiers 1 to 75 | 200 |
| Ordinary tiers 76 to 100 | 150 |
| Reward tiers 76 to 100 | 350 |

That adds up to **11,750 points to reach tier 75** and **16,900 points to reach tier 100**, the end of the pass. Tiers 76 to 100 are bonus tiers for players who keep going.

## What do the rewards do?

| Reward | What it is |
|---|---|
| Coppets | Money, added to your `/wallet`. See [Money and Wallet](/guides/economy/) |
| Energy Shards | Item currency for Secret Orders and Wild Beyonders. See [Energy Shards](/guides/economy/#energy-shards) |
| Emporium points | Spend them in the [Brilliant Emporium](/guides/emporium/) |
| Acting Bottle | Throw it to gain acting points for your current Sequence. See [Acting](/magic/acting/) |
| Boon of the Second Law, Boon of the Devouring | A Boon Token that unlocks that boon at Sequence 9. You advance it by sacrificing Beyonder items. See [Boons](/magic/boons/) |
| Recipe Sheets | Collect 3 or 4 sheets to form a full recipe |
| Recipe Token | Exchange it for any recipe up to Sequence 4 |
| Ingredient Token | Exchange it for any known ingredient |
| Simple Key | One solo re-entry to a dungeon. See [Dungeons](/guides/dungeons/) |
| Premium Key | A full party re-entry to a dungeon |
| Boss Compass | Points to the nearest world boss, used up on use. See [Rift Bosses](/guides/bosses/) |
| Universal Cosmetic Token | Apply a custom skin to a weapon or tool |
| Chat Color Package | Colours and formatting in every chat channel for 365 days |

## How do I get the premium pass?

The **Premium BattlePass** is sold in the [server store](https://www.mysterria.net/en/store). It unlocks the premium reward track above and the premium-only weekly quests.

<Notice tone="info" title="Free players get the big rewards too">
The free track has its own boon (Second Law at tier 41) and an Elytra at tier 75.
</Notice>
