### Overview

Franca Roland's oversized brass revolver. Its bullets punch through cover and throw targets back, and its recoil
punishes anyone without a Beyonder's body. Its real strength is what it can become: the
[Inevitable Gun](/firearms/inevitable-gun/).

### Statistics

| | |
|---|---|
| **Tier** | III |
| **Damage** | 16 per shot |
| **Magazine** | 5 rounds |
| **Range** | 45 blocks |
| **Shot every** | 1.1 s |
| **Reload** | The whole cylinder at once, 3.0 s |
| **Rounds** | Cannon Rounds |
| **Heard from** | 48 blocks |

### Heavy Strike

- Bullets keep **full damage through doors and glass**.
- Special rounds never hit for less than the gun's base damage.
- A hit **blasts the target backwards**.

**Recoil.** Beyonders of **Sequence 7 or stronger** handle it. Anyone weaker (mortals included) is shoved back with each
shot and may hurt their wrist (35%): 3 damage, Mining Fatigue II and Weakness for 5 seconds. The Recoil Dampener
attachment softens it.

### How it plays

**Strengths:** heavy damage that cover can't stop, and knockback that controls a fight.

**Weaknesses:** slow fire, five rounds, short range, one of the two loudest guns, and it doesn't take a Suppressor.

### Getting it

Draft its blueprint for **30 Notes III**, then forge its three parts: **Reinforced Cannon Barrel, Heavy Cannon
Cylinder, Brass Cannon Frame**. Assemble it at a **Demigod Weaponsmith Table**; it also takes a **Heavy Core**. See
[Getting a Gun](/firearms/crafting/).

### Attachments

**Recoil Dampener** (much less recoil, a little faster), **Ballast Chamber** (more damage and knockback), **Long Bore
Extension** (range, tighter spread). Details on [Upgrades & Upkeep](/firearms/upgrades/).

### Skins

Gunmetal (300 kills), Broadside, Sunforge, and the mythic **Mirror of Higdon**. The Cannon Gun and the Inevitable Gun
share their skins.
