### Overview

Anthony Reid's black revolver, tainted by the Monarch of Decay. Every bullet it fires carries one of two corruptions,
**Sure Hit** or **Certain Death**, with no limit on how often.

### Statistics

| | |
|---|---|
| **Tier** | III |
| **Damage** | 12 per shot |
| **Magazine** | 6 rounds |
| **Range** | 70 blocks |
| **Shot every** | 0.9 s |
| **Reload** | The whole cylinder at once, 2.8 s |
| **Rounds** | Winter Rounds |
| **Heard from** | 32 blocks |

### Decay

Press **swap hands (F)** to switch between the two modes (with the [HUD mod](/firearms/hud-mod/), the ability key
does it too). The gun's lore shows the current one.

- **Sure Hit** - the bullet bends onto the visible creature closest to your aim (within 25° of the crosshair). Moving
  doesn't save the target; breaking line of sight does.
- **Certain Death** - anything weaker than a demigod **dies outright** (Circle of Imagination's death-aversion
  abilities still get their chance). **Demigods** (Beyonders of Sequence 4 and stronger, Beyonder creatures and
  bosses) instead lose **20%** of their health at once and keep rotting **4% a second for 20 seconds**, unless they heal
  faster than that.

### How it plays

**Strengths:** decay on every single shot, good range, and the whole cylinder reloaded in one go.

**Weaknesses:** the lowest base damage of the tier III guns, and it takes a tier III blueprint and a Demigod table to
build.

### Getting it

Draft its blueprint for **30 Notes III**, then forge its three parts: **Winter Barrel, Winter Cylinder, Winter
Frame**. Assemble it at a **Demigod Weaponsmith Table**; it also takes a **Heavy Core**. See
[Getting a Gun](/firearms/crafting/).

### Attachments

**Frost Coil** (more damage), **Rime Sights** (tighter spread, faster), **Glacial Speed Cylinder** (faster reload, +1
round at level 3). Details on [Upgrades & Upkeep](/firearms/upgrades/).

### Skins

Copper Rose (100 headshots), Crimson Covenant, Rime Sovereign, and the mythic **Heart of the Long Night**.

<Aside type="tip">
Keep **Sure Hit** on for moving targets and fights at range, and switch to **Certain Death** for the finishing shot.
</Aside>
