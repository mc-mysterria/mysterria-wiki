### Attachments

Every gun has **three attachments of its own**, each with **three levels**. Fit them on the Weaponsmith Table's
**Workshop** page: put the gun in, click an attachment.

- **Fitting** an attachment uses up its **attachment blueprint** (draft one from Notes, see
  [Getting a Gun](/firearms/crafting/)). Raising it to the next level needs no blueprint.
- A new level replaces the old one. Materials come from your inventory:

| | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 |

| Tempered Steel | 6 | 10 | 16 |

### Kill Tally

Also on the Workshop page: a counter that keeps the gun's kills (shown on the gun and its card). It costs 4 Precision
Gears, 2 Coiled Springs, 1 Gunsmith Oil, 4 Brass, 1 Clock and 1 Comparator.

### Beyonder grades

On the **Upgrade** page a gun takes a **Beyonder grade**, one sequence at a time: first Sequence 6, then 5, and so on
down to 0. Each step uses **2 Beyonder ingredients** of that sequence (any pathway). Grades 3 to 0 need a **Demigod
table**.

**Why it matters:** in a fight between Beyonders, an ordinary gun fires **2 sequences below its wielder**. A graded
gun carries its wielder's full power, up to its grade. A gun's grade never goes past its wielder; Beyonder rounds are
the way to hit above your own sequence (see [Ammunition](/firearms/ammunition/)). The Upgrade page shows what each
grade does in numbers.

### Upkeep: fouling and cleaning

Every shot fouls the gun a little (about 0.35% a shot; the Shotgun, Cannon Gun and Inevitable Gun more, the Steam Chatterbox only 0.05%, special rounds
×1.5). Rain, water and sand speed it up.

| Fouling | What happens |
|---------|--------------|
| **40%+** | Shots spread wider, up to +2.5° |
| **60%+** | The gun may **jam** (up to 6% a shot); clearing it takes a moment |
| **Under water** | Wet powder: a third of shots misfire |

**Cleaning Kit:** click it onto the gun in your inventory to clean it fully. Recipe on
[Getting a Gun](/firearms/crafting/).

**Dying** fouls the guns you carry by 15% and marks them **battered** until cleaned.

### Suppressor

Click a Suppressor onto a gun in your inventory to fit it. A suppressed gun is heard at a quarter of the distance and
much quieter, but it fouls 50% faster. A suppressor lasts **150 shots**. It doesn't fit the Cannon Gun or the
Inevitable Gun.

### Engraving

Stand within 4 blocks of a Weaponsmith Table and run `/mwengrave <name>` to engrave a name on the gun in your hand (up to
24 characters, 5 experience levels). It shows first in the gun's lore and on its card. `/mwengrave clear` removes it
for free. `/mwengrave` on its own opens a text field to type the name in.
