### Overview

Franca's Cannon Gun after it absorbed the remnants of a Monarch of Decay divine boon. It keeps the Cannon Gun's Heavy
Strike and adds the decay, rot and disease. It hits harder than any other gun, and it can't be built: it has to be
made in a ritual.

### Statistics

| | |
|---|---|
| **Damage** | 22 per shot |
| **Magazine** | 5 rounds |
| **Range** | 55 blocks |
| **Shot every** | 1.1 s |
| **Reload** | The whole cylinder at once, 3.0 s |
| **Rounds** | Inevitable Rounds |
| **Heard from** | 48 blocks |

It has the Cannon Gun's **Heavy Strike** and recoil (see [Cannon Gun](/firearms/cannon-gun/)), and doesn't take a
Suppressor.

### The Vortex ritual

At a **Demigod Weaponsmith Table**, the **Vortex** page turns a Cannon Gun into the Inevitable Gun. What it asks is
never said outright: the page speaks in **riddles** about the gun, the deaths it has caused, what you lay with it and
your own strength, and every player draws riddles of their own. Only a strong Beyonder can perform it, and the
backlash costs them sanity.

The gun's skin and Kill Tally carry over; its attachments don't (each gun has its own).

### Decay charges

The gun holds **13 decay charges**. Press **swap hands (F)** to switch between three modes, or use the ability wheel of
the [HUD mod](/firearms/hud-mod/):

- **Plain shot** - spends nothing, and builds **rot** (below).
- **Sure Hit** (1 charge) - the bullet bends onto the creature closest to your aim, **even behind walls**. If it
  kills, the bullet **jumps once** to the nearest enemy within 8 blocks. If there's nobody to bend towards, the charge
  comes back.
- **Certain Death** (1 charge) - anything weaker than a demigod dies outright. Demigods lose **30%** of their health
  at once and rot **5% a second for 20 seconds**.

**Refilling.** Killing a **player, a Beyonder creature or a boss** with the gun gives a charge back. Ordinary mobs and
spawners give nothing.

### Rot

Every plain hit leaves rot on the target. The **third hit within 8 seconds collapses it**: the target loses **10% of
its maximum health**. On a player, a Beyonder creature or a boss the gun also gets a decay charge back.

### Recoil leap

**Sneak and fire at the ground** (looking down) and the kick throws you into the air, away from where you aim. That
landing takes no fall damage. Once every 3 seconds.

### Plague Cloud

**Sneak + swap hands** (or the ability wheel) releases a cloud where you aim, up to 30 blocks away, for **2 charges**.
For 6 seconds everyone hostile in it (4 blocks around) is slowed, withered and **can't heal naturally**. It recharges
in 20 seconds.

### Contagion aura

While the gun is **in your main hand**, every 5 seconds enemies within 32 blocks may catch a disease: poison, hunger,
weakness, nausea, slowness or mining fatigue.

<Aside>
The rot, the Plague Cloud and the aura only touch **enemies**: hostile mobs, Beyonder creatures, bosses and anyone
in a fight with you in the last 15 seconds. Friends, pets, villagers and passers-by are safe.
</Aside>

### Attachments

**Vortex Rifling** (range, tighter spread), **Ossuary Chamber** (more damage, more recoil), **Hungering Breech**
(faster reload and fire, more recoil). Details on [Upgrades & Upkeep](/firearms/upgrades/).

### Skins

Desert Bone (40 Beyonder kills), Pestilent Bloom, Crown of Ending, and the mythic **Mirror of Higdon**, shared with the
Cannon Gun.
