**Mystical Weaponry HUD** is an optional Fabric mod for Mysterria's guns. It replaces the action-bar text with a proper
HUD and adds controls that a server alone can't give you.

<Aside>
**Optional.** Every gun works without it, and the server shows its own feedback to players without it (see
[Feedback & Settings](/firearms/feedback-settings/)). Players running it deal **+10% gun damage**, since the mod's controls are
part of how the guns are meant to be played.
</Aside>

### Getting it

The mod is for **Fabric, Minecraft 26.3**, and needs Fabric API.

### What it adds

- **Weapon HUD** - the gun's ammo, reload progress, loaded rounds and mastery in one brass plate, instead of
  action-bar text.
- **Crosshair** - shows your real spread: it opens while you move or jump, and recolours when you lose accuracy. Hit
  markers, a sound for headshots, damage numbers, an arrow towards whatever hurt you.
- **Ammo wheel** - hold the reload key to pick which rounds to load from everything you carry.
- **Ability wheel** - for Winter is Coming and the Inevitable Gun: hold the ability key to pick a decay mode or the
  Plague Cloud; tap it to switch to the next mode.
- **Scope** - the Steam-Pressured Rifle's scope with 2×, 4×, 8× and 16× zoom.
- **Aim down sights** - on every other gun, the scope key raises the sights with a slight zoom.
- **Pressure gauge** - the Steam Chatterbox's boiler pressure and the vent on the HUD.
- **Gun info** - stats, attachments and the Kill Tally of the gun in your hand.
- **Skins menu** - pick a skin for the gun in your hand.
- **First-person gun on top** - your gun is drawn over the world like the vanilla hand, so it never sinks into a wall,
  and the arms holding it wear your own skin.

### Keys

| Key | Default |
|-----|---------|
| Reload (hold: ammo wheel) | R |
| Ability (tap: next mode, hold: ability wheel) | V |
| Scope / aim down sights | Z |
| Inspect the gun | I |
| Gun info | K |
| Weapon skins | J |
| HUD settings | `/mwhud` (or bind a key) |

Change them in Minecraft's **Controls** menu, under Mystical Weaponry.

### Settings

`/mwhud` opens the settings: crosshair style and colours, HUD position and size, which effects you see, scope
sensitivity and more.

Zoom levels, the scope's blur and dropping out of the scope when you fire are the same for everyone: they're part of
the game's balance, not preferences.
