### Overview

A steam-pressure automatic carbine with a thirty-round brass drum. Hold the trigger and it fires five shots a second.
Every shot builds pressure in its boiler, and when the gauge is full the gun **vents** and can't fire for a moment.

### Statistics

| | |
|---|---|
| **Tier** | II |
| **Damage** | 3 per bullet |
| **Magazine** | 30 rounds |
| **Range** | 40 blocks |
| **Shot every** | 0.2 s (300 rounds a minute) |
| **Reload** | The whole drum at once, 3.2 s |
| **Rounds** | Chatterbox Rounds |
| **Heard from** | 36 blocks |

### Holding the trigger

**Hold right-click** to fire and let go to stop. You don't need the [HUD mod](/firearms/hud-mod/) for this. While
firing you hold the gun in both hands and still walk and sprint at full speed.

**Sneak** to aim down the sights (with the HUD mod, use its scope key).

### Pressure

Every shot adds pressure; the gauge shows after your ammo count. After **18 shots** without a break the boiler
**vents**: a hiss and a cloud of steam everyone around can see, and **1.6 s** in which the gun can't fire. Pressure
drains when you stop shooting: give the trigger **0.3 s** of rest and the gauge empties at 6 shots' worth a second.

The fuller the gauge, the wider your bullets spread: from **0.2°** on an empty gauge to **2.4°** on a full one, on top
of the spread from moving or jumping.

### Recoil

Over a burst the gun climbs **up and to the right**, along the same pattern every time, so you can learn it: pull
down and a little to the left. A pause of about a third of a second ends the burst, and the next shot starts the
pattern from the beginning.

### How it plays

**Strengths:** the most damage per second up close and at mid range, a thirty-round drum, and it barely fouls (a
whole drum adds 1.5%). Every bullet lands: targets don't get the usual break between hits, and they aren't knocked
back out of your sights.

**Weaknesses:** low damage per bullet, a slow reload, and long bursts climb, spread and end in a vent at the worst
moment. It eats rounds quickly.

### Getting it

Draft its blueprint for **20 Notes II** (or find it in stronghold, mansion and mineshaft chests and in trial vaults),
then forge its four parts: **Chatterbox Barrel, Chatterbox Drum, Chatterbox Boiler, Chatterbox Receiver**. Tier II
parts need Aether Filament and Volatile Essence. See [Getting a Gun](/firearms/crafting/).

### Attachments

**Pressure Regulator** (less pressure per shot and less climb), **Extended Drum** (more rounds, slower reload),
**Cooling Jacket** (tighter bursts on the move, longer range). Details on [Upgrades & Upkeep](/firearms/upgrades/).

### Skins

Soot & Verdigris (20 bursts that kill 3 or more), Abyssal Diver, Cathedral Organ, and the mythic **Calamity
Engine**. The store skins change the sound of every shot and of the vent too.

<Aside type="tip">
Fire in short bursts of 5-8 shots. They stay on target, the pressure drains between them, and the gun never vents
in the middle of a fight.
</Aside>
