The **Steam Rifle** (announced as the Steam-Pressured Rifle) is the long-range gun. It hits for 20 damage, reaches 100 blocks and has a scope, but it fires slowest and holds only 5 rounds.

Its numbers are in the [stats table](/firearms/#weapon-stats): 20 damage, 5 rounds, 100 blocks range and 1.2 seconds between shots.

## How do I get one?

You need five parts, and **none of them can be crafted**:

- Steam Rifle Barrel
- Steam Pipe Assembly
- Mechanical Power Box
- Military Rifle Stock
- Sniper Scope

Parts come from chest loot in the Overworld. Each time a chest in a vanilla structure generates its loot, there is a 25% chance it also holds one random Deathknell or Steam Rifle part. You can also trade for parts with other players.

With all five in your inventory, right-click a [Weaponsmith Table](/firearms/crafting/#how-do-i-build-a-gun) to check, then Shift + right-click to build.

## What ammo does it use?

Only **Rifle Rounds** and the special Rifle Rounds made from them (13 kinds in all).

- 1 Rifle Cartridge Case + 1 Rifle Cartridge Core + 2 Gunpowder = **3 Rifle Rounds**.
- 3 Brass + 1 Iron Ingot + 12 Gunpowder makes **18 rounds**. It is the most Brass-hungry ammo.

The [Ammunition](/firearms/ammunition/) page has the case and core recipes and all special rounds.

## How do I use the scope?

Hold **Sneak** while holding the rifle to look through the scope. Let go to stop. The zoom comes from a Slowness effect, so you move slowly while scoped. Scoping does not improve accuracy by itself. Standing still does (see [Accuracy](/firearms/#accuracy)).

## How does reloading work?

The rifle has two reload modes, depending on whether any rounds are left.

| Magazine when you reload | What happens |
|---|---|
| Empty | All rounds go in together after **3.0 seconds**. Nothing is usable before that. |
| 1 to 4 rounds left | The first round goes in after **1.0 second**, then one more every **0.75 seconds**. Topping up 4 rounds takes 3.25 seconds. |

<Notice tone="warn" title="You cannot shoot mid-reload">
Firing is blocked while the reload runs ("Weapon is reloading!"). Switching to another hotbar slot cancels it. On a top-up, the rounds already loaded stay in the gun. On a reload from empty, nothing is loaded if you cancel before the 3 seconds are up.
</Notice>

Because a reload from empty is all or nothing, it often pays to top up after a shot or two instead of firing all 5 first.

When you reload from empty, the rifle takes the first stack of Rifle Rounds in your inventory. When it is partly loaded, it only tops up with the same kind of round already inside.
