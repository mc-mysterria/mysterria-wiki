### Overview

A bolt-action rifle driven by steam pressure, built for long range. The highest damage of the blueprint guns and a
scope, paid for with a slow bolt and a five-round magazine.

### Statistics

| | |
|---|---|
| **Tier** | II |
| **Damage** | 20 per shot |
| **Magazine** | 5 rounds |
| **Range** | 100 blocks |
| **Shot every** | 1.2 s |
| **Reload** | Empty: a stripper clip loads all five at once. Part-loaded: one round at a time |
| **Rounds** | Rifle Rounds |
| **Heard from** | 40 blocks |

### Scope

**Sneak** to look through the scope. With the [HUD mod](/firearms/hud-mod/), the scope key zooms 2× → 4× → 8× → 16×.
Each shot drops you out of the scope while the bolt cycles. Stand still: moving blurs the lens and spreads the shot.

### How it plays

**Strengths:** the longest range and the hardest hit of the blueprint guns, deadly headshots from far away.

**Weaknesses:** a slow bolt, only five rounds, and it's loud. Up close the faster guns win.

### Getting it

Draft its blueprint for **20 Notes II** (Notes III work too), then forge its five parts: **Steam Rifle Barrel, Steam
Pipe Assembly, Mechanical Power Box, Military Rifle Stock, Steam Rifle Bolt**. Tier II parts need Aether Filament and
Volatile Essence. See [Getting a Gun](/firearms/crafting/).

### Attachments

**Pressure Accelerator** (more damage, faster), **Precision Bore** (range, much tighter spread), **Stripper Clip
Guide** (faster reload). Details on [Upgrades & Upkeep](/firearms/upgrades/).

### Skins

Expedition (50 kills from 40 blocks or more), Leviathan's Breath, Aether Chronometer, and the mythic **Celestial
Orrery**.

<Aside type="tip">
Find a spot where you can stand still: a standing shot goes exactly where you aim, which is what the rifle's range is
for.
</Aside>
