### Overview

A nine-shot revolver carbine with a second, smoothbore barrel underneath for a single load of buckshot. Fast rifled
shots at range, one heavy blast up close.

### Statistics

| | Rifled barrel | Buckshot barrel |
|---|---|---|
| **Damage** | 8 per shot | 40 |
| **Magazine** | 9 rounds | 1 |
| **Range** | 50 blocks | 20 blocks |
| **Reload** | One round at a time, 0.7 s each | 1.5 s |
| **Rounds** | Le Mat Rounds | Le Mat Buckshot |

**Tier** II. **Shot every** 0.5 s. **Heard from** 36 blocks.

### Switching barrels

Press **swap hands (F)** to switch between the barrels. A reload loads the barrel you have selected.

### How it plays

**Strengths:** the fastest fire and the biggest magazine of the revolvers, plus a 40-damage answer to anything that gets close.

**Weaknesses:** low damage per rifled shot; the buckshot barrel holds one load and is slow to reload.

### Getting it

Draft its blueprint for **20 Notes II**, then forge its five parts: **High-Capacity Cylinder, Central Smoothbore
Barrel, Extended Rifled Barrel, Reinforced Stock Assembly, Articulated Hammer**. See
[Getting a Gun](/firearms/crafting/).

### Attachments

**Rifled Choke** (range, tighter spread), **Twin-Bore Linkage** (faster reload), **Grapeshot Cylinder** (more damage,
slightly slower). Details on [Upgrades & Upkeep](/firearms/upgrades/).

### Skins

Gilded Ivory (250 kills), Wraithbound, Dragon's Hoard, and the mythic **Crimson Moon Hunt**.
