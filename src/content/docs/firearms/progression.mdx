### Bounties

Every day you get **3 daily bounties**, and every week **2 weekly bounties**: kill 30 mobs with any gun, land 10
headshots with a random gun, kill Beyonder creatures, and so on. They pay Gunsmith's Notes and gunsmith materials;
weekly bounties pay Notes II and III, Volatile Essence and Resonant Cores.

- `/mwbounties` shows your bounties and their progress, and so does the [Field Tablet](/firearms/field-tablet/)'s
  **Contracts** tab.
- Claim finished bounties at a **Bounty Board**. Recipe: 3 Paper on top, then a Stick, a Lectern and a Stick.
- **Swap a bounty** you don't like: shift-click it and confirm to trade it for another one you don't have yet. A daily swap
  costs **3 Notes I** (twice a day), a weekly swap **3 Notes II** (once a week). The bounty's progress is lost.

### The weekly challenge

Each week one challenge runs for everyone: the most headshots, the most long shots with the Steam Rifle, the most
kills with the Klein's Repeater, the most Beyonder kills and others. `/mwweekly` shows this week's challenge and the
leaderboard. When the week ends the top 10 are rewarded:

| Place | Reward |
|-------|--------|
| 1st | 10 Notes III, 2 Resonant Cores, 6 Volatile Essence, 6 Aether Filament |
| 2nd | 6 Notes III, 1 Resonant Core, 4 Volatile Essence, 4 Aether Filament |
| 3rd | 4 Notes III, 3 Volatile Essence, 3 Aether Filament, 6 Precision Gears |
| 4th-10th | 3 Notes II, 6 Tempered Steel, 4 Precision Gears |

If you're offline when the week ends, you get your reward the next time you join.

### Mastery

Each gun has its own mastery, 10 levels. A kill with it gives XP: **10** for a mob, **30** for a player, **40** for a
Beyonder, **+5** for a headshot and **+5** for a shot from 40 blocks or more. Mobs from spawners don't count.

Every level makes the gun a little better in your hands: **−1.5% spread** and **−4% jam chance** per level. `/mwmastery`
shows your mastery of every gun.

### Titles

44 titles for gun deeds, from **Gunhand** (1,000 kills) and **Sharpshooter** (500 headshots) to **One With Every Gun**
(every gun mastered). There's a title for mastering each gun, for collecting skins, for winning the weekly challenge,
for bounties, and one for each mythic skin you raise to its third stage. `/mwtitles` shows your progress; titles
you earn appear in the server's titles menu.

### Skins

`/mwskin` changes the skin of the gun in your hand. Every gun has four skins:

| Gun | Standard (earn it) | Unlock challenge |
|-----|--------------------|------------------|
| Klein's Repeater | Nickel Presentation | 75 headshots |
| Deathknell | Blued Steel | 200 kills |
| Mystical Shotgun | Gravedigger | 150 kills within 4 blocks |
| Steam-Pressured Rifle | Expedition | 50 kills from 40 blocks or more |
| Le Mat Carbine | Gilded Ivory | 250 kills |
| Winter is Coming | Copper Rose | 100 headshots |
| Cannon Gun | Gunmetal | 300 kills |
| Inevitable Gun | Desert Bone | 40 Beyonder kills |
| Steam Chatterbox | Soot & Verdigris | 20 bursts that kill 3 or more |

The challenge counts kills with that gun. **Premium**, **elite** and **mythic** skins come from the server store. They
change how the gun looks in your hands, its tracers, muzzle flash and kill effects.

**Mythic skins ascend.** A gun wearing a mythic skin counts a kill streak (players, hostile mobs, Beyonder creatures):
at **3 kills** it reaches stage II, at **7** stage III, with new effects and an aura others can see. Dying resets the
streak of every mythic gun you carry.

The Cannon Gun and the Inevitable Gun share their skins.

### Practice dummy

**Recipe:** a Hay Bale on top, a Stick, an Armor Stand and a Stick in the middle, a Target block at the bottom.

Place it to get a mannequin that takes real hits (armor, headshots, distance and grade all count) but never dies. Its
name shows your last hit and the line under it your session: hits, headshots, damage per second. Right-click it to
change its armor. Hits on a dummy don't count for mastery, bounties or titles.

### Display stand

**Recipe:** a Gold Ingot on top of 3 Smooth Stone Slabs.

Right-click it with a gun to show the gun off, floating above the stand with its name, maker and kills. Others can
right-click it to read the gun's card; you take it back by right-clicking with an empty hand.

### Gun card

`/mwinspect` opens the card of the gun in your hand (so does **Inspect** on the [Field Tablet](/firearms/field-tablet/)): page one its history (engraving, who forged it, kills, your
mastery), page two how it's built (skin and ascension, Beyonder grade, attachments, condition).

<Aside type="tip">
Clicking the stand, the board or the dummy with a gun in your hand uses them instead of firing.
</Aside>
