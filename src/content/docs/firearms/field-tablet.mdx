### What it is

A gunsmith's slate of copper and glass. Raise it and it shows everything you carry for your guns, how far along your
mastery, titles and bounties are, and a compass of what is around you. No mod needed.

### Crafting it

**Recipe:** a Glass Pane in the top middle, Redstone in the centre, Copper Ingots in the other seven slots.

### Using it

**Right-click** the tablet to raise it. It stays up in front of you, and your **crosshair is the cursor**: look at a
button and **left-click** it. **Right-click** or **sneak** to put it away.

While it's up you can turn your head but not walk or jump. It goes down by itself when you take damage, look well
away from it or switch items.

### Tabs

| Tab | What it shows |
|-----|---------------|
| **Gear** | The guns, pouches and rounds you carry. Pick a gun to see it with its three attachment sockets, its mastery and Kill Tally; **Inspect** opens its card. A little figure of you holding the gun turns in the window |
| **Ammo** | Every kind of round you carry, loose and in pouches together. Click one to **load it next** in your gun, or move some **into or out of** your pouches |
| **Mastery** | Your mastery with each gun and the titles you are closest to earning |
| **Skins** | The skins of the picked gun: point at one to preview it, click an unlocked one to put it on |
| **Contracts** | Your daily and weekly bounties and the weekly challenge: progress, your place on the leaderboard, time left |
| **Field** | A brass compass of what is within **48 blocks**, turned with you (straight ahead is up): other players, hostile mobs, where you last died and your respawn point |

Long lists turn over in pages. Point at anything to read about it on the card beside the list.

<Aside>
Sneaking, invisible and hidden players don't show on the compass. Where you died and your respawn point sit on its
rim when they're farther than its reach.
</Aside>

### Gauges

Under the list, four gauges for the gun you hold:

- **Rounds** - rounds you carry for it.
- **Pouch** - how full your Ammo Pouches are.
- **Condition** - how fouled the gun is, and from where shots stray and it may jam (see
  [Upgrades & Upkeep](/firearms/upgrades/)).
- **Noise** - how far its shots are heard, and the shots a Suppressor has left.

<Aside type="tip">
**Ammo → Load** is the easiest way to choose which special rounds your gun takes next, without the HUD mod's ammo
wheel.
</Aside>
