### Overview

Klein Moretti's revolver: light, quick to fire and cheap to keep fed. It has the lowest damage of the guns, but
it shoots fast and its swing-out cylinder refills all six chambers at once with a speedloader. A good first gun and a
reliable sidearm.

### Statistics

| | |
|---|---|
| **Tier** | I |
| **Damage** | 7 per shot |
| **Magazine** | 6 rounds |
| **Range** | 40 blocks |
| **Shot every** | 0.55 s |
| **Reload** | All six at once (speedloader), 2.8 s |
| **Rounds** | Klein Rounds |
| **Heard from** | 28 blocks |

### How it plays

**Strengths:** the fastest shooting revolver, the cheapest rounds (8 cases from one Brass), the quietest gun, and a
full reload in one go.

**Weaknesses:** low damage per shot, short range, and the reload can't be split: all six or nothing.

### Getting it

Draft its blueprint for **12 Notes I** (or find it in village chests, temples and outposts), then forge its four parts
at the Weaponsmith Table: **Repeater Barrel, Repeater Cylinder, Repeater Frame, Repeater Grip**. See
[Getting a Gun](/firearms/crafting/).

### Attachments

**Lever Linkage** (fires faster), **Tube Magazine** (up to +3 rounds), **Rune-Etched Barrel** (more damage and range).
Details on [Upgrades & Upkeep](/firearms/upgrades/).

### Skins

Nickel Presentation (75 headshots), Jade Serpent, Tempest Crown, and the mythic **Above the Gray Fog**.

<Aside type="tip">
Headshots make up for the low damage: at ×1.5 a headshot comes close to a Deathknell body shot.
</Aside>
