**Klein's Repeater** is the starter gun, the only one whose parts you can craft. It fires fastest of the three guns and reloads its whole magazine at once.

Its [stats](/firearms/#weapon-stats) are 7 damage, 6 rounds, 40 blocks range, 0.55 seconds between shots and a 2.8-second reload.

## How do I make it?

Craft the four parts at a normal crafting table, then build the gun at a [Weaponsmith Table](/firearms/crafting/#the-weaponsmith-table) (right-click to check, Shift + right-click to build).

| Part | Recipe |
|---|---|
| Repeater Barrel | 3 Iron Ingots + 1 Lapis Lazuli |
| Repeater Cylinder | 2 Iron Ingots + 1 Redstone Dust |
| Repeater Frame | 2 Iron Ingots + 1 Lapis Lazuli |
| Repeater Grip | 2 Iron Ingots + 1 Dark Oak Log |

One gun takes 9 Iron Ingots, 2 Lapis Lazuli, 1 Redstone Dust and 1 Dark Oak Log in total.

## What ammo does it use?

Only **Klein Rounds** and the special Klein Rounds made from them (13 kinds in all).

- 1 Klein Cartridge Case + 1 Klein Cartridge Core + 1 Gunpowder = **4 Klein Rounds**.
- 1 Brass + 4 Iron Nuggets + 8 Gunpowder makes **32 rounds**, the cheapest ammo of any gun.

<Figure src={kleinRounds} alt="Crafting grid: a Klein Cartridge Case, a Klein Cartridge Core and Gunpowder making 4 Klein Rounds" caption="Klein Rounds." contain />

The [Ammunition](/firearms/ammunition/) page has the case and core recipes and all special rounds.

## How does reloading work?

Left-click to reload. After **2.8 seconds** the whole magazine fills at once, up to 6 rounds. You cannot fire during those 2.8 seconds.

Switching hotbar slots cancels the reload, and **nothing is loaded**. The rounds already in the gun stay there. Because the reload is all or nothing, reload when you have a safe moment rather than waiting for "No ammo!".
