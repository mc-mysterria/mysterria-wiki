### Overview

A heavy revolver whose shot rings like a funeral bell. Almost twice the damage of Klein's Repeater for a little
slower fire, and a loading gate that takes one round at a time, so you can top it up between fights.

### Statistics

| | |
|---|---|
| **Tier** | I |
| **Damage** | 12 per shot |
| **Magazine** | 6 rounds |
| **Range** | 60 blocks |
| **Shot every** | 0.6 s |
| **Reload** | One round at a time, 0.7 s each |
| **Rounds** | Deathknell Rounds |
| **Heard from** | 32 blocks |

### How it plays

**Strengths:** strong damage for a tier I gun, good range, and a reload you can stop to fire as soon as one round is
in.

**Weaknesses:** a full reload from empty takes over 4 seconds, and its rounds cost more gunpowder.

### Getting it

Draft its blueprint for **12 Notes I**, then forge its five parts: **Deathknell Barrel, Deathknell Cylinder, Deathknell
Frame, Deathknell Grip, Deathknell Lockwork**. See [Getting a Gun](/firearms/crafting/).

### Attachments

**Tolling Hammer** (more damage, slightly slower), **Fluted Cylinder** (faster reload), **Funeral Sights** (tighter
spread, more range). Details on [Upgrades & Upkeep](/firearms/upgrades/).

### Skins

Blued Steel (200 kills), Will-o'-Wisp, Last Rites, and the mythic **Requiem of the Pale Gate**.

<Aside type="tip">
Reload between targets, not when empty: one round goes in every 0.7 seconds and you can fire again at once.
</Aside>
