The **Deathknell** is a heavy revolver. It hits for 12 damage, almost twice Klein's Repeater, and reaches 60 blocks. It reloads one round at a time, so you can stop a reload early and keep what is already loaded.

Its numbers are in the [stats table](/firearms/#weapon-stats): 12 damage, 6 rounds, 60 blocks range, 0.6 seconds between shots and 0.7 seconds per round to reload.

## How do I get one?

You need four parts, and **none of them can be crafted**:

- Deathknell Barrel
- Deathknell Cylinder
- Deathknell Frame
- Deathknell Grip

Parts come from chest loot in the Overworld. Each time a chest in a vanilla structure generates its loot, there is a 25% chance it also holds one random Deathknell or Steam Rifle part. You can also trade for parts with other players.

With all four in your inventory, right-click a [Weaponsmith Table](/firearms/crafting/#how-do-i-build-a-gun) to check, then Shift + right-click to build.

## What ammo does it use?

Only **Deathknell Rounds** and the special Deathknell Rounds made from them (13 kinds in all).

- 1 Deathknell Cartridge Case + 1 Deathknell Cartridge Core + 2 Gunpowder = **3 Deathknell Rounds**.
- 1 Brass + 7 Iron Nuggets + 12 Gunpowder makes **18 rounds**.

The [Ammunition](/firearms/ammunition/) page has the case and core recipes and all special rounds.

## How does reloading work?

Left-click to reload. One round goes in every **0.7 seconds**:

| Rounds loaded | Time |
|---|---|
| 1 | 0.7 s |
| 2 | 1.4 s |
| 3 | 2.1 s |
| 4 | 2.8 s |
| 5 | 3.5 s |
| 6 | 4.2 s |

<Notice tone="warn" title="You cannot shoot mid-reload">
Firing is blocked while the reload runs ("Weapon is reloading!"). To shoot early, switch to another hotbar slot. That cancels the reload, but every round already loaded stays in the gun. Switch back and fire.
</Notice>

When you reload from empty, the gun takes the first stack of Deathknell Rounds in your inventory. When it is partly loaded, it only tops up with the same kind of round already inside.
