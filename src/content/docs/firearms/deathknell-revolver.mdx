The **Deathknell** is a heavy revolver that hits almost twice as hard as Klein's Repeater. It reloads one round at a time, so you can stop a reload early and keep what is already loaded.

Its [stats](/firearms/#weapon-stats) are 12 damage, 6 rounds, 60 blocks range, 0.6 seconds between shots and 0.7 seconds per round to reload.

## How do I get one?

You need four parts, and **none of them can be crafted**:

- Deathknell Barrel
- Deathknell Cylinder
- Deathknell Frame
- Deathknell Grip

Each time a chest in a vanilla Overworld structure generates its loot, there is a 25% chance it also holds one random Deathknell or Steam Rifle part. You can also trade for parts with other players.

With all four in your inventory, right-click a [Weaponsmith Table](/firearms/crafting/#how-do-i-build-a-gun) to check, then Shift + right-click to build.

## What ammo does it use?

Only **Deathknell Rounds** and the special Deathknell Rounds made from them (13 kinds in all).

- 1 Deathknell Cartridge Case + 1 Deathknell Cartridge Core + 2 Gunpowder = **3 Deathknell Rounds**.
- 1 Brass + 7 Iron Nuggets + 12 Gunpowder makes **18 rounds**.

## How does reloading work?

Left-click to reload. One round goes in every **0.7 seconds**:

| Rounds loaded | Time |
|---|---|
| 1 | 0.7 s |
| 2 | 1.4 s |
| 3 | 2.1 s |
| 4 | 2.8 s |
| 5 | 3.5 s |
| 6 | 4.2 s |

Firing is blocked while the reload runs ("Weapon is reloading!"). To shoot early, switch to another hotbar slot and back. That cancels the reload, but every round already loaded stays in the gun.

When you reload from empty, the gun takes the first stack of Deathknell Rounds in your inventory. When it is partly loaded, it only tops up with the same kind of round already inside.
