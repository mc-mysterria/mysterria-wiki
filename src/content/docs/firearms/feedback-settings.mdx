You don't need the [HUD mod](/firearms/hud-mod/) to see how your shots land. Without it, the server draws the
feedback itself, and every part of it can be turned off for yourself with `/mwsettings`.

### What you see when you shoot

- **Damage numbers** - each hit floats up from the target. Quick hits on the same target (the Steam Chatterbox, Klein's
  Repeater, shotgun pellets) add up into one number.
- **Hit markers** - a mark on your crosshair for a hit, a headshot and a kill.
- **Reload ring** - a ring around the crosshair that fills while the gun reloads.
- **Ammo dial** - a brass plate at the top of the screen while you hold a gun: its name, its rounds, and a track that
  fills with the magazine. The action bar still shows your ammo too.
- **Bullet tracers** - a lit beam from the muzzle to where the bullet lands, seen by the players around you.
- **Bullet holes and blood** - holes on the blocks you hit (8 seconds) and blood marks on the mobs you hit (3 seconds).
- **Mythic ascension** - when a mythic skin reaches its next stage, a flash at the edges of your screen and cracks
  closing in, in the skin's colour (see [Bounties, Mastery & Rewards](/firearms/progression/)).

Numbers, markers, the ring and the dial are shown only to you.

### Aiming and inspecting

**Sneak** to aim down the sights of any gun (the Steam-Pressured Rifle looks through its scope instead).

**Inspect** your gun to turn it over in your hands. The first time you hold a gun, the action bar tells you its key:

| Gun | Inspect |
|-----|---------|
| Most guns | Swap hands (F) |
| Le Mat Carbine, Winter is Coming (F switches mode) | Sneak + F |
| Inevitable Gun (sneak + F is the Plague Cloud) | Sneak + F twice |

### Your settings

`/mwsettings` opens a list of switches, each only for you:

| Switch | Off means |
|--------|-----------|
| Damage numbers | no numbers |
| Hit and kill markers | no crosshair markers |
| Reload ring | no ring |
| Ammo dial at the top of the screen | the action bar only |
| Lit bullet tracers | the old dust trail instead of the beam |
| Bullet holes and blood | no decals |
| Mythic ascension flash and cracks | no screen effect |
| Aim down the sights while sneaking | sneaking doesn't raise the sights |

Everything starts **on**. Tick what you want and press **Save**.

<Aside>
With the HUD mod, the mod draws all of this itself: `/mwsettings` points you to its own settings, `/mwhud`.
</Aside>

### Confirmations

Things you can't undo ask first, in a Yes / Cancel window:

- **Salvaging** a blueprint or spare parts on the Draft page, with a slider for how many.
- **Swapping a bounty** for Notes.
- **Letting the Vortex corrupt** your Cannon Gun.

`/mwengrave` with no name opens a text field with the gun's engraving: **Engrave**, **Remove it** or **Cancel**.
`/mwinspect` and display stands show the gun's card in a window too, with the gun on top.

### What others see

- **Holsters** - the guns in your hotbar that you aren't holding are worn on your body: revolvers on the hips (right
  first, then left), long guns across your back. Others see them with their skins and attachments; you don't see your
  own.
- **Recoil and reloads** - players within 48 blocks see your gun kick when you fire and move when you reload.

| On the hips | Across the back |
|-------------|-----------------|
| Klein's Repeater, Deathknell, Winter is Coming, Cannon Gun, Inevitable Gun | Steam-Pressured Rifle, Le Mat Carbine, Mystical Shotgun, Steam Chatterbox |
