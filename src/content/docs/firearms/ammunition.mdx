Each gun fires its own rounds: **Klein Rounds** for Klein's Repeater, **Deathknell Rounds** for the Deathknell and **Rifle Rounds** for the Steam Rifle. You craft standard rounds from a case, a core and gunpowder, then can turn them into one of 12 kinds of special round.

For how much damage each round does in each gun, see [Damage by ammo type](/firearms/#damage-by-ammo-type).

## Step 1: Brass

3 Copper Ingots + 1 Gold Ingot = **4 Brass**. See [Gun Crafting](/firearms/crafting/#brass).

### What does a full batch cost?

One batch uses up a whole craft of cases and cores:

| Rounds | Raw materials | Makes |
|---|---|---|
| Klein Rounds | 1 Brass + 4 Iron Nuggets + 8 Gunpowder | 32 rounds |
| Deathknell Rounds | 1 Brass + 7 Iron Nuggets + 12 Gunpowder | 18 rounds |
| Rifle Rounds | 3 Brass + 1 Iron Ingot + 12 Gunpowder | 18 rounds |

## Step 5: Special rounds

Put one effect material in the centre of a crafting table and surround it with **8 standard rounds of the same type**. You get **8 special rounds** of that type. For example, 8 Klein Rounds around Blaze Powder make 8 **Fire Klein Rounds**, which only fit Klein's Repeater.

<Figure src={effectAmmo} alt="Crafting grid: 8 Klein Rounds around Blaze Powder making 8 Fire Klein Rounds" caption="Fire Klein Rounds: 8 Klein Rounds around Blaze Powder." contain />

<Notice tone="warn" title="Do not mix round types">
All 8 rounds around the material must be the same kind. Mixing Klein, Deathknell and Rifle Rounds does not craft anything.
</Notice>

Special rounds are named "*Effect* *Gun* Rounds", for example "Fire Deathknell Rounds" or "Demon Hunter Rifle Rounds". With the standard rounds, each gun has 13 kinds of ammo.

| Effect | Centre material | Damage | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fire | Blaze Powder | 85% | Sets the target on fire for 5 seconds |
| Poison | Spider Eye | 85% | Poison I for 8 seconds |
| Armor Piercing | Iron Ingot | 90% | Ignores armor |
| Aging | Clock | 85% | Slowness I and Wither II for 10 seconds |
| Spirit Control | Soul Sand | 85% | Slowness IV and Blindness for 5 seconds |
| Deceiving | Eye of Ender | 85% | Turns the target around 180 degrees |
| Deprivation | Milk Bucket | 85% | Removes the target's positive potion effects |
| Exorcist | Glowstone Dust | 100%, or 250% against undead mobs | No status effect |
| Implosive | Diamond | 125% | No status effect, just more damage |
| Rotten | Rotten Flesh | 85% | Hunger III and Weakness I for 8 seconds |
| Weakening | Fermented Spider Eye | 85% | Weakness II and Mining Fatigue II for 10 seconds |
| Demon Hunter | Nether Star | 100%, or 250% against Beyonder Creatures | No status effect |

### Who do Exorcist and Demon Hunter rounds work on?

- **Exorcist:** zombies, zombie villagers, husks, drowned, skeletons, strays, wither skeletons, the Wither, zoglins and zombified piglins. Other mobs and players take normal damage.
- **Demon Hunter:** the [Beyonder Creatures](/magic/creatures/) that the server spawns. It gives **no bonus against Beyonder players**.

## Loading the right rounds

- When the magazine is **empty**, a reload takes the first stack of matching rounds in your inventory, standard or special.
- When the gun is **partly loaded**, you can only top up with the same kind of round already inside.
- To switch from standard to special rounds (or back), fire the magazine empty first, and keep the rounds you want first in your inventory.
