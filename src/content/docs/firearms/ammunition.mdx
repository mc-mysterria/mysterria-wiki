### Rounds

Every gun fires its own rounds. They come in three kinds:

- **Plain rounds** - crafted from cases, cores and gunpowder.
- **Special rounds** - plain rounds turned into armor piercing, exorcist, aging and other rounds by a catalyst.
- **Beyonder rounds** - rounds bound to a Beyonder ingredient: they take its sequence and pathway.

### Special rounds

A catalyst turns plain rounds into special rounds:

- **Crafting grid:** 8 plain rounds around 1 catalyst make **8** special rounds.
- **Weaponsmith Table** (Ammunition page): lay the rounds and the catalyst in the two slots. One catalyst turns up to
  **12** rounds.

Every gun's rounds have all eight special kinds.

| Special round | Catalyst | Damage | Effect |
|---------------|----------|--------|--------|
| **Armor Piercing** | Iron Ingot | 90% | Ignores 10 armor; skips half of a Beyonder's absorption shield |
| **Exorcist** | Glowstone Dust | 100% | ×2.5 damage to undead monsters |
| **Aging** | Wither Rose | 80% | Wither III for 5 s, Weakness for 10 s; removes Regeneration and Absorption |
| **Deceiving** | Eye of Ender | 80% | Turns the target around |
| **Deprivation** | Phantom Membrane | 85% | Removes every positive effect |
| **Spirit Control** | Ghast Tear | 70% | Blindness 6 s, Slowness IV 4 s, Nausea 5 s |
| **Implosive** | Breeze Rod | 100% | Pulls every creature within 5 blocks towards the target |
| **Demon Hunter** | Volatile Essence | 100% | ×2 damage to Beyonder creatures |

<Aside>
A shotgun shell's effect lands with one of its pellets. Special rounds foul a gun faster than plain ones
(see [Upgrades & Upkeep](/firearms/upgrades/)).
</Aside>

### Beyonder rounds

On the Ammunition page, lay rounds next to a **Beyonder ingredient** or a **Characteristic** to bind them:

- The rounds take the ingredient's **sequence as their grade** (a supplementary ingredient counts one sequence weaker)
  and its **pathway**.
- One main or supplementary ingredient binds **16 rounds**. A Characteristic makes **6 silver bullets** that hit
  **×1.5** harder.
- A shot fires at the stronger of the gun's grade and the round's, but a round reaches at most **2 sequences above** the
  one firing it. Mortals count as Sequence 9, so they reach grade 7.
- Rounds are **attuned** to their pathway: **+25%** damage to Beyonders of that pathway, **+10%** to its neighbouring
  pathways.
- Grades 3 to 0 can only be bound at a **Demigod table**.

Special rounds can be bound too: they keep their effect.

### Which rounds the gun loads

On a reload the gun loads the first matching rounds it finds in your inventory (hotbar first), then in your Ammo
Pouch. To pick a kind, keep those rounds first, or choose it on the [Field Tablet](/firearms/field-tablet/)'s **Ammo**
tab (**Load**). With the [HUD mod](/firearms/hud-mod/), hold the reload key to choose from a wheel of everything you
carry for that gun.

### Ammo Pouch

**Recipe:** 2 String and 6 Leather (String in the top corners, Leather in the top middle, both sides and the bottom
row).

One inventory slot that holds up to **256 rounds** of any kinds. Right-click it to open it: shift-click ammo in, click a
kind to take a stack out. Guns reload from the pouch too, after loose rounds.
