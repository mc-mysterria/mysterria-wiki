### Overview

A pump shotgun that fires a cone of 8 pellets. Devastating up close, weak beyond a few blocks.

### Statistics

| | |
|---|---|
| **Tier** | I |
| **Damage** | 8 pellets × 3, falling to 35% beyond 5 blocks |
| **Magazine** | 6 shells |
| **Range** | 18 blocks |
| **Shot every** | 1.0 s |
| **Reload** | One shell at a time, 0.55 s each |
| **Rounds** | Shotgun Shells |
| **Heard from** | 40 blocks |

### How it plays

**Strengths:** the most damage at close range when all pellets land, and it forgives a shaky aim.

**Weaknesses:** useless at range, no headshot bonus, loud, and it fouls faster than most guns.

### Getting it

Draft its blueprint for **12 Notes I**, then forge its four parts: **Shotgun Barrel, Shotgun Pump, Shotgun Receiver,
Shotgun Stock**. See [Getting a Gun](/firearms/crafting/).

### Attachments

**Full Choke** (tighter cone, more range), **Extended Tube** (up to +3 shells), **Rune-Sprung Pump** (fires faster).
Details on [Upgrades & Upkeep](/firearms/upgrades/).

### Skins

Gravedigger (150 kills within 4 blocks), Brimstone Preacher, Witch Hunter, and the mythic **Black Locust Swarm**.
