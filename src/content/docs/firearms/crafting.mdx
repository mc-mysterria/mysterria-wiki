Every gun is built from parts at a **Weaponsmith Table**. Klein's Repeater parts are crafted. Deathknell and Steam Rifle parts are found in chests. For the guns' stats, see the [stats table](/firearms/#weapon-stats).

## The Weaponsmith Table

**Recipe:** a Smithing Table in the centre, 4 Iron Blocks on the sides and 4 Redstone Blocks in the corners.

<Figure src={weaponsmithTable} alt="Crafting grid: Smithing Table in the centre, Iron Blocks on the sides, Redstone Blocks in the corners, making a Weaponsmith Table" caption="The Weaponsmith Table recipe." contain />

Place it anywhere. If you break it, it drops as a Weaponsmith Table again.

## How do I build a gun?

1. Put all the parts for one gun in your inventory (any slot).
2. **Right-click** the Weaponsmith Table. The chat shows the gun you can build and the parts it needs.
3. **Shift + right-click** the table to build it. The parts are used up and the gun goes into your inventory.

There is no menu. If you carry parts but not a full set, the chat lists the parts you have instead. If you carry no parts at all, nothing happens.

<Notice tone="warn" title="Carry one set at a time">
If you carry full sets for more than one gun, the table builds the first one in this order: Klein's Repeater, Deathknell, Steam Rifle. Put the other parts in a chest first.
</Notice>

## Brass

Brass goes into all cartridge cases and the Rifle Cartridge Core.

**Recipe:** 3 Copper Ingots + 1 Gold Ingot = **4 Brass** (shapeless).

<Figure src={brass} alt="Crafting grid: 3 Copper Ingots and 1 Gold Ingot making 4 Brass" caption="Brass." contain />

## Klein's Repeater parts

Klein's Repeater is the only gun you can craft from scratch. Each part is crafted at a normal crafting table.

| Part | Recipe |
|---|---|
| Repeater Barrel | 3 Iron Ingots + 1 Lapis Lazuli |
| Repeater Cylinder | 2 Iron Ingots + 1 Redstone Dust |
| Repeater Frame | 2 Iron Ingots + 1 Lapis Lazuli |
| Repeater Grip | 2 Iron Ingots + 1 Dark Oak Log |

**One full gun costs:** 9 Iron Ingots, 2 Lapis Lazuli, 1 Redstone Dust and 1 Dark Oak Log.

## Deathknell and Steam Rifle parts

These parts **cannot be crafted**. You find them in chests:

- Each time a chest in a vanilla structure in the Overworld generates its loot, there is a **25% chance** that one random part is added.
- The part is picked at random from all 9 Deathknell and Steam Rifle parts, so expect duplicates.
- Parts are normal items. You can trade them with other players or store them.

| Gun | Parts needed |
|---|---|
| [Deathknell](/firearms/deathknell-revolver/) (4 parts) | Deathknell Barrel, Deathknell Cylinder, Deathknell Frame, Deathknell Grip |
| [Steam Rifle](/firearms/steam-rifle/) (5 parts) | Steam Rifle Barrel, Steam Pipe Assembly, Mechanical Power Box, Military Rifle Stock, Sniper Scope |

Want more chests? The [Beyonder Loot](/magic/loot/) page explains how chest loot and refills work.
