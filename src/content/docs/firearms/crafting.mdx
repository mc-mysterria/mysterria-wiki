### The path to a gun

1. **Gunsmith's Notes** from chests, trial vaults, Beyonder creatures and bounties.
2. **A blueprint** - drafted from Notes at the table, or found whole now and then.
3. **The parts** - forged at the table from gunsmith materials, with the blueprint in your inventory.
4. **The gun** - assembled at the table from its parts. This uses up the blueprint.

Nothing on this path is random at its core: every qualifying chest gives Notes, so you always know how far you are
from your next gun.

**Recipe:** 1 Smithing Table in the middle, 4 Iron Blocks and 4 Redstone Blocks around it.

Right-click a placed table to open it. Its pages:

| Page | What it does |
|------|--------------|
| **Blueprints** | Forge a gun's parts (carry its blueprint) |
| **Draft** | Turn Gunsmith's Notes into a blueprint; salvage blueprints and spare parts |
| **Assembly** | Build the gun from its parts |
| **Workshop** | Fit attachments and a Kill Tally |
| **Upgrade** | Give the gun a Beyonder grade |
| **Ammunition** | Press rounds, make special rounds, bind Beyonder rounds |
| **The Vortex** | (Demigod table only) the ritual that makes the Inevitable Gun |

**Demigod Weaponsmith Table.** Right-click a table with a Beyonder potion of **Sequence 4 or stronger** to raise it to a
Demigod table (the potion is used up). Winter is Coming and the Cannon Gun can only be assembled there, and the
strongest Beyonder grades (3 to 0) can only be applied there.

### Gunsmith's Notes

Notes come in three tiers. Every qualifying chest or vault holds Notes of its tier the first time it is opened:

| Where | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| Village chests (houses too), ruined portals, igloos, underwater ruins, shipwrecks, pillager outposts, desert and jungle temples | **Notes I** |
| Strongholds, woodland mansions, mineshafts, buried treasure, dungeons | **Notes II** |
| Trial chamber vaults | **Notes II** |
| Ancient cities, end cities, bastions, Nether fortresses | **Notes III** |
| Ominous trial vaults | **Notes III** |
| Beyonder creatures (15% per kill) | **Notes II**, or **Notes III** from Sequence 4 and stronger |
| Bounties and the weekly challenge | see [Bounties, Mastery & Rewards](/firearms/progression/) |

On top of the Notes, a chest has a **2.5%** chance of a whole gun blueprint and **2.5%** of an attachment blueprint
(trial vaults 3% each, ominous vaults twice that). Beyonder creatures sometimes carry an attachment blueprint too.

`/mwblueprints` shows the Notes you carry and what they draft.

### Drafting a blueprint

On the **Draft** page, click a blueprint to draft it. It costs Notes of its gun's tier:

| | Tier I | Tier II | Tier III |
|---|--------|---------|----------|
| **Gun blueprint** | 12 Notes I | 20 Notes II | 30 Notes III |
| **Attachment blueprint** | 4 Notes I | 6 Notes II | 10 Notes III |

A **higher-tier Note pays for a lower tier**, and the lowest ones you can use are spent first.

| Tier | Guns |
|------|------|
| **I** | Klein's Repeater, Deathknell, Mystical Shotgun |
| **II** | Steam-Pressured Rifle, Le Mat Carbine, Steam Chatterbox |
| **III** | Winter is Coming, Cannon Gun (and the Inevitable Gun's attachments) |

**Salvage.** Lay a blueprint you don't need in the salvage tray: it gives back half its draft cost in Notes. Spare
weapon parts go there too and give back half of the materials they took to forge. A window asks you to confirm
first, with a slider for how many.

<Aside>
A blueprint is used up by what it makes: a gun blueprint when the gun is assembled, an attachment blueprint when
the attachment is fitted. Forging parts doesn't use it, so you can forge spares.
</Aside>

### Gunsmith materials

Craft these on an ordinary crafting table:

| Material | Recipe | Makes |
|----------|--------|-------|
| **Brass** | 3 Copper Ingots + 1 Gold Ingot | 4 |
| **Tempered Steel** | 4 Iron Ingots, 2 Coal Blocks, 1 Blaze Powder | 2 |
| **Precision Gear** | 4 Brass around 1 Tempered Steel | 2 |
| **Coiled Spring** | 2 String, 1 Tempered Steel | 2 |
| **Gunsmith Oil** | 2 Slime Balls, 1 Ink Sac, 1 Glass Bottle | 2 |
| **Aether Filament** | 6 Copper Ingots, 2 Glowstone Dust, 1 Phantom Membrane | 2 |
| **Resonant Core** | 1 Echo Shard, 2 Volatile Essence, 1 Diamond, 1 Aether Filament | 1 |
| **Cleaning Kit** | 1 White Wool, 1 Gunsmith Oil, 1 Stick | 2 |
| **Suppressor** | 2 Tempered Steel, 2 White Wool, 1 Coiled Spring | 1 |

**Volatile Essence** can't be crafted. It drops from fights:

| Mob | Chance |
|-----|--------|
| Ghast | 20% |
| Evoker | 25% |
| Wither Skeleton | 5% |
| Beyonder creatures | 12% |

Ordinary mobs give each player at most **12 Essence a day**, and mobs from spawners give none. Beyonder creatures,
bounties and the weekly challenge have no limit.

### Forging parts

On the **Blueprints** page, with the gun's blueprint in your inventory, forge its parts one by one. Each part costs:

| | Tier I | Tier II | Tier III |
|---|--------|---------|----------|
| Tempered Steel | 6 | 9 | 16 |
| Precision Gear | 6 | 9 | 16 |
| Coiled Spring | 3 | 5 | 8 |
| Gunsmith Oil | 2 | 3 | 6 |
| Brass | 9 | 12 | 24 |
| Aether Filament | - | 3 | 8 |
| Volatile Essence | - | 4 | 9 |
| Resonant Core | - | - | 1 |
| Breeze Rod | 1 | 3 | - |
| Echo Shard | - | - | 1 |
| Netherite Ingot | - | - | 2 |

| Gun | Parts |
|-----|-------|
| Klein's Repeater | 4 |
| Deathknell | 5 |
| Mystical Shotgun | 4 |
| Steam-Pressured Rifle | 5 |
| Le Mat Carbine | 5 |
| Steam Chatterbox | 4 |
| Winter is Coming | 3 |
| Cannon Gun | 3 |

### Assembling the gun

On the **Assembly** page, with all of a gun's parts and its blueprint in your inventory, build the gun. Winter is Coming
and the Cannon Gun also take a **Heavy Core**, and they can only be assembled at a **Demigod table**.

The Inevitable Gun is never assembled: it's what a Cannon Gun becomes in the Vortex ritual. See
[Inevitable Gun](/firearms/inevitable-gun/).

<Aside type="tip">
Every gun remembers who forged it and when. `/mwinspect` opens the card of the gun in your hand: its history and how
it's built.
</Aside>
