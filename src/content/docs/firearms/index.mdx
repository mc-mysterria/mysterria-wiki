### What are mystical firearms?

Mysterria has nine firearms from the world of Lord of the Mysteries, from Klein Moretti's revolver to the
Inevitable Gun. You build them yourself at a **Weaponsmith Table**, feed them ammunition you craft, and grow them
with attachments, Beyonder grades, skins and mastery.

A gun is a sidearm for everyone: a mortal can carry one, and a Beyonder uses it next to their pathway's abilities.

### Where to start

1. **Craft a Weaponsmith Table** - your workshop for everything gun-related. See [Getting a Gun](/firearms/crafting/).
2. **Collect Gunsmith's Notes** - village chests, temples, strongholds and trial vaults hold them. Notes become
   blueprints.
3. **Forge the parts and assemble the gun** at the table.
4. **Craft ammunition** - see [Ammunition](/firearms/ammunition/).

<Aside type="tip">
Start with a **tier I gun** (Klein's Repeater, Deathknell or the Mystical Shotgun): its blueprint costs the fewest Notes
and its parts only need farmable materials.
</Aside>

### Controls

| Action | Key |
|--------|-----|
| **Fire** | Right-click (Steam Chatterbox: hold it) |
| **Reload** | Left-click |
| **Switch mode** (LeMat barrel, decay modes) | Swap hands (F) |
| **Scope** (Steam-Pressured Rifle) / **aim down sights** (other guns) | Sneak |
| **Inspect the gun** | Swap hands (F); sneak + F on guns that switch modes |
| **Open the Ammo Pouch** | Right-click the pouch |
| **Raise the Field Tablet** | Right-click the tablet |

With the [HUD mod](/firearms/hud-mod/) you also get a reload key with an ammo wheel, an ability wheel, aim down sights
and a proper scope. Without it the server shows damage numbers, hit markers and an ammo dial itself; see
[Feedback & Settings](/firearms/feedback-settings/).

### How gun combat works

**Accuracy.** Standing still, every shot goes exactly where you aim. Moving adds up to **4°** of spread, and being in
the air up to **8°**. Attachments, mastery and a clean gun reduce it; a fouled gun adds to it.

**Headshots.** A bullet that lands on the head deals **×1.5** damage (shotgun pellets don't count).

**Ammunition.** Every gun has its own rounds, and every kind of round has special variants: armor piercing, exorcist,
aging and more. You choose what the gun loads.

**Noise.** Shots are heard by players 28-48 blocks away (depending on the gun) and wake up mobs nearby. A Suppressor
muffles most guns.

**Upkeep.** Every shot fouls the gun. A dirty gun spreads wider and can jam; a Cleaning Kit fixes it.

**Close to walls.** Step up to a wall and your character raises the gun so it doesn't poke through the wall.
