Mysterria has three guns inspired by Lord of the Mysteries: **Klein's Repeater**, the **Deathknell** and the **Steam Rifle**. You build them from parts at a **Weaponsmith Table** and load them with crafted rounds.

## How do I get my first gun?

1. Craft a [Weaponsmith Table](/firearms/crafting/#the-weaponsmith-table) and place it.
2. Craft the four parts of **Klein's Repeater** from iron, lapis, redstone and a dark oak log. It is the only gun whose parts you can craft.
3. Carry the parts, right-click the table, then **Shift + right-click** to build the gun.
4. Craft Brass, then cartridge cases, cores and [Klein Rounds](/firearms/ammunition/).

The **Deathknell** and **Steam Rifle** parts cannot be crafted. They turn up in chest loot in the Overworld: each time a chest in a vanilla structure generates its loot, there is a 25% chance it also holds one random part from either gun. Parts are normal items, so you can also trade for them.

## Weapon stats

This is the one stats table for all guns on the wiki. Damage is in health points per hit, before armor.

| Weapon | Damage | Magazine | Range | Between shots | Reload |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Klein's Repeater](/firearms/kleins-repeater/) | 7 | 6 | 40 | 0.55 s | 2.8 s (all at once) |
| [Deathknell](/firearms/deathknell-revolver/) | 12 | 6 | 60 | 0.6 s | 0.7 s per round |
| [Steam Rifle](/firearms/steam-rifle/) | 20 | 5 | 100 | 1.2 s | 3.0 s from empty |

Range is in blocks. Each gun fires only its own rounds (Klein Rounds, Deathknell Rounds, Rifle Rounds). A full Deathknell reload takes 4.2 s. The Steam Rifle reloads all 5 rounds at once from empty, but when topping up it loads the first round after 1.0 s and then one every 0.75 s. Klein's Repeater parts can be crafted; the other two guns need parts from chest loot.

### Damage by ammo type

Special rounds change the damage. See [Ammunition](/firearms/ammunition/) for what each effect does.

| Ammo | Multiplier | Klein's Repeater | Deathknell | Steam Rifle |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Standard rounds | 100% | 7 | 12 | 20 |
| Fire, Poison, Aging, Spirit Control, Deceiving, Deprivation, Rotten, Weakening | 85% | 5.95 | 10.2 | 17 |
| Armor Piercing | 90% | 6.3 | 10.8 | 18 |
| Implosive | 125% | 8.75 | 15 | 25 |
| Exorcist against undead mobs | 250% | 17.5 | 30 | 50 |
| Demon Hunter against Beyonder Creatures | 250% | 17.5 | 30 | 50 |

Exorcist and Demon Hunter rounds deal 100% against everything else.

## Controls

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| Fire | Right-click |
| Reload | Left-click |
| Cancel a reload | Switch to another hotbar slot |
| Scope in (Steam Rifle only) | Hold Sneak |

You **cannot fire while a reload is running**. The chat says "Weapon is reloading!". If you need to shoot, switch hotbar slots to cancel the reload, then switch back:

- **Klein's Repeater** loads nothing if you cancel.
- **Deathknell** keeps every round it has already loaded.
- **Steam Rifle** keeps the rounds already loaded when topping up. A reload from empty loads nothing if you cancel it before the 3 seconds are up.

Guns cannot be enchanted, used in an anvil or moved to your off hand.

## The action bar

While you hold a gun, the bar above your hotbar shows:

- the gun's name and its rounds, for example "Klein's Repeater 4/6"
- a yellow **Reloading...** while it reloads
- a red **No ammo!** when the magazine is empty

## Accuracy

| You are | Bullet spread |
|---|---|
| Standing still | 0 degrees (perfectly accurate) |
| Moving (any movement in the last 0.3 seconds) | 4 degrees |
| In the air | 8 degrees |

Stand still for important shots. Sneaking alone does not tighten the spread.

## What bullets go through

- Bullets pass through leaves, grass, flowers, crops, sugar cane, bamboo and glass. Each block they pass through takes 10% off the damage, down to a minimum of 1.
- Glass and glass panes that a bullet passes through break.
- Each shot leaves a coloured trail, and players within 10 blocks of a passing bullet hear it crack by.

## Which ammo does the gun load?

- **Empty magazine:** the reload takes the first stack of matching rounds in your inventory, standard or special.
- **Partly loaded:** you can only top up with the same kind of round that is already in the gun.
- **To switch ammo type**, fire the magazine empty, then reload with the new rounds first in your inventory.

<Notice tone="info" title="Each gun has its own rounds">
Klein Rounds only fit Klein's Repeater, Deathknell Rounds only fit the Deathknell, and Rifle Rounds only fit the Steam Rifle. Special rounds keep the gun type they were made from.
</Notice>
