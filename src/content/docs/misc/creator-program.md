We're eager to publish short-form content on TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Reels about the server, to tell the world about
us. For that we need quality videos, and after some considerations we have decided that you make the videos; we publish
them; and then you get rewarded. No channel needed. No audience needed. No application, no ticket, nothing. Just one
good edit is the entire entry requirement, anyone and anytime. You can try doing it whenever you like, if it's not
resonates with you - you are not forced to continue

---

## 🎬 How It Works

1. **Record or grab footage** - from your own gameplay, or from the shared footage pool.
2. **Edit a short** - vertical, 25–60 seconds, one idea.
3. **Submit it** in the `#submissions` forum on Discord.
4. **We review it** - accepted or rejected with a reason code, usually within 72 hours.
5. **You get paid on acceptance**, then again after 30 days based on how it performed.

Accepted videos are published by us with credit to you in the caption or pinned comment.

## 💰 Rewards

You choose how you are paid. Every payout is offered as either **cash** or **Store Credit** - Store Credit is worth
roughly double the cash value, because it costs us less to give. If you insist, we can also consider in-game currency
you may need (Brilliant Emporium points; Coppets, Licks, V'erl Dors; Energy Shards etc)

**Base - paid on acceptance**. Paid regardless of how the video performs. You do not control our posting schedule or the
algorithm, so you do not carry

**Payment terms**

- Store Credit payouts are capped at **$40 equivalent per person per month**. Above that, the remainder is paid in cash.

## 🪜 Tiers

Tiers control how many videos you can have in review at once and what your base rate is. Everyone starts at Applicant as
soon as they submit their very first submission.

| Tier            | Requirement                        | Submission slots / month | Base rate |
|-----------------|------------------------------------|--------------------------|-----------|
| **Applicant**   | -                                  | 2                        | ×1.0      |
| **Contributor** | 3 accepted videos                  | 5                        | ×1.25     |
| **Resident**    | 10 accepted + strong average views | 8, priority review       | ×1.5      |

**Resident** also unlocks everything the old Author rank carried: the in-game tag, beta server access, the Eternal Edict
Sequence 8 boon, and the right to post links in global chat.

We accept a maximum of **12 videos per month** across all creators. Slots are first-come - submit early. Three
consecutive rejections put you on a two-week cooldown. Don't consider this as a punishment; it is simply a signal to
re-read the checklist
below.

## ✅ What Gets Accepted

We've also gathered some info about what actually has high chances of being accepted. Most rejections will be not about
effort, but about structure. Short-form is a format with rules we have to strictly follow to have results.

### The checklist

- **Open on your most absurd frame.** No intro, no logo, no "hey guys"
- **Text hook on screen within half a second.** Six words or fewer
- **One idea per video.** One spell, one moment, one joke, one general idea
- **Payoff at ~70%**, then loop back toward the opening beat
- **30–59 seconds.** Longer only if the payoff truly needs it
- **English text only** as well

### What Mysterria has that nothing else does

Lead with the strange. The market is saturated with generic SMP content - our advantage is that the magic systems are
genuinely unusual. Madness effects, possession, Sealed Artifacts, church rituals, mutation, Crimson Moon, spell chains,
Beyonder transformations.

"I got possessed by a god in Minecraft" sounds cool. "New dungeon update" does not.

### Technical requirements

Technical spec is also important, submissions will be auto-rejected if missing these:

- 1080 × 1920 vertical format, MP4 / H.264
- No watermarks, including editor trial watermarks
- Music on a separate track, or send a clean no-music export
- Don't use copy righted music if the video is longer than 60 seconds

P.S. 1920 x 1080 videos are valid, but we prefer 1080 x 1920. However, if you submit one of these, they will also be
reviewed on special terms, especially if those are several minutes videos on YouTube with gameplay or lets-plays.

### Rejection reason codes

As a result of review, you'll get a tag, not a paragraph. Faster for us and omits issues with overexplaining things. If
your submission was rejected, we're sorry: this is the life.

| Code   | Meaning                                                     |
|--------|-------------------------------------------------------------|
| **R1** | Hook too slow - nothing grabs in the first second           |
| **R2** | No payoff - the video does not land anywhere                |
| **R3** | Pacing - dead frames, holds too long, over-edited           |
| **R4** | Too many ideas - split it into separate videos              |
| **R5** | Technical spec failure - see requirements above             |
| **R6** | Overlaps something we have already published                |
| **R7** | Footage is not visually interesting enough to carry an edit |

## 📹 Footage Suppliers

Not an editor? You can still contribute.

Drop raw, unedited clips into `#footage`: rituals, boss fights, spell effects, madness, anything visually loud.
Editors pull from this pool, and you are paid in **Brilliant Emporium points** for clips that get used in an accepted
video.

No editing skill required. No slots, no tiers, no limit.

---

## ⚖️ Rules

1. **By submitting, you grant Mysterria a perpetual, worldwide right to publish, edit, re-cut, cross-post and monetize
   the video on any platform.** You keep the right to post your own copy anywhere you like.
2. **The work must be yours.** No re-uploads, no stolen edits, no AI-generated footage passed off as gameplay. Confirmed
   theft is a permanent ban from the program.
3. **Good standing.** No active bans or mutes on the server or Discord.
4. **Family friendly-ish.** Mild language is fine. Slurs, excessive toxicity and controversial topics are not.
5. **No exploiting glitches for content**, and no harassing players on camera.
6. Rejections are final. Resubmitting the same edit without changes burns a slot.

## 🤝 Partner Program

Separate from the above. If you already run a channel with an established audience and want to make Mysterria content
**on your own platform**, that is sponsorship, not a submission.

Open a ticket in `#create-ticket` titled **"Partner Application"** with your channel link, average views, and what you
would want to make. Your content has to be linked to Minecraft or Lord of The Mysteries theme.
