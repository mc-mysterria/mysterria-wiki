Every Beyonder has a hidden **Beyonder HP** pool that grows with each Sequence, while your hearts stay the same. Spells and Beyonder creatures hit that pool, and your hearts show what share of it is left.

## How much HP does each Sequence have?

| Sequence | 9 | 8 | 7 | 6 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Max HP** | 50 | 75 | 125 | 175 | 225 | 450 | 675 | 1,025 | 1,375 | 2,500 |

Your max HP comes from your **strongest** Sequence. Some abilities change it for a while.

## How does it work with my hearts?

Your Beyonder HP and your hearts always show the **same percentage**. At 60% of your Beyonder HP, you are at 60% of your hearts.

A spell's damage is taken from your Beyonder HP, and your hearts drop by the same share, so a stronger Beyonder loses fewer hearts to the same spell. Take two players.

- Player A is a Sequence 4 Sun Beyonder with **450 HP**. Player B is a Sequence 8 Fool with **75 HP**.
- A hits B with a spell for 60 damage. B has 15 HP left, so **80% of their hearts are gone** in one spell.
- B hits A with a spell for 28 damage. A has 422 HP left and loses barely more than half a heart.

## Does armour protect me from spells?

No. **Beyonder spell damage is true damage**, so armour, enchantments, potion effects and shields do not reduce it. The only vanilla protection that works is **Absorption**, and it is capped at **40% of your max HP**.

## PvP damage limits

To stop one-click kills between players of similar strength, PvP spell damage has limits. **Per hit**, one spell can take at most this much:

| Attacker compared to you | Max per hit |
|---|---|
| Weaker (higher Sequence number) | 20% of your max HP |
| Same Sequence | 30% |
| One Sequence stronger | 50% |
| Two or more Sequences stronger | No limit |

Within any 2 seconds, burst damage past 35% of your max HP is cut to 30% of its value, and nothing goes past 60%. This only applies when the attacker is not two or more Sequences stronger.

Stacked damage boosts and reductions are also kept within a range, so a hit always deals between 15% and 250% of its normal damage.

## Healing during a fight

For 15 seconds after you last took magic damage, you are **in magic combat** and healing is weaker. Natural healing from food heals only **15%** of what it normally would. Instant Health and Regeneration heal **50%**, and each heal is capped at **5% of your max HP**.

## How can I see my real HP?

Your Health is listed on your character screen (`/coi menu character`). The [Client Mod](/guides/client-mod/) can also replace your hearts with a Beyonder HP display in the styles Hearts + Numbers, Bar, Ornate Bar or Segments.

A [Visionary](https://www.mysterria.net/en/pathways/visionary) Sequence 9 Spectator's **Body Language Analysis** shows another player's current and max HP.
