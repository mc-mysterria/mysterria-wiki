Besides Health, Spirituality and Madness, Beyonders carry three **conditions**, each from 0 to 100. **Tiredness** builds up on its own, but **Freeze** and **Mental Pressure** come from enemy abilities. All three show on your character screen (`/coi menu character`).

## Tiredness

Tiredness is plain fatigue from staying awake and using magic.

### How does Tiredness build up?

- You gain about **1 Tiredness every 5 minutes** you are online and awake.
- Some abilities add Tiredness to their targets.

### What does it do?

| Tiredness | Effect |
|---|---|
| 40 or more | Slowness I |
| 65 or more | Slowness II, Spirituality regenerates at 85% speed |
| 85 or more | Slowness III, Spirituality regenerates at 70% speed, Madness fades at 60% speed |

### How do I get rid of it?

- **Sleep in a bed.** While you are actually lying in bed, Tiredness drops by **1 every second**. If the night is skipped, you wake up after a few seconds, so you may need several sleeps to clear a lot of it.
- A church can give the **Tiredness Recovery** gift. See [Churches](/magic/churches/).
- Some abilities remove Tiredness from you or your allies.

### Who gets tired more slowly?

- Every Beyonder at **Sequence 2 or stronger** gains Tiredness at **half** the normal rate.
- **Darkness** Beyonders resist it with Nocturnality, as long as it is active and their Darkness Reserve is at least 25. They gain 30% less at Sequence 9, then 40, 50, 60, 70, 78, 85 and 90% less, up to full immunity at Sequence 1.
- Believers of a god's Pathway standing within 24 blocks of that god gain **15% less** Tiredness (and 10% less Madness), from the god's Divine Presence. See [Sequence 0](/magic/sequence-zero/).

## Freeze

Freeze stacks come only from abilities that freeze their target.

| Freeze | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 to 24 | Slowness I |
| 25 to 49 | Slowness II |
| 50 to 74 | Slowness III |
| 75 to 100 | Slowness IV, plus vanilla freezing (frosted screen and freeze damage), stronger the closer you get to 100 |

Freeze drops by **1 every second**, or 3 a second while you sleep.

## Mental Pressure

Mental Pressure comes only from abilities that attack the mind.

| Mental Pressure | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 to 29 | Nausea, Slowness I |
| 30 to 59 | Weakness, stronger Nausea, Slowness I, and your **movement controls are reversed** |
| 60 to 89 | Blindness, Hunger, Slowness II |
| 90 to 100 | Your **Madness rises** every 10 seconds, by 0.02% for each point above 89, so 0.22% every 10 seconds at 100 |

Mental Pressure drops by **1 every 2 seconds**, or 3 every 2 seconds while you sleep. A caster who keeps you at 90 or more is slowly pushing you towards a loss of control. See [Madness and Mutation](/magic/mutation/).
