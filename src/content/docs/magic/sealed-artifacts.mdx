A Sealed Artifact is an item that holds a Beyonder ability, usable by anyone, even a non-Beyonder or a Beyonder of another Pathway. In return every artifact carries **drawbacks** that hurt the person using it.

## How do I get a Sealed Artifact?

| Source | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Expired potion** | A Sequence potion that is not drunk within **48 hours** turns into a Sealed Artifact of the same Pathway and Sequence. The ability inside is random. If no artifact can be made for it, you get a Beyonder Characteristic instead, and failing that a Ruined Potion |
| **Paragon crafting** | Paragon Beyonders make them on purpose (see below) |
| **Wild Beyonders** | A naturally spawned [Wild Beyonder](/magic/wild-beyonders/) drops one 5% of the time at Sequence 6 and 10% at Sequence 5 |
| **Church vault** | A [church](/magic/churches/) can store artifacts in its Church Vault, and members can request to borrow one |
| **Other players** | Artifacts are ordinary items, so you can trade for them |

Potions held as a stake in a Usurpation do not expire.

### How does the Paragon Pathway craft artifacts?

Only the [Paragon Pathway](https://www.mysterria.net/en/pathways/paragon) can craft Sealed Artifacts:

| Sequence | Ability | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | Artifact Crafting | Combine a Beyonder Characteristic with ingredients to make an artifact. Better materials give a better result. A failed craft uses up the Characteristic and every ingredient |
| 3 | Dual Artifact Crafting | Put **two** abilities into one artifact, with double the base success chance. Right-click uses the first ability, left-click the second |
| 2 | Manufacturing Authority | Make a temporary artifact from any Pathway or Sequence that has no drawbacks and lasts 18 minutes |

## How do I use a Sealed Artifact?

Hold it and right-click to use its ability. Left-click uses the second ability on a dual artifact.

- **Cooldowns are shared.** Using an artifact puts every artifact with the same ability on cooldown, not just the one in your hand.
- **Beyonders pay Spirituality** to use one, as for their own abilities. Non-Beyonders pay nothing.
- **Toggled abilities cost [Madness](/magic/mutation/).** While an artifact's toggled ability is switched on, you gain 0.05 Madness every second.

### Which abilities can be sealed into an artifact?

Most active abilities. Passive abilities, Contracts and Deity Authority can never be sealed. The Door Pathway's **Recording** can copy a spell from an artifact you hold in your offhand, but with half its usual chance of success.

## What are the drawbacks?

Every artifact has a **grade** set by the Sequence of its ability. The stronger the ability, the worse the drawbacks:

| Grade | Ability Sequence | Drawbacks |
|---|---|---|
| Grade 3 | 9 to 8 | 1 light |
| Grade 2 | 7 to 6 | 1 medium |
| Grade 1 | 5 to 4 | 1 medium and 2 light |
| Grade 0 | 3 to 0 | 2 severe and 2 medium |

The first number on the **Artifact** line of its description is its grade, so **Artifact: 2-990025** is Grade 2. The description also lists its drawbacks. There are dozens of drawbacks, most themed on the artifact's Pathway.

<Figure src={artifactTooltip} alt="Tooltip of a sealed artifact with its ID, the Prohibition ability and the Law Restriction drawback" caption="An artifact's tooltip names its ability and lists its drawbacks." />

Drawbacks come in two kinds:

- **One-shot** drawbacks trigger **every time** you use the ability.
- **Permanent** drawbacks keep ticking for as long as an activated artifact is in your inventory. They stop when it leaves your inventory.

:::danger[Read the drawbacks first]
A Grade 0 artifact can be deadly to its own user. Check the drawback list in the item description before you use an artifact in a fight.
:::

### How can I reduce the drawbacks?

| Pathway | Sequence | Ability | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Twilight Giant](https://www.mysterria.net/en/pathways/giant) | 7 | Artifact Mastery | 25% chance to cancel each drawback |
| Paragon | 7 | Mastery Shield | A personal shield that reduces or blocks drawbacks, depending on their danger |
| Paragon | 3 | Arcane Scholar Complete Mastery | Immune to Grade 3, 2 and 1 drawbacks, partly resistant to Grade 0 |
| [Death](https://www.mysterria.net/en/pathways/death) | 4 | Artifact Sealing | Seals one artifact's drawbacks for up to 24 hours |
| [Tyrant](https://www.mysterria.net/en/pathways/tyrant) | 4 | Artifact Sealing | Suppresses drawbacks of all nearby artifacts for 30 seconds, or seals their abilities |

The [Sun Pathway's](https://www.mysterria.net/en/pathways/sun) Sequence 4 **Beyonder Characteristic Purification** can also dissolve an artifact you hold in your offhand back into its Beyonder Characteristic.
