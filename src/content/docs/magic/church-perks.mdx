This page has the formulas and tables behind church perks. For the basics, read [Churches: Getting Started](/magic/churches/) first. All values are the live server values as of November 2026.

## What makes perks stronger or weaker?

Church perks are worked out from several things at once. If one is weak, the final bonus can feel much smaller.

- **Anchor weight**: how much active, praying support the church has, compared with what the leader's Sequence needs.
- **Membership age**: new members start at 20% and reach full value after 14 days (21 days after switching churches).
- **Piousness and activity**: recent prayer and site participation.
- **Site network**: an active main core, healthy branches, Regional HQs.
- **Site leadership**: Head Deacons and Bishops covering each site.
- **Role**: leaders and co-leaders get the most; new followers the least.
- **Pathway**: the church's own Pathway helps most, neighbouring Pathways a bit less, others least.
- **Your Sequence**: stronger members get somewhat stronger effects.
- **War**: see [church wars](/magic/church-management/#church-wars).
- **Main core**: without one, every perk runs at 50%.

`/coi church perks` and `/coi church status` show where you stand.

## Anchor weight

Each active member adds **anchor weight** by Sequence:

| Member's Sequence | Anchor weight |
|---|---:|
| 0 | 12.0 |
| 1 | 9.0 |
| 2 | 7.0 |
| 3 | 5.0 |
| 4 | 3.0 |
| 5 | 0.75 |
| 6 | 0.60 |
| 7 | 0.45 |
| 8 | 0.35 |
| 9 | 0.25 |
| Non-Beyonder | 0.15 |

Then multiplied by Pathway:

| Member's Pathway | Multiplier |
|---|---:|
| Same as the church | x1.5 |
| A neighbouring Pathway | x1.25 |
| Any other | x1.0 |

For example, a Sequence 4 member gives 4.5 on the church's own Pathway, 3.75 from a neighbouring Pathway and 3.0 otherwise. A same-Pathway Sequence 9 member gives 0.375.

### Who counts?

- A member counts only if they logged in **and** prayed in the last **14 days**.
- The **leader never counts** as an anchor for their own church.
- **Co-leaders do not count** as member anchors either.

### Limits on anchor weight

- **High-Sequence taper:** weight from members at Sequence 4 or stronger starts to taper at **18** and can never pass **34**.
- **Group bonus:** every **10** active members at Sequence 5 or weaker add **+1.0**.
- **Site capacity:** each active site can hold a certain amount of weight, set by the leader's Sequence. Weight above your total capacity tapers off and stops near 1.35 times capacity.

| Leader's Sequence | Main site capacity | Each branch |
|---|---:|---:|
| 4 | 12 | 8 |
| 3 | 16 | 10 |
| 2 | 20 | 12 |
| 1 | 24 | 14 |
| 0 | 28 | 16 |

A site's capacity is lowered if it is not active enough or has a weak or missing Head Deacon, and raised 12% by each upgrade level. With no active main core, the whole church's capacity is 6.

## How many anchors does a church need?

Full support needs this much effective anchor weight, set by the **leader's** Sequence. Every member is measured against the leader's row, whatever their own Sequence.

| Leader's Sequence | Anchors for full support |
|---|---:|
| 4 | 15 |
| 3 | 25 |
| 2 | 28 |
| 1 | 38 |
| 0 | 30 |

These are weight targets, not member counts. A Sequence 4 leader can reach 15 with a few strong same-Pathway members or many weaker ones. Note that the same leader's main site holds only 12 before tapering, so they will want a branch or an upgrade.

<Notice tone="info" title="Sequence 0 reads this too">
To claim a throne, your church needs **20** effective anchors, and you must be its leader, co-leader or Angel. A seated god's church is measured against **30**. See [Sequence 0](/magic/sequence-zero/).
</Notice>

## Church tiers

| Tier | Share of the anchor target | Acting bonus x | Recovery bonus x | Blessing x |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | below 25% | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 |
| 2 | 25% | 1.03 | 1.15 | 1.12 |
| 3 | 45% | 1.06 | 1.32 | 1.28 |
| 4 | 70% | 1.09 | 1.52 | 1.45 |
| 5 | 95% | 1.12 | 1.75 | 1.65 |

For a Sequence 4 leader (target 15), tier 2 starts around 3.75 anchors, tier 3 around 6.75, tier 4 around 10.5 and tier 5 around 14.25.

### Church Progress

Holding [services](/magic/church-management/#church-services) builds **Church Progress**. Every **100** progress points count as 1 extra anchor toward tier and ranking, up to **12**. It helps a young church grow, but it cannot replace real, praying members.

## Religion ranking

Several churches can follow the same Pathway; the one with the highest religion score leads it. A church has one official Pathway: the leader's.

The score is built from effective anchor weight, Church Progress, tier (+8 per tier), active sites (+2 each) and current health. See it with `/coi church top` or `/coi church top <pathway>`.

The religion score also makes up **25%** of a seat holder's Defense Score in [Seats and Usurpation](/magic/usurpation/#step-2-nomination).

### Support for other Pathways

- Two or more active, praying members of the same Pathway form a **cluster** and get a small bonus.
- Active co-leaders, Regional HQ managers and Angels of another Pathway add capped **secondary support** for that Pathway (up to 15%).
- This never makes the church a second official religion.

Check any Pathway in your church with `/coi church pathway <pathway> progress`.

## Acting bonus

The church acting bonus adds to your acting gains. It reaches up to about **16%** and tapers from 14%. It is highest for the church's own Pathway and for leaders and Angels, and grows with tier, prayer and membership age. Check yours with `/coi church acting`.

## Madness recovery bonus

The recovery bonus helps recoverable Madness fall faster. It can reach **24** (tapering from 14, hard cap 30) and grows with tier. It never removes Permanent Madness.

## Madness floor relief

From Sequence 4, Beyonders have a godhood Madness floor that recovery cannot go below:

| Sequence | Godhood floor |
|---|---:|
| 0 | 90% |
| 1 | 75% |
| 2 | 60% |
| 3 | 40% |
| 4 | 25% |
| 5 to 9 | 0% |

A church can relieve part of that floor:

> Your minimum Madness = Permanent Madness + godhood floor x (1 - relief)

The total is capped at **95%**, so it never reaches 100%. Recovery, gifts, blessings and curing abilities all stop at this floor.

Relief is your church's anchor fill (effective anchors divided by the target) times your role's share, up to your role's cap. Regular members also need membership age, piousness and recent activity to reach their full share.

| Role | Share | Cap |
|---|---:|---:|
| Leader | 100% | 100% |
| Co-leader | 95% | 100% |
| Angel | 75% | 90% |
| Head Deacon | 65% | 80% |
| Bishop | 55% | 70% |
| Senior Follower | 45% | 60% |
| Follower | 38% | 55% |
| Junior Follower | 30% | 45% |
| New Follower | 20% | 35% |

<Notice tone="warn" title="A fresh god sits near 30%">
A new god's church usually has about the 20 anchors needed to ascend, but a god is measured against 30. At 20 of 30 they get two thirds of the relief, so their 90% floor only drops to about 30%. Growing the congregation to 30 clears the rest.
</Notice>

Red Priests without a church can get relief from their Chain of Command cohort instead. Cohort and church relief never stack. See [Red Priest cohorts](/magic/sequence-zero/#red-priest-cohorts-instead-of-a-church).

## Blessings

A blessing is a quick support action on a church member: it lowers recoverable Madness and Tiredness and gives a short support effect. Use `/coi church bless <player>` or the church menu. You cannot bless yourself, and the target should be near an active site.

| Who blesses | Madness relief | Tiredness relief | Support effect | Spirituality cost |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Site officers (Head Deacons, Bishops) | 4 | 6 | 6 s | 10 |
| Leader, co-leaders, active Angels | 6 | 10 | 10 s | 18 |

Each target can be blessed once every **10 minutes**. After **2** blessings in 30 minutes, more blessings on that target lose strength (down to 35%).

## Gifts

Gifts are stronger support from the leader, co-leaders and active Angels: `/coi church gift <player> <type>`. They cost the giver Spirituality and add a little Madness to the giver. You cannot gift yourself.

| Gift | Effect | Spirituality cost | Giver's Madness |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| `madness-relief` | Up to 10 recoverable Madness removed | 30 | +1.0 |
| `spirituality-restore` | Up to 35 Spirituality restored | 24 | +0.5 |
| `tiredness-recovery` | Up to 20 Tiredness removed | 28 | +0.75 |
| `sanctuary-renewal` | 20 s Regeneration II and 120 s Absorption I | 36 | +1.5 |

The same gift to the same target has a **5-minute** cooldown. A second gift to one target within an hour is weaker (down to 40%). A gift is refused if it would push the giver above 95% Madness.

## Why are my perks weak?

- Is the main core placed and active?
- Have members, the leader and site heads prayed in the last 14 days?
- Are branches active, or have they decayed?
- Does every site have a Head Deacon?
- Are there more Angels than Angel slots?
- Is the church in an accepted war, or under a war penalty?
- Is the member new and still ramping up?
- Is the member's Pathway the church's own, a neighbour, or unrelated?

Commands that help: `/coi church status`, `/coi church perks`, `/coi church acting`, `/coi church pathway progress`, `/coi church my-site`, `/coi church cooldowns`.
