## What is a throne?

There are **22 thrones**, one for each normal Pathway. Outer Pathways have none. The player on a throne is that Pathway's **god**. A god is Sequence 0, with the rank of Deity, a title above their head, a place in the public Pantheon and an ability nobody else has.

Nobody can take a throne from a seated god. There is no seat war for Sequence 0. A throne empties only when its god stops being Sequence 0 on that Pathway, most often because they stopped showing up (see [Keeping the throne](#keeping-the-throne)). That is why the contest for a throne, the **vigil**, happens *before* anyone sits on it.

See who sits each throne, and every other high seat, on the main site.

## Who can try?

You are turned away straight away if:

- The Pathway is an outer Pathway.
- You are not **Sequence 1** on it.
- It is not your **primary** Pathway (the one you are counted on).
- You are already part of a vigil.

## What do I need to claim a throne?

`/coi ascend` opens the Throne menu. `/coi ascend status [pathway]` prints the same checklist in chat. It has **11 items**: seven requirements and four checks.

### The seven requirements

| | Requirement | What it takes |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | **Uniqueness held and accommodated** | You hold your Pathway's [Uniqueness](/magic/uniqueness/) **and** have it at **95%** accommodation or more |
| R2 | **Your church carries the anchors** | Your [church](/magic/churches/) has **20** effective anchor weight, and you are its leader, co-leader or Angel. Members of any Pathway count, and your own Pathway counts most. A Red Priest can use a cohort instead (see [below](#red-priest-cohorts-instead-of-a-church)) |
| R3 | **Every ritual of the last step** | A Sequence 0 ritual set worth **30 points** (see [Rituals](#the-30-point-ritual-set)) |
| R3b | **The story ritual the staff certified** | A legendary story ritual approved by staff (see [below](#the-story-ritual)) |
| R4 | **Banked Acting for the stake** | **31,104 Overflow**, which is half a Sequence 1 bar (see [Seats and Usurpation](/magic/usurpation/#what-is-overflow)). A fallen god in their grace period pays half |
| R5 | **Long enough in the seat** | **14 calendar days** at Sequence 1 on this Pathway since you last advanced or arrived. A fallen god's grace period waives this |
| R6 | **Sequence 0 potion in hand** | Brewed from **three Sequence 1 Beyonder Characteristics of the same Pathway**, nothing else |

### The four checks

- **The throne stands empty.**
- **Settled into this Pathway**: not within 30 days of arriving on it sideways (a switch, transfer or takeover).
- **Not fighting for a seat**: you are not in a seat war.
- **No claim is barred**: no Pathway or personal cooldown is running.

<Notice tone="warn" title="You cannot drink the Sequence 0 potion">
Drinking it on its own is refused. Only the ascension rite uses it.
</Notice>

`/coi ascend board` shows everyone's progress on the public race board. `/coi ascend private` hides your name from the board, but you are still counted.

## The 30-point ritual set

R3 works like the rituals for every other Sequence (check yours with `/ritual status`), with the highest target in the game. Your set is **dealt** from the whole ritual pool, not chosen. From Sequence 4 up, the pool includes legendary entries worth 10 points each, such as Blaze and Cave challenge advancements ("Collect 64 nether stars", "Obtain all the items!") and these server deeds, which are recorded automatically:

| Deed | How you earn it | Needed |
|---|---|---:|
| A seat war fought to its verdict | Fight in a [seat war](/magic/usurpation/) to the end, win or lose | 1 |
| A Uniqueness defended | Keep your Uniqueness through a paid challenge | 1 |
| Devout church service | Attend [church services](/magic/church-management/#church-services) | 10 |
| Your honorific invoked | Someone chants your [Honorific Name](/magic/honorific-names/) in full | 1 |
| An Ordeal survived | Come through an Ordeal and keep your Beyonder | 1 |
| A pinnacle bounty | Complete a bounty while at Sequence 1 | 1 |
| Diligence kept | Earn Acting on 7 different days (every day has a Tide) | 7 |

Up to **10** of the 30 points can come from service credits instead:

| Service credit | Points |
|---|---:|
| A church service attended | 1 |
| A bounty objective | 1 |
| A staff-approved story ritual | 2 |
| A seat war fought | 3 |
| A Uniqueness defended | 3 |

You can ask staff to reroll a single entry in your set.

## The story ritual

The story ritual is a **separate, hard requirement** (R3b), on top of the 30 points. It is for things the server cannot measure:

> Stage a public, server-wide event that acts out your Pathway's role in the novel, and make the server live through it.

It is not a screenshot or a solo grind. It is an event announced in advance, attended by other players, with real consequences, that only a Beyonder of *your* Pathway could have run. The Fool might gather a Tarot Club above the grey fog. The Red Priest might declare and fight a real, agreed war between two churches. The Hanged Man might broker a treaty between two hostile groups and make it hold.

Talk to staff **before** you run it, and tell them what you plan, when and with whom. They will tell you whether it counts. Once staff certify it, the checklist item turns green. A claim declared without it is called off with everything refunded and no cooldown.

## Declaring and the vigil

When every item is green, you **declare**. Your stake is spent, your potion goes into escrow with its expiry frozen, and the whole server is told. A **72-hour** window opens.

### Who may contest?

Another Sequence 1 player on the same Pathway can pay the same stake to contest you, but only if they:

- Have been at Sequence 1 for 14 days, are settled into the Pathway, are not in a seat war and are not on a contest cooldown.
- Have the Overflow for the stake.
- Already meet at least **3 of these 4**: the anchors, the 30 ritual points, the story ritual, and a Sequence 0 potion.

With only 3 Archangel seats per Pathway, at most 2 rivals can exist. If nobody contests, you go straight to the rite.

### If someone contests

The highest bid at the window's close contests, and other bids are refunded. A **7-day** contest follows, scored in War Points from real Acting gains, as in a [seat war](/magic/usurpation/#step-3-the-war). These are calendar days. Both of you chose to be here, so absence simply scores zero. **Ties go to the claimant.**

| Verdict | Loser pays | Winner gets | Afterwards |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Claimant wins** | The rival burns half their stake | The claimant goes on to the rite | The rival cannot contest this Pathway's vigil for 14 days |
| **Rival wins** | The claimant burns half their stake | **The Uniqueness**, not the throne. The rival's own stake comes back in full | Nobody can declare on the Pathway for 7 days. The Uniqueness is protected for 14 days. The claimant has a 24-hour re-declare cooldown |

A winning rival does **not** become a god. They must walk the whole road themselves, including growing 95% accommodation from zero. A lost contest burns half the stake but always returns the potion.

### Withdrawing and other exits

| What happened | Stake | Potion | Cooldown |
|---|---|---|---|
| Withdrew within 15 minutes | Full refund | Returned | 24-hour personal re-declare cooldown |
| Withdrew after 15 minutes | Half burned | Returned | 24-hour personal re-declare cooldown |
| Outbid, throne filled, cancelled, error | Full refund | Returned | None |
| Missed the 48-hour window to be online after winning | Full refund | Returned | 24-hour personal re-declare cooldown |
| Never answered the summons in 7 days | Full refund | Returned | None |

## The summons, the herald and the rite

Winning the vigil **summons** you. You have **24 hours** to pick your moment before the rite starts by itself, and an unanswered summons is returned after **7 days**. Answer it with `/coi ascension begin`.

The server then gets **10 minutes** of warning: a boss bar, a chat card with the coordinates, and a one-minute call. Anyone can check the spot with `/coi ascension where`. You can still walk during these 10 minutes, but once the rite starts you are rooted. If two rites are summoned close together, the second waits its turn.

### During the rite

The rite lasts about **2 and a half minutes**, and each of the 22 Pathways has its own scene.

- **Rivals cannot interrupt it.** You are invulnerable and cannot be hard-controlled. It only ends early if you leave, staff stop it, or you enter a state no rite can survive.
- **Nothing is spent until the halfway point.** Before that, an aborted rite costs nothing and you can answer the summons again. The throne is taken at the halfway mark.
- **Witnesses are paid.** Stand within **24 blocks** and you get **3%** of your own Acting bar and 30 seconds of Regeneration.
- **The crowd chants.** Five times a call goes up in chat. Each answer earns another **1.5%** of your bar. Any language's wording is accepted.

When the throne is taken, the whole server feels it for about **3 minutes**. The Sun turns night into glare, doors slam for the Door, the sea rises for the Tyrant, and so on. None of it can kill you or take anything permanent, and nothing survives a restart. The spot is marked on the map for good.

## What does a god get?

A god keeps their Sequence 9 to 1 abilities and gains two more:

**Deity Authority** (active). Left-click to switch between Favor, Behest and Ledger. Right-click to use the selected one.

- **Favor**: give a believer of your Pathway Acting worth **5%** of *their* bar. You have **60%** of bar per week to hand out (banking up to 2 weeks), and each believer can receive one Favor a week. It costs 10% Spirituality, with a 60-second cooldown. Favor never creates Overflow, War Points or Uniqueness progress, and never helps anyone's own claim on a throne.
- **Behest**: ask a devoted member of your church to appear, to proclaim your name, or to make a pilgrimage. Answering pays them one Favor. They have **30 minutes** to answer. You can have **3** open at once and send **6** a day, and each member can be asked once every **72 hours**. It costs 10% Spirituality, with a 5-minute cooldown. Answer one with `/coi deity answer <id>` or refuse with `/coi deity refuse <id>`.
- **Ledger**: your public record.

Refusing or ignoring a Behest brings **Disfavor**: no Favor or Behest from that god for **3 days**, **-25%** Acting gain during that time, and **+5** Madness once. It touches nothing else.

**Divine Presence** (passive). Believers of your Pathway within **24 blocks** take **10% less** incoming Madness and **15% less** Tiredness gain.

Favor and Presence reach every believer of your Pathway, church member or not. Church membership only makes you reachable by a Behest. Each member has a devotion toggle. Switching it off is instant, but switching it back on takes 7 days.

Your [Honorific Name](/magic/honorific-names/#what-happens-when-you-chant-a-gods-name) shrinks to 3 lines, and anyone who chants it in full gets your Pathway's blessing, even while you are offline.

<Notice tone="info" title="A fresh god sits at about 30% Madness floor">
A god is measured against **30** church anchors for [Madness floor relief](/magic/church-perks/#madness-floor-relief), more than the 20 needed to ascend. A new god with 20 anchors only relieves their 90% floor down to about 30%. Growing the congregation lowers it further.
</Notice>

## Keeping the throne

> Be online and not AFK for 20 minutes on at least 4 days out of any 30, or the throne empties.

That is the whole upkeep. Days are counted on Kyiv time. Abilities, chants and anchors do not count toward it. A new reign cannot lapse before its first 30 days have passed.

**Going away?** `/coi god absence <days>` declares a leave of up to **21 days**, usable once every **60 days**. Those days are left out of the window.

**Warnings:** you are warned privately with **14 days** left, then publicly at **7, 3 and 1**.

**If it lapses anyway:**

- You drop to **Sequence 1** on that Pathway with **95%** of a full bar.
- The throne empties and anyone who meets the requirements can claim it.
- For **60 days** you can re-ascend for **half the stake**, with your 14 days of tenure already counted.

A god who stops being Sequence 0 on that Pathway in any other way, such as switching Pathway, also loses the throne.

## Red Priest cohorts instead of a church

A [Red Priest](https://www.mysterria.net/en/pathways/priest) commander's **Chain of Command** cohort is not a church, but it can do two church jobs for players who have no active church.

**Madness floor relief.** From Sequence 4, a cohort lowers the godhood [Madness floor](/magic/church-perks/#madness-floor-relief) of its commander, Generals and Soldiers:

| Cohort rank | Share of relief |
|---|---:|
| Commander | 85% |
| General | 45% |
| Soldier | 30% |

- Relief = (cohort anchor weight / anchors needed) x your rank's share, at most your rank's share.
- Anchors needed come from the church table, using the **commander's** Sequence (15 at Sequence 4 up to 38 at Sequence 1).
- Cohort anchor weight uses the same per-Sequence weights as churches, with no Pathway bonus and the same high-Sequence taper (from 18, at most 34). The commander does not count.
- A member counts only if active in the last **14 days** and not in an active church.
- Cohort and church relief **never stack**. If you have an active church, the church relief applies instead.

**The Sequence 0 anchors.** If you do not hold a leader, co-leader or Angel role in a church, the throne checklist reads your cohort's anchor weight for R2 instead.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/coi ascend` | Open the Throne menu, or answer a waiting summons |
| `/coi ascend status [pathway]` | The 11-item checklist in chat |
| `/coi ascend board` | The public race board |
| `/coi ascend declare` | Declare your claim |

| `/coi ascend withdraw` | Withdraw your stake |
| `/coi ascend private` | Hide your name from the board (you are still counted) |
| `/coi ascension begin` | Answer a summons |
| `/coi ascension where` | Coordinates of the rite being heralded |
| `/coi ascension notify [on\|off]` | Mute ceremony chat (never the bell or boss bar) |

| `/coi ascension gods` | The Pantheon list |
| `/coi deity gods` | The Pantheon window |

| `/coi menu native` | Switch between Client Mod screens and chest menus (saved per character) |

The [Client Mod](/guides/client-mod/) shows the Throne and Pantheon menus as native screens. Without it, every window opens as a chest menu.

When the epoch ends, all 22 thrones empty and everyone starts again.
