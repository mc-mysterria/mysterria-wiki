A church is a player-run religion built around a leader, a Pathway, praying members and physical church sites. This page covers the basics: founding, joining, praying, roles and commands. Two more pages go deeper:

## What does a church give its members?

- An **Acting bonus** on top of your normal Acting gains.
- A **Madness recovery bonus** that helps recoverable Madness fall faster.
- **Madness floor relief** for Sequence 4 and stronger (see [below](#madness-floor-relief)).
- **Blessings** and **gifts** from church officers, for Madness, Tiredness and Spirituality support.
- **Prayer effects** near church sites.
- A shared **Church Vault** for [Sealed Artifacts](/magic/sealed-artifacts/).
- **Danger alerts**: the leader, co-leaders, Angels and Head Deacons can be told when a member is in trouble.

None of these are flat. They grow with how active and healthy the church is: active members, recent prayer, site activity, Pathway match and more. [Church Perks and Anchors](/magic/church-perks/) explains the numbers.

For the main site's short overview, see [Advanced systems](https://www.mysterria.net/en/guide/advanced) in the player guide.

## How do I found a church?

You need:

| Requirement | Value |
|---|---|
| Beyonder | Yes |
| Sequence | **4 or stronger** |
| Current church | You must not be a member, leader or co-leader of another church |
| Cooldown | **14 days** after you last left, were removed from, or disbanded a church |

Every church starts with a **Church Core**:

1. Craft a Church Core (recipe below).
2. Place it where your main church site should be.
3. Type the church name in chat when asked.
4. Manage the church by clicking the core or with `/coi church menu`.

## How do I join a church?

- The church leader or a co-leader invites you with `/coi church add <player>`. Accept with `/coi church accept` within **5 minutes**.
- If you chant the [Honorific Name](/magic/honorific-names/) of a church member, they can also offer you an invitation.

There is no member limit. Anyone can join, including players of other Pathways and non-Beyonders, but members of the church's own Pathway count for the most. The 14-day cooldown after leaving a church also applies to joining a new one.

New members do not get full perks straight away. You start at **20%** and reach full value after **14 days** (21 days if you switched from another church).

### Leaving

Use `/coi church leave`. A leader cannot leave. They must hand the church to someone else with `/coi church ownership handoff <player> confirm`, or disband it with `/coi church disband` (you then have 45 seconds to type the exact church name in chat to confirm).

## How do I pray?

Prayer keeps church bonuses alive. Members who have not prayed in **14 days**, or have not logged in for 14 days, stop counting toward the church.

Ways to pray:

- `/coi church pray`
- the prayer button in the church menu
- chanting the complete [Honorific Name](/magic/honorific-names/) of any member of the church, line by line in chat (this counts as an outside prayer)

| Prayer rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Full-strength range | Within **100 blocks** of an active church site |
| Away from a site | Counts at **10%** strength |
| Prayer connection lasts | **30 minutes** |
| Prayer effects cooldown | **12 hours** |

The prayer message tells you exactly what you received. If your perks feel weak, the first fix is to get members praying near a church site.

## What do the church roles do?

| Role | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Leader** | Owns the church and sets its Pathway. Places cores, manages members, sites and Angels. Does not count as an anchor for their own church |
| **Co-leader** | Leader-like authority over most church systems. At most **2**. Can manage a Regional HQ. Does not count as a member anchor |
| **Angel** | Needs **Sequence 3** or stronger. Strong blessings, gifts and vault rights. Slots: **3**, plus 1 per active branch. If there are more Angels than slots, Angel effects pause |
| **Head Deacon** | Runs one church site and keeps it covered |
| **Bishop** | Supports a site. At most **2** per site |
| **Regional Manager** | Runs a Regional HQ |
| **Follower** | Prays and adds anchor weight. Becomes Junior Follower after 3 days, Follower after 7 and Senior Follower after 14 |

Site roles are explained in [Church Management](/magic/church-management/).

## Church chat

Talk to your whole church with `/coi church chat <message>`, or start a normal chat message with `!c`, `!cc` or `!church`, for example `!c meet at the cathedral`.

## Madness floor relief

From Sequence 4, every Beyonder has a godhood Madness floor, which is Madness that recovery cannot remove. A healthy church lowers that floor for its members, most of all for leaders and co-leaders. A fresh god, for example, sits at about 30% until their congregation grows.

The floors, the relief formula and the role caps are on [Church Perks and Anchors](/magic/church-perks/#madness-floor-relief). For how Madness works in general, see [Madness and Mutation](/magic/mutation/).

<Notice tone="info" title="Red Priest cohorts">
A Red Priest's Chain of Command cohort gives its own floor relief to members who have no active church. That is not a church role. See [Sequence 0](/magic/sequence-zero/#red-priest-cohorts-instead-of-a-church).
</Notice>

## Churches and gods

A church matters a great deal on the road to [Sequence 0](/magic/sequence-zero/). A claimant needs their church to carry **20** anchors, and they must be its leader, co-leader or Angel. Attending **10** church services is one of the legendary deeds.

Once a god sits a throne, church membership gives no stat bonus of its own. It only lets the god reach you with a **Behest**. Each member has a devotion toggle. Turning it off is instant, but turning it back on takes a week.

## Where do I find the church menu?

- `/coi church menu`, or click your Church Core
- the church slot (a Beacon) in your character menu, `/coi menu character`
- the Churches menu in the [Client Mod](/guides/client-mod/) (default key M)

## Commands for members

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/coi church menu` | Open the church menu |
| `/coi church accept` | Accept the latest invite |
| `/coi church leave` | Leave your church |
| `/coi church pray` | Pray |
| `/coi church chat <message>` | Church chat (or `!c <message>`) |
| `/coi church info` | Your church's details |
| `/coi church members` | The member list |
| `/coi church status` | Church health at a glance |
| `/coi church perks` | Your current perks and what limits them |
| `/coi church acting` | Your church Acting bonus |
| `/coi church pathway progress` | Support for your own Pathway |
| `/coi church pathway <pathway> progress` | Support for another Pathway in your church |
| `/coi church my-site` | Your assigned church site |
| `/coi church cooldowns` | Your church cooldowns |
| `/coi church top [pathway]` | The strongest churches, overall or for one Pathway |
| `/coi church vault` | Open the Church Vault |
| `/coi church artifacts list` | Artifacts stored by your church |
| `/coi church artifact request <artifactId>` | Ask to borrow an artifact |
| `/coi church artifact return` | Return a borrowed artifact |

Leader and manager commands are on [Church Management](/magic/church-management/#commands-for-leaders-and-managers).
