A **Beyonder** is a player who has drunk a Beyonder potion and gained its powers. It is the power system of Lord of the Mysteries (LOTM), and on Mysterria it is the heart of the game. Every Beyonder walks one of the [22 Pathways](/magic/pathways/), climbing from **Sequence 9** (the weakest) towards **Sequence 0** (a god).

## The Beyonder loop

Every Sequence follows the same loop.

1. **Find the recipe** for your next potion: a full recipe, or all of its Recipe Pages. Recipes come from loot chests, and [Loot Focus](/magic/loot/#loot-focus-pity) guarantees one in the end.
2. **Gather the ingredients.** Some are found in chests, some drop from Beyonder creatures, some are mined from glowing nodes.
3. **Brew the potion** at a Magic Cauldron and pass the short brewing minigame.
4. **Drink it.** Your first potion makes you a Sequence 9 Beyonder of its Pathway.
5. **Digest it by acting**: use your abilities the way your Sequence is meant to be played, take bounties, and so on, until your acting bar is at least 95% full.
6. **Complete your ritual** for the next Sequence (from Sequence 8 on). Skipping it is allowed, but costs Madness.
7. Repeat with the next potion.

## The stats that keep you alive

Being a Beyonder comes with four gauges. You can see all of them on your character screen (`/coi menu character`).

| Stat | What it does | Page |
|---|---|---|
| **Spirituality** | Pays for your abilities and refills over time | [Spirituality](/magic/spirituality/) |
| **Health** | Your hidden Beyonder HP, used when spells hit you | [Health](/magic/health/) |
| **Tiredness** | Fatigue that slows you down; sleep clears it | [Conditions](/magic/conditions/) |
| **Madness** | How close you are to losing control | [Madness and Mutation](/magic/mutation/) |

At 100% Madness you **lose control** of your power. Strong Beyonders then face an [Ordeal](/magic/ordeals/) and lose Sequences; weak ones lose everything.

## Beyonder Characteristics

A **Beyonder Characteristic** (players say "Char") is a coloured head that holds the power of one Pathway and Sequence. One Characteristic of the right Pathway and Sequence replaces all the main ingredients of that potion. It is also used to make [Sealed Artifacts](/magic/sealed-artifacts/), and a failed brew leaves one behind.

## Later on

- Change Pathway, or learn your neighbours: [Pathways](/magic/pathways/#can-i-change-pathways).
- Gain a second, smaller power from an Outer God: [Boons](/magic/boons/).
- From Sequence 3, seats are limited: [Usurpation](/magic/usurpation/), [Uniqueness](/magic/uniqueness/) and [Sequence 0](/magic/sequence-zero/).

The main site's player guide covers the same loop in short form: [Brew and drink your first potion](https://www.mysterria.net/en/guide/first-potion) and [Advancement](https://www.mysterria.net/en/guide/progression).
