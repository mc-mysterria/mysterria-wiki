A **Beyonder** is a player who has drunk a Beyonder potion and gained its powers, as in Lord of the Mysteries (LOTM). Every Beyonder walks one of the [22 Pathways](/magic/pathways/), climbing from **Sequence 9** (the weakest) towards **Sequence 0** (a god).

## The Beyonder loop

Every Sequence follows the same loop. Your first potion (Sequence 9) needs no Acting or ritual and costs no Madness, and the [starter bonus](/general/start/#4-choose-your-starter-bonus) can even skip it.

1. **Find the recipe** for your next potion, either a full recipe or all of its Recipe Pages. Recipes come from loot chests, and [Loot Focus](/magic/loot/#loot-focus-pity) guarantees one in the end.
2. **Gather the ingredients.** Some are found in chests, some drop from Beyonder creatures, some are mined from glowing nodes.
3. **Brew the potion** at a Magic Cauldron and pass the short brewing minigame.
4. **Drink it.** Your first potion makes you a Sequence 9 Beyonder of its Pathway.
5. **Digest it by acting.** Use your abilities the way your Sequence is meant to be played and take bounties until your Acting bar is at least 95% full.
6. **Complete your ritual** for the next Sequence (from Sequence 8 on). You may skip it, but that costs Madness.
7. Repeat with the next potion.

## The stats that keep you alive

Every Beyonder has four gauges, all shown on your character screen (`/coi menu character`).

| Stat | What it does | Page |
|---|---|---|
| **Spirituality** | Pays for your abilities and refills over time | [Spirituality](/magic/spirituality/) |
| **Health** | Your hidden Beyonder HP, used when spells hit you | [Health](/magic/health/) |
| **Tiredness** | Fatigue that slows you down. Sleep clears it | [Conditions](/magic/conditions/) |
| **Madness** | How close you are to losing control | [Madness and Mutation](/magic/mutation/) |

At 100% Madness you **lose control** of your power. Strong Beyonders then face an [Ordeal](/magic/ordeals/) and lose Sequences, but weak ones lose everything.

## Beyonder Characteristics

A **Beyonder Characteristic** is a coloured head that holds the power of one Pathway and Sequence. One Characteristic of the right Pathway and Sequence replaces all the main ingredients of that potion. It is also used to make [Sealed Artifacts](/magic/sealed-artifacts/), and a failed brew leaves one behind.
