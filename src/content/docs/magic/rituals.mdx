From Sequence 8 on, every advancement has a **ritual**, a personal list of Minecraft **advancements** to finish before you drink the next potion. Skipping it costs Madness.

Your **first potion (Sequence 9) needs no ritual.**

## How does a ritual work?

When you reach a Sequence, the game gives you a random set of advancements for the **next** Sequence. Each advancement's difficulty is worth some points, and your set adds up to the score that Sequence needs. The set stays the same when you log out.

| Next Sequence | 8 | 7 | 6 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Score needed** | 2 | 5 | 9 | 12 | 16 | 20 | 24 | 28 | 30 |

| Difficulty | Trivial | Easy | Medium | Hard | Expert | Master | Legendary | Exotic | Mythic |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Points** | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 | 15 | 20 |

A few rules shape your set.

- **Trivial** tasks only appear for rituals up to Sequence 5.
- **Legendary, Exotic and Mythic** tasks only appear from the Sequence 4 ritual on.
- Tasks in **the Nether** only appear once you are Sequence 7 or stronger, and tasks in **the End** once you are Sequence 4 or stronger.

## How do I check my ritual?

- `/ritual status` shows your score, for example "7/9 ritual score completed".
- To see **which** advancements you need, carry the recipe for your next Sequence. That is either a full recipe or its **Recipe Page 3** (the ritual page). Without it, you only see the score.
- `/ritual view <sequence>` opens your tasks in a menu. Reading the ritual page of a recipe also gives you a clickable link to it.

## Can I reroll a task?

If a task is impossible, bugged or unreasonable, you can ask staff to replace it.

1. Open `/ritual view <next sequence>` while carrying the recipe or page 3.
2. **Shift-click** the task.
3. Pick a reason (for example "Impossible on this server" or "Appears to be bugged") and send it.

Staff review the request and approve or deny it. You cannot request a reroll for a task you have already completed, and you can have only one pending request per task.

## Ritual credit

From the Sequence 5 ritual on, some activities count as **ritual credit** and fill part of your score without advancements. Each kind counts once per ritual.

| Activity | Points | Rituals |
|---|---|---|
| Attend a church service at an active church site | 1 | Sequence 5 to 0 |
| Complete an Acting bounty | 1 | Sequence 5 to 0 |
| Complete a staff-approved story ritual | 2 | Sequence 5 to 0 |
| Fight a seat war to its verdict | 3 | Sequence 3 to 0 |
| Hold your Uniqueness through a paid challenge | 3 | Sequence 2 to 0 |

Credit is capped per ritual: 3 points at Sequence 5, 4 at 4, 5 at 3, 6 at 2, 7 at 1 and 10 at 0.

The Sequence 0 ascension also needs a staff-certified **story ritual** of its own. See [Sequence 0](/magic/sequence-zero/).

## Ritual Books

A **Ritual Book** lets you skip ritual tasks. Right-click it and choose the task to skip.

| Book | Skips | Outer Gods Anchors shop (`/oga rewards`) |
|---|---|---|
| Ritual Book (Tier I) | 1 task | 160 points, 2 a week |
| Ritual Book (Tier II) | 2 tasks | 300 points, 1 a week |
| Ritual Book (Tier III) | Every task of the ritual | 550 points, 1 a month |

Ritual Books are also in the Helping Card exchanges of the [dungeons](/guides/dungeons/).

## What if I skip the ritual?

You can always drink without finishing the ritual, but the missing part costs Madness in proportion to how much is left. A fully skipped ritual costs **40% temporary** and **10% permanent** Madness. A half-finished ritual costs half of that, 20% temporary and 5% permanent.

The confirmation screen shows the exact cost before you drink. An incomplete ritual also cancels a [safe Pathway switch](/magic/pathways/#can-i-change-pathways). More on costs in [Madness and Mutation](/magic/mutation/#advancement-costs).

:::caution[Permanent Madness adds up]
Permanent Madness never fades with time. A few skipped rituals can leave you with a high floor that makes every later advancement more dangerous.
:::
