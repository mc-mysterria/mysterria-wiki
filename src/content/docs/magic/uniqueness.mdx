## What is a Uniqueness?

A **Uniqueness** is a Pathway's single authority, held by one Beyonder at a time. Holding your Pathway's Uniqueness and **accommodating** it to 95% is a requirement for [Sequence 0](/magic/sequence-zero/), and on the way it wakes four bonus traits.

Each of the 22 normal Pathways has exactly one. Outer Pathways and Boons do not.

## How do I open the Uniqueness menu?

Open your character menu (`/coi menu character`, or the M key with the [Client Mod](/guides/client-mod/)) and choose **Uniqueness**. Without the mod it opens as a chest menu. Its five sections are **Where you stand**, **Candidacy score**, **Participation**, **Physical manifestation** and **Usurpation**.

## How is a Uniqueness granted?

Nobody buys or rolls a Uniqueness. Every 2 hours the server scores every eligible Beyonder on the Pathway and grants it to the strongest candidate.

### Who can be a candidate?

- **Sequence 4 or stronger** on that Pathway
- an account at least **7 days** old
- at least **10 hours** of playtime
- an activity index of at least **1.5**
- a total score of at least **40**
- not withdrawn from evaluation

You can hold only one Uniqueness at a time.

### The candidacy score

Each part is scored out of 100, then weighted:

| Part | Weight | What it measures |
|---|---:|---|
| Sequence | 35% | Sequence 1 = 100, Sequence 2 = 90, Sequence 3 = 80, Sequence 4 = 60 |
| Playtime | 25% | Total hours on the server, counted up to 100 |
| Activity | 20% | How recently and regularly you play (index up to 5.0). This drops if you stop logging in |
| Acting | 15% | How far along the whole Pathway you have acted, from Sequence 9 up. Advancing never lowers it |
| Dedication | 5% | Half from days you played at least 30 minutes (up to 31), half from your average session length (up to 180 minutes) |

Staff can add a **Manual adjustment** from -20 to +20 on top of the weighted total. It is shown in the menu.

The current holder's whole score is multiplied by up to **x1.25** at 100% accommodation, which makes a settled holder harder to replace.

Daily Tides never change Uniqueness scoring.

## How can a Uniqueness change hands?

Killing the holder does **not** move it. These five things do:

1. The **automatic re-check** moves it to another candidate who scores at least **6 points** higher than the holder. A new holder is safe from this for **3 days**.
2. Another player spends Overflow on a [paid challenge](/magic/usurpation/#how-do-i-challenge-a-uniqueness) to force a re-score.
3. A rival who wins a [Sequence 0 vigil](/magic/sequence-zero/) against the holder takes the Uniqueness, but not the throne.
4. If the holder withdraws from evaluation, it goes straight to the next candidate.
5. Entering an [Ordeal](/magic/ordeals/) releases it automatically.

<Notice tone="warn" title="A new holder always starts from 0%">
When a Uniqueness changes hands, accommodation, the death count and the growth record all reset to zero.
</Notice>

## Can I opt out?

Yes. Taking part is the default. Switch it off under **Participation** with **Withdraw from evaluation**. The button asks you to confirm twice.

- If you hold the Uniqueness, it goes to the next candidate **immediately**.
- Rejoining later puts you back in the queue. It does not give the Uniqueness back.
- While withdrawn you are not scored and cannot challenge for a Uniqueness.

## How does accommodation grow?

Accommodation is how far the Uniqueness has settled into you, from 0% to 100%. It grows only while you hold it, from using your Pathway:

| Source | Accommodation gained |
|---|---|
| Acting earned | +0.02 points per 100 Acting |
| Spirituality spent | +0.001 points per 100 Spirituality |
| Each ability cast | +0.00005 points |

Acting is by far the biggest source. These amounts are then multiplied by your Sequence:

| Sequence | Growth multiplier |
|---|---:|
| 1 | x1.2 |
| 2 | x1.1 |
| 3 | x1.0 |
| 4 | x0.8 |

### Dying costs accommodation

Each death while holding a Uniqueness costs a flat **10 percentage points** (80% becomes 70%), whatever killed you. It never goes below **10%**. A death that is cancelled, for example by an artifact, costs nothing.

## What does it give?

### The four traits

Each trait wakes at a set accommodation and then grows in a straight line with accommodation, reaching its full value at 100%.

| Trait | Wakes at | Effect at 100% | Value when it wakes |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Spiritual Dominance** | 25% | +50% Spirituality regeneration | +12.5% |
| **Authority Amplification** | 50% | +30% ability power and damage | +15% |
| **Divine Resilience** | 75% | +20% maximum Spirituality | +15% |
| **Acting Acceleration** | 100% | +40% Acting gains | +40% |

Pathway-specific traits are not in the game.

### The road to Sequence 0

To claim a throne you must hold your Pathway's Uniqueness **and** have it at **95%** accommodation or more. [Sequence 0](/magic/sequence-zero/) lists the other requirements.

### Physical manifestation

Each Uniqueness has a cosmetic item. You can take it out or hide it from the **Physical manifestation** section. It is bound to you and can never leave your inventory. Some can be worn on the head.
