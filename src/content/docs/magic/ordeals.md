When a Beyonder **loses control**, their power breaks loose. On Mysterria that no longer always means losing everything. If you are strong enough, you face an **Ordeal**, a punishment you can partly play your way out of. You lose Sequences and Acting, but you keep your character.

## When do I get an Ordeal?

You lose control when:

- your **Madness reaches 100%**,
- you fail the **survival roll** of a risky Pathway switch, or drink a refused potion again within 5 seconds,
- you break a Sun **Notarization** contract, or another power pushes you over the edge.

What happens next depends on your **strongest** Sequence:

| Strongest Sequence | Outcome |
|---|---|
| 9, 8 or 7 | **Wipe**: your Beyonder is gone (see below) |
| 6 or stronger | An **Ordeal** |

:::tip[Anti-Control Charm]
An **Anti-Control Charm** in your inventory is used up and cancels the loss of control entirely, except for Notarization punishments. See [Madness and Mutation](/magic/mutation/#the-anti-control-charm).
:::

## What every Ordeal costs

Whichever Ordeal you get, entering it always:

- takes **half of your current Acting** on every Pathway you walk,
- ends your claim to a **Uniqueness**, and
- counts as an **offense** (see below).

The Sequences you lose depend on the Ordeal, and apply to all your Pathways.

## Which Ordeal will I get?

The cause decides, so the punishment fits what you did:

| Cause | Ordeal |
|---|---|
| Madness reached 100% | **Bloodlust** |
| A potion (failed switch roll, re-drinking a refused potion) | **Hollowing** |
| Anything else (broken contract, some Hanged Man and Visionary powers, staff) | **Sealing** |

## Bloodlust

You black out and **become a rampaging Warden**. For **90 seconds** you steer it from spectator view, and everything you kill adds to your score. The higher your score, the fewer Sequences you lose:

| Score | Sequences lost |
|---|---|
| Below 1,000 | 3 |
| 1,000 or more | 2 |
| 1,800 or more | 1 |
| 2,800 or more | 0 |

- The score thresholds are set for 10 players online. With fewer players they are lower (down to 30% of the table), with more they are higher.
- **What counts:** killing a Sequence 9 Beyonder is worth 150, and each Sequence stronger is worth 1.6 times more. A Warden is worth 3,000, a Wither 4,000 and the Ender Dragon 8,000. Other mobs count by their health and attack. Players who are not Beyonders are worth almost nothing, and killing the same target again is worth half as much each time.
- The Warden's hits are strong: two hits kill a Beyonder of your own Sequence.
- Bloodlust always adds **5% permanent Madness**, however well you do.
- Your **inventory goes into the Warden**. If someone kills it, your items drop where it died.
- If you disconnect or the server restarts mid-rampage, you lose 1 Sequence.

## Hollowing

Your soul is thrown out of your body. You drift as a spectator **in the Nether** and must find a **Nether portal** and fly through it back to the Overworld. There you hunt for a **Wild Beyonder** to take as a new body. Within 100 blocks your action bar shows a hint with its Pathway, Sequence and direction, and you possess it by drifting into it.

You have **2 hours** in total, and the clock runs even while you are offline. The body you take decides the cost:

| New body | Sequences lost | Permanent Madness |
|---|---|---|
| Same Pathway as you | 1 | None |
| A neighbouring Pathway | 1 | +10% |
| An unrelated Pathway | 2 | +25% |
| No body found in 2 hours | 1 | +20% |

Do not take too long. While you hunt in the Overworld you lose **5% of your Acting every 2 minutes**, and after 30 minutes of hunting you lose **one extra Sequence every 30 minutes**, up to 3 extra. If nothing is near you, a fresh Wild Beyonder sometimes spawns close by, sometimes of your own Pathway. See [Wild Beyonders](/magic/wild-beyonders/).

## Sealing

You are **sealed in a private realm**, in your own body, for **14 days**. The sentence counts in real time, including while you are offline. You lose **1 Sequence**, and your inventory is stored and returned when you are freed.

There is one way to shorten it. When a [Crimson Moon](/magic/crimson-moon/) rises naturally, you are released into the world for as long as the moon lasts (at most 30 minutes) and given an **exile bounty**. Each exile bounty you complete takes **6 hours** off your sentence, up to half of the 14 days.

## Repeat offenses

Every Ordeal counts as an offense. The count resets after **14 days** without a new one.

| Offense within 14 days | Extra cost |
|---|---|
| 1st | None |
| 2nd | +1 extra Sequence lost and **+15% permanent Madness** |
| 3rd | No Ordeal. You are **wiped**, whatever your Sequence |

## The wipe

If you are Sequence 7 or weaker, or on your third offense, losing control wipes your Beyonder:

- You lose your **Pathway, Sequence and all abilities**.
- Your power becomes a **mutant** that hunts you and nearby players. Your **items go into it**, and killing it gets them back. It may also drop a Beyonder Characteristic.
- You must wait **three days** before you can become a Beyonder again.

## How do I avoid all this?

- Keep your Madness low, and watch your floor. See [Madness and Mutation](/magic/mutation/).
- Finish your [rituals](/magic/rituals/) and reach 100% [Acting](/magic/acting/) before you drink.
- Avoid unsafe Pathway switches, and never re-drink a refused potion.
- Keep an Anti-Control Charm in your inventory once you have something to lose.
