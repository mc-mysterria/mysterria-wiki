Beyonder Creatures are monsters touched by Beyonder power. They hit harder than ordinary mobs and are the main source of **droppable** Beyonder ingredients, the ones you cannot find in [chests](/magic/loot/).

## What kinds of Beyonder Creatures are there?

There are two kinds.

| | Custom creatures | Badge creatures |
|---|---|---|
| Looks | Their own models and animations | A normal Minecraft mob with a Beyonder badge |
| Behaviour | Advanced AI. They group up, use special abilities, retreat and call for help | Vanilla AI with more health and stronger attacks |
| Name and health | Coloured nameplate, health shown in the action bar | Badge over the mob |
| Ingredient drops | **x8** the base chance | **x2** the base chance, plus a pity bonus |

Both kinds deal their own damage to Beyonder players directly, instead of going through the normal Minecraft hit.

## What do Beyonder Creatures drop?

Each creature carries its own droppable ingredients. The drop chance depends on the ingredient's Sequence and the kind of creature:

| Sequence | Base chance | Badge creature (x2) | Custom creature (x8) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | 15% | 30% | 100% |
| 8 | 10% | 20% | 80% |
| 7 | 7% | 14% | 56% |
| 6 | 5% | 10% | 40% |
| 5 | 3% | 6% | 24% |
| 4 | 2% | 4% | 16% |
| 3 | 1% | 2% | 8% |
| 2 | 0.5% | 1% | 4% |
| 1 | 0.2% | 0.4% | 1.6% |

A custom creature always drops its Sequence 9 ingredient, because 15% x 8 is capped at 100%. The pity bonus and Daily Tides raise these chances further.

### The Mob Slaying pity bonus

Each badge creature kill that drops no ingredient adds **+5%** to your next roll, up to **+50%**. A "Mob Slaying pity increased" message tells you it went up. The bonus resets when you get a drop.

### Acting from kills

Killing a Beyonder Creature gives Acting through the **World** source. That source is capped for your Sequence, so hunting alone will not fill your bar. The caps are on the [Acting](/magic/acting/) page. Creature kills never digest the Abyss Pathway, which only digests from killing Beyonder players.

## Where do Beyonder Creatures spawn?

- **Not near spawn.** No Beyonder Creature spawns within 300 blocks of 0, 0.
- **By biome.** Every creature has a list of biomes it can spawn in. If an area has had no creatures for weeks, no creature claims that biome. Try a different one.
- **By dimension.** Most weaker creatures (Sequence 9 to 7) live in the Overworld, and most stronger ones (Sequence 6 to 4) in the Nether. A few appear in both.

The exact biome lists are kept secret on purpose.

## When do more creatures appear?

| Event | Spawns | Drops |
|---|---|---|
| [Crimson Moon](/magic/crimson-moon/) night | x2.5 | |
| Hunting Tide | x2 | x2, and Sequence 3 or stronger ingredients x3 |
| Crimson Tide | | x1.5 |

The [Daily Tides](/guides/tides/) page lists which Tide runs on which day. The spawn boosts from all sources stack, capped at x4.

The Crimson Moon is the best night to hunt. You cannot sleep it away, and fishing can pull up ingredients or hostile creatures. The bounty board (`/bounty`) sometimes offers a **Crimson Moon Hunt** or a creature hunt for extra Acting.

## How do I survive a fight with one?

:::caution[They can kill you]
Custom creatures can outclass a young Beyonder. Bring armour and a way out, and do not fight a Nether creature at Sequence 9.
:::

- Check the nameplate before you engage. A creature far above your Sequence is not worth the risk.
- Custom creatures retreat when hurt and come back with friends. Finish them quickly or leave.
- Hunt in a group on Crimson Moon nights, when spawns pile up.
