### What is a throne?

There are **22 thrones**, one for each normal Pathway (outer Pathways do not have one). Whoever sits a throne is that
Pathway's **god**: Sequence 0, a title above their head, a row in the public Pantheon, and one spell nobody else in the
world has.

This is the top of the ladder. There is nothing above it.

:::danger[A throne cannot be taken from a seated god - ever]
There is no seat war for Sequence 0, and there never will be. A god has no acting bar, so a war against one is
unwinnable by construction. A throne changes hands for exactly one reason: **its god stopped showing up.**
:::

That single rule is why the road to a throne is as long as it is. If a seat cannot be contested once it is held, the
contest has to happen *before* it is held - that contest is the **vigil**, and everything in this page up to the rite
itself is how it works.

---

### Before you can even try

Four things are checked before any requirement is. These are refusals, not failures - no amount of preparation gets
around them. You are turned away outright if:

- The Pathway is an **outer Pathway**.
- You are not **Sequence 1** on it.
- It is not your **primary** Pathway (your census match).
- You are already involved in a vigil somewhere else.

If none of those block you, six real requirements stand between you and declaring.

### The six requirements

| # | Requirement | What it takes |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | **The Uniqueness, accommodated** | You hold your Pathway's [Uniqueness](/magic/uniqueness/) **and** have accommodated it to at least **95%**. A holder at 94% fails; a 95%-accommodated player who is not the holder also fails. |
| R2 | **A congregation** | Your [church's](/magic/churches/) effective anchor weight reaches **20**. That sits under what a single active core site run by a Sequence 1 leader can produce on its own - a test of *does a congregation exist*, not *do you run the biggest town on the server*. Believers of any Pathway count; believers of yours count for more. You must be its leader, co-leader, or angel. |
| R3 | **A 30-point ritual set** | A Sequence 0 ritual set, scored like every other sequence's. See the next section. |
| R4 | **The stake** | **31,104 banked Overflow** - half a Sequence 1 acting bar, and deliberately the exact price of a paid Uniqueness challenge. A fallen god re-ascending inside their grace period pays half of this instead. |
| R5 | **Fourteen days at Sequence 1** | Calendar days on this Pathway since you last advanced or arrived here, not active-play days - a holiday must not restart godhood, and no timezone is favoured. A fallen god's grace period waives this. |
| R6 | **A Sequence 0 potion** | Brewed in the cauldron from **three Sequence 1 Beyonder Characteristics of the same Pathway**, and nothing else - no supplementary half. This is deliberately the rarest ingredient price in the game. |

:::caution[The potion is a requirement, not an advancement]
Drinking a Sequence 0 potion on its own does nothing - it is refused outright. A throne is never drunk into existence.
Only the ascension rite itself consumes it.
:::

`/coi ascend` shows you all six as a live checklist, so you always know exactly what is missing. `/coi ascend board`
shows everyone else's progress on the public race board, and `/coi ascend private` takes your name off that board -
never out of the count.

### The eight legendary deeds

R3 asks for a ritual set worth **30 points** - the same mechanic as every other advancement, just carrying the
highest number in the game. What makes a Sequence 0 set different is the **legendary pool**: eight deeds tied to
things the server actually watched you do, each worth **10 points**, and only ever rolled for Sequence 4 and below.
Every one is recorded automatically the moment it completes, except the last, which staff certify by hand.

| Deed | Earned by | Tally needed |
|---|---|---|
| A seat war fought to its verdict | Won or lost, both sides are credited - you simply have to stand in one | 1 |
| A Uniqueness defended | Hold yours through a paid challenge | 1 |
| Devout church service | Attend church services | 10 |
| Your honorific invoked | Be the resolved target of a successful [honorific](/magic/honorific-names/) chant | 1 |
| An Ordeal survived | Come through an Ordeal with your Beyonder intact | 1 |
| A pinnacle bounty | Complete a bounty objective while standing at Sequence 1 | 1 |
| Diligence kept | Earn acting on seven separate Tide days | 7 |
| A story ritual | Certified by staff - see below | 1 |

:::note[Your set is assigned, not chosen]
Rituals are dealt to you from the pool, so you will not necessarily draw all eight of these - and you can ask staff to
reroll an individual entry. Up to **10 of the 30 points** can also be covered by service instead of advancements: a
church service is worth 1 point, a bounty objective 1, a staff-approved story ritual 2, a seat war fought 3, and a
Uniqueness defended 3.
:::

#### The story ritual - the deed you earn from us

The eighth deed has no automated hook, on purpose. It exists for things worth more than the server can measure:

> Stage a public, server-wide event that enacts your Pathway's role - the role it actually plays in the novel - and
> make the server live through it.

Not a screenshot, not a solo grind: an event that was announced in advance, that other players attended, that had
consequences, and that could only have been run by a Beyonder of *your* Pathway. The Fool might convene a Tarot Club
above the fog; the Red Priest might declare and fight a real, consented war between two churches; the Hanged Man might
broker a treaty between two genuinely hostile factions and make it hold.

Write to staff **before** you run it, not after - tell them what you are planning, when, and who it involves. They
will tell you whether it counts and what would make it count.

### Declaring, and the vigil

With all six requirements met, you **declare**. Your stake is spent, your Sequence 0 potion goes into escrow with its
expiry frozen (so it cannot be traded away or crystallise while nobody can drink it), and the whole server is told.

A **72-hour window** opens. Any other eligible Sequence 1 player on that Pathway may pay the same stake to contest you
during it.

If nobody does, the window closes and you move straight to the rite.

#### If somebody contests

The highest bid standing at the window's close wins the right to contest; every other bid is refunded in full. A
**seven-day contest** follows, scored in War Points earned from real acting gains - exactly the metric a seat war
uses. Standings broadcast once a day from the second day on. These are calendar days, not contested-only days: both of
you chose to be here, so absence simply scores zero.

**Ties go to the ascender** - they hold the Uniqueness and already passed every requirement, so a draw changes
nothing.

| Verdict | Loser pays | Winner gets | Armed afterwards |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ascender wins** | Challenger burns half their stake | The ascender proceeds to the rite | 14-day contest ban on the challenger |
| **Challenger wins** | Ascender burns half their stake | **The Uniqueness** - not the throne | 7-day pathway cooldown, 14-day winner protection on the Uniqueness, 24h re-declare cooldown on the ascender |

A challenger who wins does **not** become a god. They take the claim and then have to walk the whole road themselves -
including regrowing 95% accommodation from zero. The beaten ascender keeps their seat, their tenure, and every other
requirement they already had.

:::tip[Every exit without a verdict refunds you in full]
Withdrawn, outbid, the throne filled under you, cancelled, an error - every one of these returns your stake and your
potion whole, and arms no cooldown. Withdrawing is free for the first **15 minutes**; after that it costs half,
because a declaration is broadcast server-wide and cannot be spammed for free. A **lost** contest burns half the stake
but still returns the potion whole - it bought a claim on the throne, and the throne itself was never taken.
:::

Once the vigil is decided, you have **48 hours** to be online for the ceremony before it lapses on its own - stake
back in full, no cooldown, because nothing irreversible has happened yet.

### The summons and the herald

Winning the vigil summons you. You have **24 hours** to pick your hour before the rite starts on its own, and **7
days** before an unanswered summons is returned to the gate as forfeited.

When you answer it, the server gets **10 minutes of warning**: a boss bar, a card in chat, the coordinates of where it
is happening, and a one-minute call. `/coi ascension where` shows them any time during the countdown. Those ten
minutes are for everybody else to arrive - you are not rooted yet, so walk to the spot and let the crowd gather.

Ceremonies are serialised: if two are summoned close together, the second waits and is told whose rite it is queued
behind.

### The rite

The whole act runs **about two and a half minutes**, and it belongs to your Pathway - the sky changes, the ground
changes, and all 22 Pathways ship their own set piece rather than a recoloured copy of a shared one.

A few things worth knowing if you are standing in one:

- **It cannot be interrupted by rivals.** From the moment it begins you are invulnerable and cannot be
  hard-controlled. A rite ends early for exactly three reasons: you left, staff aborted it, or you entered a state no
  rite can survive. Damage is never one of them.
- **Nothing is spent until the halfway point.** Up to then the rite can abort freely and you lose nothing at all - the
  stake and the potion stay exactly where they are and you can answer the summons again. The throne is actually taken
  at the halfway mark, and that moment is the one irreversible tick.
- **Witnesses are paid.** Stand within 24 blocks of the rite and you get **3% of your own acting bar**, a flash, and
  30 seconds of Regeneration.
- **The crowd chants.** Five times during the rite a cry goes up - your Pathway's own line - and anyone who answers it
  in chat earns **another 1.5% of their bar per call**. Answer all five and that is worth more than one acting
  trigger, just for showing up. Any installed language's wording is accepted, and punctuation, spacing and case are
  all forgiven.

When the throne is taken, the whole server feels it for a few minutes, wherever players are standing - the Fool drags
everyone down one sequence, the Sun turns night to glare, the Door sets nearby doors slamming, the Tyrant brings rain
and a tsunami that was never actually there. None of it can kill you, strand you, disarm you, or take anything
permanent from you, and nothing it does survives a restart.

The spot where it happened is marked permanently on the map (BlueMap and squaremap both show it) - the site outlives
the god.

### What godhood grants: Deity Authority

Becoming a god hands you **Deity Authority**, one spell with three faces, shared by all 22 thrones with no
per-Pathway code behind it - a 23rd throne is a config edit, not a rewrite.

**Favor** - you hand a believer of your own Pathway a slice of their own acting bar, out of a capped allowance that
refills on the clock: **60 bar-percent a week**, about 12 favors, 5% at a time, once per recipient per week, banking
up to two weeks' worth. The unit is always a percentage of *their* bar, never raw points, so one favor is worth the
same to a Sequence 9 as to a Sequence 2. A Favor can never inflate Overflow, War Points, Uniqueness, your own
progress, or anyone's own run at Sequence 0 - a full bar is simply not eligible.

**Behest** - you ask a devoted member of your church to appear, to proclaim your name, or to make pilgrimage.
Answering pays them one Favor from that same allowance - a behest is a favor with a task attached, never a second
income stream. Every verb completes on something the member actively does (a click, a chant, a walk); none of them
can move, damage, or disarm someone who did not act, so a behest can never pull anyone out of a dungeon or into a
trap. Refusing or ignoring one costs **Disfavor** - a few days without Favor or Behest from that god, plus a real drag
on acting gain and a one-off dose of madness. It touches nothing else: no uniqueness, no membership, no kit.

**Presence** - a passive aura within 24 blocks: believers of your Pathway nearby take **10% less incoming madness**
and **15% less tiredness gain**. There is deliberately no acting bonus here - that would turn standing next to a god
into an income.

**What church membership buys, exactly: no stat at all.** Favor reaches every believer of your Pathway whether or not
they joined a church, and so does Presence. Membership unlocks exactly one thing - *being addressable by a Behest* -
and the reward for answering is a Favor the god could have handed out for free anyway. A per-member devotion toggle
lets anyone opt out of being summoned instantly and for free; opting back in waits a week.

Your [honorific name](/magic/honorific-names/) also keeps working while you are offline: when someone completes a
chant of it, your Pathway's own blessing fires at their feet regardless of whether you are online to see it.

:::note[A fresh god sits at a 30% madness floor]
That is not a bug - it is the price of standing above what a human mind is built to hold. A larger, healthier
congregation pushes that floor down; letting your church decay pushes it back up. The floor is computed against your
church's anchor weight the same way other high-sequence madness relief is - see the [Churches](/magic/churches/) page
for how anchor weight and madness floor relief work.
:::

### Keeping the throne

> Be online and not AFK for twenty minutes, on at least four days out of any thirty, or the throne empties.

That is the entire cost of upkeep. Nothing else counts toward it - not abilities cast, not chants received, not
anchors held, not time spent on another server in the network. Presence is the one metric nobody can argue with, and
it is deliberately the cheapest thing a real player does by accident.

Four present days, not three or five, is deliberate: three would let an infrequent login hold a throne forever, and
five would start punishing someone for one genuinely bad month. A brand-new reign can never lapse before the
thirty-day window has even filled up once.

**Going away?** `/coi god absence <days>` declares a leave of up to **21 days**, usable once every 60. The declared
days are excluded from the window rather than paused inside it, so the question is postponed, not reset.

**The warning ladder.** You are warned privately at **14 days** left on the clock, then publicly at **7, 3, and 1**.
The public half is not shaming - since nobody can ever take a throne by force, making an opening visible early enough
for someone else to be ready for it is simply fair, and a god who is surprised by a public warning is exactly the god
who was not reading the private ones.

**If it lapses anyway**, you are not wiped out:

- You drop to **Sequence 1** on that Pathway, landing with **95% of a full bar**.
- The throne empties and anybody who meets the gate may claim it.
- You get a **60-day grace period**, during which re-ascending costs **half the stake** and your fourteen days of
  tenure are already counted as served.

Halved, never waived - a lapse should sting, but it should never be cheaper than the aspirant who spent two months
honestly banking for their first attempt.
