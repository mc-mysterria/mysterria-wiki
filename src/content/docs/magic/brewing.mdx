Every Sequence starts with a potion, and you brew it yourself at a Magic Cauldron. Brewing works the same way for Sequence 9 and for every potion after it.

## What do I need to brew a potion?

- A **recipe** for the potion's Pathway and Sequence, or a full set of its **Recipe Pages**.
- A **Magic Cauldron**, the brewing altar you build from a blueprint.
- The recipe's **main** and **supplementary ingredients**. One **Beyonder Characteristic** of the same Pathway and Sequence can replace all the main ingredients.

To drink anything stronger than Sequence 9, you also need at least 95% [Acting](/magic/acting/), and your [ritual](/magic/rituals/) counts.

## The Magic Cauldron (brewing altar)

The altar comes in three tiers, and the tier shifts your brewing result by one grade.

| Altar | Effect on the brewing minigame |
|---|---|
| **Crude Altar** | Your result drops one grade (Excellent becomes Good, Good becomes Poor, Poor becomes Failed) |
| **Ritual Altar** | No change |
| **Grand Altar** | Your result rises one grade (Failed becomes Poor, Poor becomes Good, Good becomes Excellent) |

Blueprints come from loot chests as a **Strange Scroll**, the **Altar Scrolls** category of [Loot Focus](/magic/loot/#loot-focus-pity). Hold the scroll and **right-click a block** to preview the structure, then **right-click again** to place it in a clear area. Left-clicking does nothing.

<Notice tone="warn" title="Sacrificial Altars cannot brew">
  The **Crude Sacrificial Altar**, **Sacrificial Altar** and **Grand Sacrificial Altar** look similar, but they are for advancing [Boons](/magic/boons/) and cannot brew potions. Check the scroll's name before you place it.
</Notice>

## Recipes and Recipe Pages

You can find a **full recipe**, or a recipe split into **Recipe Pages** named "Recipe Page *n* (Sequence *s*)".

| Page | Contains |
|---|---|
| Page 1 | The main ingredients |
| Page 2 | The supplementary ingredients |
| Page 3 | The ritual requirements |

Sequence 9 has no ritual, so its recipes have only pages 1 and 2. Right-click a page to read it. To turn pages into a recipe, put **exactly one of each page** for the same Pathway and Sequence into a crafting grid, with nothing else.

Recipes can be for any Pathway, not only your own. To switch Pathway with a potion, see [Pathways](/magic/pathways/#can-i-change-pathways).

## Ingredients

Each potion has its own main and supplementary ingredients, and they come in three kinds.

- **Foundable** ingredients come mostly from loot chests.
- **Droppable** ingredients come mostly from [Beyonder Creatures](/magic/creatures/).
- **Mineable** ingredients grow as glowing red nodes in the world. Right-click one to harvest it.

Wild Beyonders and Rift Bosses are other sources, covered in [Loot and Loot Focus](/magic/loot/#ingredients). With the [Client Mod](/guides/client-mod/), hovering an ingredient shows its Pathway, its Sequence, and whether it is main or supplementary.

## How do I brew?

Open your Magic Cauldron. Put the **main ingredients** (or one Beyonder Characteristic) in the red slots on the **left**, the **supplementary ingredients** in the blue slots on the **right**, and the **recipe** in the yellow slot at the **top centre**.

<Figure src={cauldron} alt="The Magic Cauldron menu with the main slots on the left, the supplementary slots on the right and the recipe slot at the top" caption="Main ingredients left, supplementary right, recipe at the top." contain />

Put the ingredients in the same order the recipe lists them. If anything is wrong, nothing happens and you keep your items. Extra copies of an ingredient are given back.

When everything matches, a short **brewing minigame** starts, and your grade decides the result.

| Grade | Result |
|---|---|
| **Excellent** | You get the potion |
| **Good** | You get the potion |
| **Poor** | 50% chance of the potion. Otherwise the brew fails |
| **Failed** | The brew fails, with a small explosion and a few seconds of Poison |

**A failed brew** uses up the **recipe** and the **supplementary ingredients**. Your main ingredients fuse into a **Beyonder Characteristic** of that Pathway and Sequence, which you can use as the main ingredient next time.

The minigame gets harder for stronger Sequences, from very easy at Sequence 9 to hardest at Sequence 2 and above. **Moon** Beyonders get it one step easier.

<Figure src={minigame} alt="The Focus your will minigame, a chest menu with three buttons where one glows green" caption="Focus your will, one of the minigames. Click the glowing button before time runs out." contain />

`/coi practice` lets you rehearse the minigames for free, with nothing at stake.

## Drinking the potion

When you drink, a confirmation screen shows what the advancement will cost in missing Acting, an incomplete ritual or a risky Pathway switch. Nothing happens until you confirm, and cancelling gives the potion back.

<Figure src={drinkConfirm} alt="The Advancement Details screen with a green Confirm button, the potion, and a tooltip saying there are no drawbacks and the advancement is safe" caption="The confirmation screen for a first potion. With nothing missing, it says the advancement is safe." contain />

After you confirm, another short minigame runs. **Excellent** gives a short burst of Regeneration and Speed, and **Good** gives nothing extra. **Poor** or **Failed** adds some temporary Madness (more on Failed), plus Nausea or Blindness for a few seconds.

A potion only works if it is **exactly one Sequence stronger** than your current Sequence. Any other potion is refused. See [Madness and Mutation](/magic/mutation/#advancement-costs) for what advancing can cost.

## How long does a potion last?

A potion lasts **48 hours** from the moment it is made, and its description shows the time left. When it expires, it turns into a **Sealed Artifact** of the same Pathway and Sequence. If there is none, it becomes a **Beyonder Characteristic**, and failing that a **Ruined Potion**. An expired potion can no longer advance you.

A potion you receive as a reward gets a fresh 48 hours when it first enters your inventory.

## Other ways to get a potion

- Loot chests sometimes hold a finished potion, and the **Potions** category of [Loot Focus](/magic/loot/#loot-focus-pity) guarantees one.
- A Sun Beyonder at Sequence 4 can dissolve a potion or Sealed Artifact held in the offhand into its Beyonder Characteristic, with **Beyonder Characteristic Purification**.
- The **Sequence 0** potion is brewed from the Sequence 0 recipe and **three Sequence 1 Beyonder Characteristics** of the same Pathway, with nothing else. See [Sequence 0](/magic/sequence-zero/).
