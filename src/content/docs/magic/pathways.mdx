A **Pathway** is the road of power you walk as a Beyonder. Mysterria has all **22 Pathways** from Lord of the Mysteries (LOTM). Each one climbs from **Sequence 9**, the weakest, to **Sequence 0**, a god. You join a Pathway by drinking its Sequence 9 potion, and every potion after that moves you one Sequence up the same road.

This page is the overview: every Pathway with its first and last Sequence, its neighbours, and the rules for moving between them. For every ability of a Pathway, open its page on the main site.

## Sequences and ranks

Every Pathway has ten Sequences. A lower number is stronger. From Sequence 4 up, each step has a rank name:

| Sequence | Rank | Seats per Pathway |
|---|---|---|
| 9 to 5 | (no rank) | No limit |
| 4 | Demigod | No limit |
| 3 | Saint | 18 |
| 2 | Angel | 9 |
| 1 | Archangel | 3 |
| 0 | Deity | 1 |

Seats are counted **per Pathway**, so 18 Fool Saints do not block a Sun Beyonder. When a Sequence is full, nobody else can drink into it until a seat opens. You can see who holds each seat on the [Ascension page](https://www.mysterria.net/en/ascension). Seats can be challenged: see [Usurpation](/magic/usurpation/). Sequence 0 is not an ordinary potion step but a full ascension, explained on [Sequence 0](/magic/sequence-zero/).

## Neighbouring Pathways

Some Pathways are **neighbours** (adjacent). Neighbours decide how risky a Pathway switch is, and which [Wild Beyonders](/magic/wild-beyonders/) will trade with you. The groups are:

| Group | Pathways |
|---|---|
| 1 | Sun, Visionary, Tyrant, White Tower, Hanged Man |
| 2 | Fool, Error, Door (the "Lord of Mysteries" group) |
| 3 | Red Priest, Demoness |
| 4 | Darkness, Death, Twilight Giant |
| 5 | Paragon, Hermit |
| 6 | Mother, Moon |
| 7 | Abyss, Chained |
| 8 | Black Emperor, Justiciar |
| None | Wheel of Fortune has no neighbours |

## Choosing your first Pathway

- You join a Pathway only by **drinking its Sequence 9 potion**. The Pathway you click in the newbie menu just aims your starter bonus; see [Quick Start](/general/start/#4-choose-your-starter-bonus).
- The Pathway you get is decided by the recipe or potion you find, so stay flexible early on. [Loot Focus](/magic/loot/#loot-focus-pity) helps you get recipes.
- Read a Pathway's abilities on the main site before you commit. The server's abilities are its own and often differ from the novel.

## Can I change Pathways?

Yes, but a switch is also an advancement, and it can be dangerous.

1. The new potion must be **exactly one Sequence stronger** than your current Sequence. You cannot switch sideways at the same Sequence with a potion.
2. You need at least **95% acting** on your current Pathway, as with any advancement. See [Acting](/magic/acting/).
3. Your [ritual](/magic/rituals/) for the new Sequence counts as usual.

A switch is **safe** (no switch penalty) only when all of these are true:

- the new Pathway is a **neighbour** of your current one,
- the new potion is **Sequence 4 or stronger** (Sequence 3 or stronger if either Pathway is Fool, Error or Door), and
- your ritual is **complete**. An incomplete ritual cancels the safe status.

Every other switch has a cost and a survival roll:

| Switch | Temporary Madness | Permanent Madness | Survival chance |
|---|---|---|---|
| To a neighbour, before the safe Sequence | 25% | 10% | 75% |
| To a Pathway that is not a neighbour | 45% | 15% | 35% |
| To or from Fool, Error or Door | 55% | 20% | 20% |

These are on top of the normal acting and ritual Madness. The confirmation screen shows the exact cost before you drink. Failing the survival roll means you **lose control**, which starts an [Ordeal](/magic/ordeals/) or, for weaker Beyonders, wipes your character.

<Notice tone="warn" title="Permanent Madness">
  Permanent Madness does not fade with time and ordinary cures cannot remove it. See [Madness and Mutation](/magic/mutation/).
</Notice>

### Pathway Transfer Token

A **Pathway Transfer Token** moves you to another Pathway sideways: you keep your Sequence and your acting progress, with no switch Madness. It is single use. Your banked Overflow on the old Pathway is lost, and for some days you cannot contest seats on the new Pathway. It can be bought in the Outer Gods Anchors shop (`/oga rewards`) for 1,200 points, at most once a month.

## Can I go back down a Sequence?

Yes. There are a few ways down:

- **Beyonder Characteristic Purification** (Sun, Sequence 4). The Sun Beyonder must keep you within 5 blocks for 150 seconds. You drop one Sequence and drop your Beyonder Characteristic. Used on a Sequence 9, it turns them back into an ordinary human. A Sequence 0 cannot be purified.
- **Beyonder Characteristic Theft** (Error, Sequence 2) takes one Sequence from a willing target.
- **Ordeals** after a loss of control regress you. See [Ordeals](/magic/ordeals/).
- Dying in the Death zone of a [Cosmos Incursion](/guides/cosmos-incursions/) can cost a Sequence at Sequence 4 and stronger.
