## What is an Honorific Name?

In Lord of the Mysteries, chanting the honorific name of a powerful Beyonder lets the spiritual world carry your prayer to them. On Mysterria, every Beyonder at **Sequence 4 or stronger** can write their own Honorific Name. Other players can then chant it in chat to reach you, wherever you are.

An example in five lines:

> *King of the Skies*
>
> *Emperor of the Seas*
>
> *Lord of the Calamity*
>
> *God of the Storms*
>
> *The Wanderer Known to Mysterria*

## How do I set my Honorific Name?

Open your character menu (`/coi menu character`, or the M key with the [Client Mod](/guides/client-mod/)) and click the Honorific Name slot. The Client Mod's character sheet has it too. Until you set one, you get a chat reminder every 30 minutes with a button that opens the editor.

<Figure src={honorificEditor} alt="The Honorific Name editor with three filled lines and a status tooltip saying Complete and Active" caption="The Honorific Name editor. The book at the top shows whether your name is complete and active." contain />

The number of lines depends on your Sequence:

| Your Sequence | Lines |
|---|---:|
| 4 or 3 | 5 |
| 2 or 1 | 4 |
| 0 | 3 |

When you advance and the name gets shorter, the last line is kept. The name is then checked again and may stay inactive until its lines describe your new self.

### Line rules

Each line must:

- Be 2 to 80 characters long.
- Not start with `!` or `/`.
- Not be only digits.
- Have no double spaces and no spaces at the start or end.
- Use letters (Latin or Cyrillic), digits and basic punctuation only.

### What makes a name valid?

A valid name must point clearly to **you**. The editor shows how much identity evidence it found, and you need a score of **3**. One strong personal descriptor or several related clues are enough. Evidence that counts:

- Your username or a meaningful part of it
- Your town, church, church site, linked land or nation
- Your Pathway or your current Sequence title
- An ability you have at your current Sequence
- Recognised Pathway concepts and official phrases
- A personal descriptor approved by staff

Matching ignores capital letters, punctuation, spacing, apostrophes and hyphens. English and Ukrainian names for Pathways, Sequences and abilities are recognised.

The editor rejects incomplete names, wrong line counts, blank or repeated lines, ordinary chat phrases, one-word filler and names that already answer to someone else. A rejected name is kept as a draft, but chanting it does nothing until it is valid.

<Notice tone="warn" title="Choose wisely">
Your name does not need your username, but it must have enough real identity evidence to tell you apart. Nobody else can claim an invocation that already answers to you.
</Notice>

## How do I chant someone's name?

Type each line of their name as a normal chat message, in order.

- You have **15 seconds** between lines.
- A line of three or more words still counts if it sits inside a longer sentence.
- You cannot chant your own name, except through a puppet or Avatar body.

When the last line lands:

- You enter a **15-minute** chanting cooldown.
- If the owner is online, they see your coordinates and a prompt to respond, which expires after **2 minutes**.
- If the owner belongs to a church, the chant counts as an outside prayer to that [church](/magic/churches/), and the owner may invite you to join it.
- The owner is credited with the legendary deed **Your honorific invoked**, which counts toward [Sequence 0](/magic/sequence-zero/).

<Notice tone="info" title="Watch out for Disguise">
The Error Pathway's Disguise ability can secretly intercept a church's next completed Honorific Name call or Church Blessing. The church owner and Angels are warned and can stop it by saying the thief's name in chat.
</Notice>

## What can I do when my name is chanted?

Click the prompt to open the **Divine Response** menu:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Teleport** | Opens a portal and takes you to the chanter. You must be in the same world, and it does not work in dungeons |
| **Smite** | Strikes the chanter with lightning for 4 damage (2 hearts) |
| **Gift Items** | Sends one item from your inventory to the chanter, at any distance |

Using any of them starts a **30-minute** cooldown for you as the owner. Other players can still chant your name in the meantime, and each chanter has their own cooldown.

## What happens when you chant a god's name?

At [Sequence 0](/magic/sequence-zero/) the name has 3 lines. Chanting it still opens the god's Divine Response menu as usual. On top of that, every completed chant of a god's name blesses the **chanter** straight away, even if the god is offline or does not answer.

- The blessing lasts up to **2 minutes** and is shown with the Pathway's own particles and sound.
- Each god's blessing has a **60-minute** cooldown that is **shared by every chanter** of that god. If someone else chanted it within the hour, your chant still reaches the god, but no blessing fires.
- The blessing only affects the chanter. It never places blocks or touches anyone else.
