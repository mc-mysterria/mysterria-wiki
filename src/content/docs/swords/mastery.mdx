A Mysterria weapon grows stronger the more it fights. **Mastery** is kept on the weapon itself, so whoever wields it gets its bonuses.

## Earning mastery

Every hit the weapon lands, swings and arts alike, earns it mastery XP: about **1 XP for every 1% of the foe's health** it takes off, so killing an equal foe is worth about 100.

| Foe | XP |
|---|---|
| Monsters and Beyonder creatures | ×1, at most **2,000 a day** across all your weapons |
| [Wild Beyonders](/magic/wild-beyonders/) | ×2, no limit |
| Players | ×3, no limit |

Killing the same player again on the same day gives less: 100%, then 50%, 25% and 10%. Fighting different players is never reduced. When you reach the daily limit from monsters, the chat tells you once; Wild Beyonders and players still teach your blade.

## Tiers

| Tier | XP | Bonuses |
|---|---|---|
| I | 1,000 | Art cooldown −3% |
| II | 3,000 | Art cooldown −5%, art cost −5% |
| III | 7,000 | +2% damage, art cooldown −5%, art cost −5%, a slightly longer combo window |
| IV | 15,000 | +3% damage, art cooldown −8%, art cost −10% |
| V | 30,000 | +5% damage, art cooldown −12%, art cost −15%, a longer combo window |

The bonuses don't add up across tiers: each tier lists everything the weapon has at that tier. From the monster limit alone, a weapon reaches tier I in a day and tier V in about two weeks; fighting Beyonders and players is faster.

The weapon's lore shows its tier, its XP towards the next tier and its bonuses. You get a message when it reaches a new tier.

:::caution
Mastery lives on the weapon. Trading it passes its mastery on; losing it (every Mysterria weapon [drops when you die](/swords/#losing-a-weapon)) or salvaging it loses the progress.
:::
