The **Twin Fangs** are a silver duelling sabre in your right hand and a black, crimson-veined demon blade in your left, carried and forged as **one weapon**. They are the fastest weapon, and they reward staying on one target.

| | |
|---|---|
| Hands | Two (both blades) |
| Swings per second | 2.0 |
| Combo | Sabre 85%, demon blade 80% (brands), both blades crossing 100% |
| Reach | 3.2 blocks |
| Blueprint | Tier I (8 Notes) |
| Carried | Crossed at the small of the back |

## The demon brand

The demon blade's strike (step 2) brands its target with a crimson sigil for 2 seconds. If your crossing finisher (step 3) lands on the branded target, the brand detonates a moment later for another 35% of the finisher's damage. Switch targets mid-combo and you lose it. The victim sees the sigil over their head, which gives them time to step out of the finisher.

## Weapon art: Severing Line

Sneak + right-click. You cross the blades before your face, then dash up to 8 blocks straight ahead, cutting everyone in a 1.2-block-wide lane for about 16% of an equal foe's health, and lifting and pulling them towards where you land. The lane is drawn on the ground through the 0.7-second wind-up, so a sidestep gets out of it. The dash stops at the first block in the way.

| Cooldown | Cost |
|---|---|
| 20 s | 14 s of natural Spirituality regeneration |

## How do I make it?

| Part | Materials |
|---|---|
| Blade | 14 Iron Ingots, 8 Coal, 8 Redstone Dust + a main ingredient (Sequence 9–8) |
| Guard | 3 Iron Ingots, 2 Gold Ingots + a supplementary ingredient (Sequence 9–7) |
| Grip | 4 Leather, 4 String + a supplementary ingredient (Sequence 9–7) |

One set of parts makes both blades. To dual-wield two separate weapons, see [the overview](/swords/#two-handed-weapons-and-dual-wielding); the Twin Fangs are already two.

## Relic: Sinner's Pair

Severing Line can be cast again once within 3 seconds, for free. See [Relics](/swords/relics/).
