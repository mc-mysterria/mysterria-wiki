The **Jade Naginata** is a polearm: a long black-lacquered shaft bound in teal cord and a curved blade with a jade rune. It reaches further than any other weapon and keeps enemies where it wants them.

| | |
|---|---|
| Hands | Two |
| Swings per second | 1.4 |
| Combo | Rising cut 90%, flat sweep 90%, overhead chop 120% that shoves |
| Reach | 4.2 blocks, 4.6 for the chop |
| Blueprint | Tier II (14 Notes) |
| Carried | Across the back, its blade under a cover |

## How it fights

Every step reaches over 4 blocks, so you can hit enemies who can't yet hit you. The overhead chop shoves its target back. Its hits never spill onto other enemies, and it deals about 8% less damage per second than the others: reach is its edge.

## Weapon art: Ashigarai

Sneak + right-click. You drop into a low crouch with the blade swept back, then sweep it round at ankle height through 180° in front of you, up to 5 blocks out. Everyone hit takes about 14% of an equal foe's health, is slowed for 1.5 seconds and shoved away. An arc on the ground shows the sweep's reach during the 0.6-second wind-up.

| Cooldown | Cost |
|---|---|
| 15 s | 12 s of natural Spirituality regeneration |
