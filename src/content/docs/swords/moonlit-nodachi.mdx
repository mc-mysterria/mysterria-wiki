The **Moonlit Nodachi** is a very long, slightly curved blade with a jade line along it, held high over your head in both hands. Its sweeps catch everyone around your target.

| | |
|---|---|
| Hands | Two |
| Swings per second | 1.2 |
| Combo | Flat sweep 85%, sweep back 85%, lunging thrust 130% |
| Reach | 3.2 blocks for the sweeps, 4.6 for the thrust |
| Blueprint | Tier II (14 Notes) |
| Carried | Across the back |

## How it fights

The two flat sweeps cover 150° and also hit up to 4 other enemies in the arc for half the damage. The thrust reaches further and steps you forward, on the ground only. Its single-target damage is lower than the others'; it pays off in a crowd.

## Weapon art: Moonreap

Sneak + right-click. The blade twirls over your head, then you spin a full circle, hitting everyone within 4.5 blocks for about 12% of an equal foe's health. A moment later the spin's arc flies off ahead as a wave, 9 blocks long, hitting everyone it crosses for 75% of that. A target caught by both takes about 21%. During the 0.7-second wind-up, a ring on the ground shows the spin's reach and a line shows where the wave will fly.

| Cooldown | Cost |
|---|---|
| 16 s | 14 s of natural Spirituality regeneration |
