The **Cathedral Greatsword** is a broad silver blade with violet runes in its fullers, a gilt crossguard and a long two-hand grip. It swings slowly and hits hard.

| | |
|---|---|
| Hands | Two |
| Swings per second | 0.8 |
| Damage per swing | About twice the Longsword's |
| Combo | Sweep 90%, sweep back 90%, overhead 120% that cleaves |
| Reach | 3.2 blocks |
| Blueprint | Tier III (20 Notes) |
| Carried | Across the back |

## How it fights

Each swing is a commitment: a miss costs more than with a fast weapon. The overhead finisher also **cleaves**, dealing half its damage to every other enemy within 3.5 blocks in a 120° arc in front of you.

## Weapon art: Shatter

Sneak + right-click. You bring the blade down and the ground breaks in a wave running out through an 80° wedge in front of you, 6 blocks long. Each enemy is hit as the wave reaches them, for about 16% of an equal foe's health, then knocked up and pushed slightly away. The wedge is drawn on the ground through the 0.8-second wind-up, and the wave takes about half a second to reach the far edge, so it can still be escaped.

| Cooldown | Cost |
|---|---|
| 18 s | 14 s of natural Spirituality regeneration |

## Relic: Cathedral's Toll

Shatter tolls an aftershock a second after its wave ends, hitting everyone it hit again for half the damage. See [Relics](/swords/relics/).
