The **Starless Katana** is a blade cut from the night sky: black steel full of stars, a nebula glowing along its spine and an eclipse for a guard. It is the hardest weapon to make and the only one that can **execute**.

| | |
|---|---|
| Hands | One |
| Swings per second | 1.6 |
| Combo | Slash 90%, backhand 90%, a full spin 120% that also hits up to 4 others for half |
| Reach | 3.2 blocks |
| Blueprint | 5 Void Notes, at a Demigod table only |
| Carried | Left hip |

Its damage per second is the Longsword's; its power is in what it does.

## Void corruption

Every hit you land, the spin's sweep included, puts a stack of **void corruption** on the target, up to 5. The stacks fade 8 seconds after the last one.

- **You** see a row of five pips over each target you've corrupted, filling as stacks land, and a glowing cracked row once that target can be executed. The action bar also shows the corrupted foe in front of you.
- **Everyone** sees stars circling a corrupted target, and a cracked violet halo over one that can be executed.
- **A player** you can execute is told so on their action bar.

## Two weapon arts

Press **F** to switch between the two arts; **Sneak + right-click** (or Sneak + F) uses the selected one. Each has its own cooldown.

### Severance

The left hand rises and tears the corruption out. A target in front of you (within 6 blocks, a 60° cone) **dies** if:

- it carries **5 corruption**,
- it is at or below **25% health**,
- it is not a boss,
- it is at most one Sequence stronger than you.

No revive ability and no Totem of Undying can stop it. Duels and other fights where death isn't real still end the way they always do.

Severance only starts on a target it can take, so it is never wasted. The target is warned during the 0.7-second wind-up: if they get out of reach, break line of sight or heal above the line before it ends, they take about 16% of an equal foe's health instead. Either way, their corruption is spent.

| Cooldown | Cost |
|---|---|
| 24 s | 16 s of natural Spirituality regeneration |

### Event Horizon

You thrust the blade and a beacon flies up to 24 blocks ahead, anchoring on the first body or block it hits. For 2 seconds it drags every enemy within 6 blocks towards it. Each enemy caught is hit once for about 10% of an equal foe's health, **takes 15% more damage from every source for 6 seconds**, and gains a stack of corruption. It sets up Severance, and your allies' hits too.

| Cooldown | Cost |
|---|---|
| 20 s | 14 s of natural Spirituality regeneration |

## How do I make it?

1. **Void Notes** are found **only in End City chests**. A chest holds them 35% of the time for whoever opens it, and you never go more than 3 such chests in a row without them. About 12 chests (3 to 4 End Cities) yield the 5 you need.
2. Draft the Blueprint from 5 Void Notes at a **[Demigod table](/swords/crafting/#demigod-tables)**. The Starless Katana is listed only there, and every step of making it happens there.
3. Forge the parts and assemble it as usual:

| Part | Materials |
|---|---|
| Blade | 3 Netherite Ingots, 6 Echo Shards, 16 Chorus Fruit + a main ingredient (Sequence 9–8) |
| Guard | 1 Netherite Ingot, 4 Eyes of Ender + a supplementary ingredient (Sequence 9–7) |
| Grip | 2 Shulker Shells, 4 String + a supplementary ingredient (Sequence 9–7) |

It is one-handed, so it can be dual-wielded with another one-handed Mysterria weapon.

## Relic: Nameless Star

The blade turns towards a dying star: a black sun with a gold corona, and every effect burns starfire gold instead of void violet. **Singularity:** a foe you sever collapses into a black hole for 2 seconds that drags enemies within 5 blocks in and corrupts them each second, and a successful execution resets Severance to 4 seconds, so one kill can set up the next. See [Relics](/swords/relics/).
