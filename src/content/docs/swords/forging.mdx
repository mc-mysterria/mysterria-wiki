Anyone can forge a full-strength weapon. Two things make a blade special: **who forged it** and **which ingredient** went into its Blade.

## Paragon forging

Paragon is the smith's Pathway. Paragons are the forgers other Pathways go to, and forging is part of how they act out their Sequence.

| From | A Paragon's hand gives |
|---|---|
| Sequence 9 | **Craftsman's eye:** 1.5× wider timing windows, a 25% wider heat band, a 15% slower hammer and the perfect zone drawn |
| Sequence 9 | **Masterwork:** only a Paragon can forge one. Anyone else's Masterwork-level forge comes out Fine, and the table says so |
| Sequence 9 | **Paragon-forged blades:** +5% damage and the weapon art 3 seconds sooner, for whoever wields the blade |
| Sequence 7 | **Appraisal:** Sneak + right-click the table holding a weapon to write its forging scores into its lore |
| Sequence 6 | **Runes** (below) |

The Paragon-forged bonus goes to whoever holds the blade, so commissioning a Paragon is worth it, and your own blade is never worthless.

**Forging is acting.** Every part a Paragon forges by hand advances their acting: 0.5% for a Standard result, 1.5% for Fine, 3% for a Masterwork, up to 20% a week. Working steadily gives none.

**Smith's ledger.** The table counts the blades a Paragon has forged by hand and their Masterworks. `/swords ledger` shows yours. Titles go into your maker's mark:

| Title | Blades forged | Masterworks |
|---|---|---|
| Journeyman Bladesmith | 10 | 0 |
| Master Bladesmith | 25 | 3 |
| Grandmaster Bladesmith | 60 | 15 |

### Runes

A Paragon of Sequence 6 or stronger chooses a rune before forging a Blade by hand. Forging then has a fourth stage: five runes appear over the blade, four of them light up in order, and you click them back in the same order. The more you recall, the stronger the rune, from half to full strength.

| Rune | At full strength |
|---|---|
| Thrift | The weapon art costs 20% less Spirituality |
| Swiftness | The weapon art is ready 1 second sooner |
| Endurance | The blade wears 50% slower |

## Resonance

The **main ingredient** in a Blade gives the weapon its Pathway's **resonance**, if the forger walks that Pathway. A Paragon can forge any Pathway's resonance. Otherwise the blade is unresonant. The outer Pathways forge unresonant.

Resonance adds a **rider** to the weapon art. Riders change how the art plays and never add damage. Effects on other players respect the server's hostility rules, and effects that slow or blind lose strength when repeated on the same target (full, half, a quarter).

| Pathway | Rider on the weapon art |
|---|---|
| Fool | Slowness I for 2 s on each target hit |
| Error | Refunds 15% of the art's Spirituality on its first hit |
| Door | You step 2 blocks back after the art |
| Visionary | Weakness I for 3 s on each target hit |
| Sun | Sets targets on fire for 2 s |
| Tyrant | Knocks targets back further |
| White Tower | Targets glow for 5 s |
| Hanged Man | Wither I for 2 s on each target hit |
| Darkness | Blindness for 1 s on each target hit |
| Death | Heals you for 10% of the damage dealt |
| Twilight Giant | Resistance I for 2 s on you |
| Demoness | Poison I for 3 s on each target hit |
| Priest | Speed I for 3 s on you |
| Mother | Regeneration I for 3 s on you |
| Moon | Slowness I for 2 s on targets, Night Vision for 10 s on you |
| Abyss | Hunger I and Weakness I for 3 s on each target hit |
| Chained | Slowness II for 1.5 s on each target hit |
| Black Emperor | Mining Fatigue I for 3 s on each target hit |
| Justiciar | Targets can't sprint for 2 s |
| Hermit | Refunds 10% of the art's Spirituality on its first hit |
| Paragon | The weapon art is ready 1 second sooner |
| Wheel of Fortune | A chance for the art's cooldown to halve, which rises after every miss |

The weapon's lore shows its resonance and what its rider does.
