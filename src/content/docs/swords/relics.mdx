Every Mysterria weapon has a **relic**: a ritual-forged version with its own look and a signature twist to its weapon art. Anyone who completes a weapon's ritual gets its relic, but the ritual is hidden, and finding it takes time.

## The relics

| Weapon | Relic | Signature twist |
|---|---|---|
| Knight's Longsword | **Dawnoath** | *Judgement of Dawn:* the Weapon Art brands its target with a sun sigil for 5 s, and each combo hit you land on it calls down a sunbeam for an extra 30% of that hit |
| Twin Fangs | **Sinner's Pair** | Severing Line can be cast again once within 3 s, for free |
| Moonlit Nodachi | **Moonmirror** | Moonreap's wave turns at its end and flies back, cutting again |
| Jade Naginata | **Riverbane** | *Undertow:* Ashigarai leaves a whirlpool for 3 s that drags enemies to its centre and slows them, then throws them up |
| Cathedral Greatsword | **Cathedral's Toll** | Shatter tolls an aftershock a second later for half its damage |
| Starless Katana | **Nameless Star** | *Singularity:* a foe you sever collapses into a black hole for 2 s that drags enemies in and corrupts them, and a kill resets Severance to 4 s |

A relic keeps everything the weapon had: its grade, rune, resonance, maker and [mastery](/swords/mastery/). Its base damage is the same; the twist is what sets it apart.

## Torn Codex Pages

Each ritual is written in a smith's codex, torn apart long ago. **Torn Codex Pages** are found:

- in ancient city, End City, bastion and Nether fortress chests, for whoever opens them. Not every chest has one, but you never go more than 5 such chests in a row without a page;
- sometimes on Beyonder creatures of Sequence 4 or stronger.

Pages aren't tied to a weapon, so you can trade them.

## Deciphering a page

At a [Demigod table](/swords/crafting/#demigod-tables), hold the page in your main hand and the weapon whose ritual you want in your off-hand, then right-click the table. The page reveals the **next line of that weapon's ritual riddle** to you. Each ritual has four lines.

What you've deciphered is yours: it's saved on you, and your **Ritual Codex**, a book you're given with the first page, keeps every line you know, in your language.

## Attempting a ritual

Once you know all four lines of a weapon's ritual, meet what they describe, then **sneak and right-click a Demigod table** while holding the weapon.

- If something is missing, the table tells you how many of the four conditions are met, and nothing is taken. Try again as often as you like.
- If everything is met, the weapon becomes its relic, any offering the ritual asks for is consumed, and the whole server hears about it.

You need to know the whole ritual yourself to attempt it, even if someone else tells you the answer.

:::caution
Relics are Mysterria weapons, and every Mysterria weapon [drops when you die](/swords/#losing-a-weapon).
:::
