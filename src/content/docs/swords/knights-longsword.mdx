The **Knight's Longsword** is the baseline every other weapon is measured against: a balanced one-handed blade with a straight double edge, a glowing fuller and a jewelled pommel. It is one of the two weapons you can dual-wield.

| | |
|---|---|
| Hands | One |
| Swings per second | 1.6 |
| Combo | Diagonal cut 90%, backhand 90%, overhead 120% |
| Reach | 3.2 blocks |
| Blueprint | Tier I (8 Notes) |
| Carried | Left hip |

## How it fights

Two diagonal cuts sweep across in front of you, then an overhead chop comes down hard. Nothing fancy: steady damage, a clear finisher, and a free off-hand for a second Longsword or a Starless Katana.

## Weapon Art

Sneak + right-click. A focused strike on the nearest target in a 70° cone up to 4 blocks ahead, for about 20% of an equal foe's health. A gold rune circle opens under you during the 0.6-second wind-up.

| Cooldown | Cost |
|---|---|
| 12 s | 10 s of natural Spirituality regeneration |

## Relic: Dawnoath

**Judgement of Dawn:** the Weapon Art brands its target with a sun sigil for 5 seconds, and each combo hit you land on it calls down a sunbeam for an extra 30% of that hit. See [Relics](/swords/relics/).
