Every Mysterria weapon is forged at a **Bladesmith's Table** in four steps: Notes, a Blueprint, three parts, then assembly. Grades come after, at the same table.

## The Bladesmith's Table

Craft it in a crafting table (it is in your recipe book):

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| | Bricks | |
| Blast Furnace | Leather | Anvil |
| Smooth Stone | Smooth Stone | Smooth Stone |

It is two blocks wide: a hearth on the left and an anvil on the right. Right-click it to open its menu. It can't be mined, pushed or blown up; in survival, hit it five times in a row to take it apart and get it back.

## Bladesmith's Notes

Notes come in three tiers and are found as loot:

| Tier | Where |
|---|---|
| I | Village, ruined portal, igloo, ocean ruin, shipwreck, pillager outpost, desert and jungle temple, and mineshaft chests |
| II | Stronghold, woodland mansion, buried treasure and dungeon chests; trial vaults; Beyonder creatures |
| III | Ancient city, End City, bastion and Nether fortress chests; ominous trial vaults; Beyonder creatures of Sequence 4 or stronger |

Each matching chest holds one Note for whoever opens it. A Beyonder creature you kill (not from a spawner) drops one 15% of the time, and never more than 9 kills in a row without one.

## Blueprints

Open the table, pick a weapon and click its **Blueprint** card to draft one:

| Blueprint tier | Notes needed |
|---|---|
| I | 8 |
| II | 14 |
| III | 20 |

Higher-tier Notes can pay for a lower-tier Blueprint. A Blueprint is used up when the weapon is assembled, so every weapon needs a new one. The Starless Katana uses **Void Notes** instead (see [its page](/swords/starless-katana/#how-do-i-make-it)).

## Parts

Carrying the Blueprint, forge the **Blade**, **Guard** and **Grip**. Each needs its materials and **one Beyonder ingredient**:

- **Blade:** a main ingredient of Sequence 9 or 8. Its Pathway becomes the blade's [resonance](/swords/forging/#resonance).
- **Guard and Grip:** a supplementary ingredient of Sequence 9 to 7.

| Weapon | Blade | Guard | Grip |
|---|---|---|---|
| Knight's Longsword | 12 Iron Ingots, 8 Coal | 3 Iron Ingots, 2 Gold Ingots | 3 Leather, 4 String |
| Twin Fangs | 14 Iron Ingots, 8 Coal, 8 Redstone Dust | 3 Iron Ingots, 2 Gold Ingots | 4 Leather, 4 String |
| Moonlit Nodachi | 16 Iron Ingots, 2 Diamonds, 8 Nether Quartz | 4 Gold Ingots, 2 Iron Ingots | 3 Leather, 8 String |
| Jade Naginata | 10 Iron Ingots, 2 Diamonds, 8 Lapis Lazuli | 4 Gold Ingots, 2 Iron Ingots | 4 Dark Oak Logs, 6 String, 2 Leather |
| Cathedral Greatsword | 20 Iron Ingots, 4 Diamonds, 4 Blaze Rods | 6 Gold Ingots, 8 Amethyst Shards | 6 Leather, 6 String |
| Starless Katana | 3 Netherite Ingots, 6 Echo Shards, 16 Chorus Fruit | 1 Netherite Ingot, 4 Eyes of Ender | 2 Shulker Shells, 4 String |

The weapon's page shows exactly what you have and what you're missing before anything is taken. Parts are items, so you can trade them, or have a Paragon forge your blade from your ingredient.

## Forging at the anvil

**Left-click** a part's card to forge it by hand: short games played on the table that only you can see. **Right-click** to *work steadily* instead, with no games, for a Standard weapon. Nothing you put in is ever lost: the materials are taken and your progress saved before the first stage, so stepping away, taking damage or logging out just pauses the job, and any Bladesmith's Table picks it up where you left off.

| Stage | What you do |
|---|---|
| Heat (Blade) | Click to work the bellows and keep the heat between the two marks for 7 seconds. The glow colour and a word ("Too cold", "Forging heat", "Too hot") tell you where you are. |
| Hammer (Blade) | Click when the sweeping hammer is over the glowing spot, six times, each pass faster |
| Quench (Blade) | Click when the glow peaks; a chime rises towards it |
| Guard and Grip | Three strikes each, on the same kind of sweeping target |
| Assembly | Click as the guard, then the grip, sway past their seats; then a metronome counts in and you strike the pommel on three beats |

How well it goes sets the weapon's **temper**: Standard, Fine (60% and up) or Masterwork (85% and up). Temper is for show: the name in the lore, your name as the maker, and a shine on a Masterwork. Every temper hits just as hard. Only a [Paragon](/swords/forging/#paragon-forging) can forge a Masterwork.

## Assembly

With the Blueprint and all three parts in your inventory, click **Assemble**: left-click for the assembly game, right-click to assemble steadily. The Blueprint and parts are used up and the weapon is yours.

## Grades

The menu's grade button (bottom left) raises the grade of the weapon in your main hand by one step: an ungraded weapon becomes grade 9, then 8, and so on down to 0. Each step takes **2 Beyonder ingredients** (main or supplementary) of the new grade's Sequence; grade 0 also takes a Sequence 0 characteristic. The screen shows the cost and the result before you confirm. See [attunement](/swords/#attunement-and-grades) for what grades do.

### Demigod tables

Grades 3 to 0 need a **Demigod table**. Right-click a Bladesmith's Table while holding a Sequence 4 or stronger potion: the table drinks it and becomes a Demigod table for good. Its coals burn violet, and anyone can use it. The [Starless Katana](/swords/starless-katana/) and [relic rituals](/swords/relics/) also need one.

## Salvage

The menu's salvage button (bottom right) breaks down the weapon or part in your main hand and gives back **half of its materials**, rounded down. Beyonder ingredients, Notes, the Blueprint, grades, runes and resonance are not recovered. The screen lists what you get back and what you lose before you confirm. A weapon that was never forged at a table gives nothing.
